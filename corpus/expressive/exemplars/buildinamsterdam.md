## What works

- **The viewport is split into a "stage" half and a "speech" half.** Desktop divides ~50/50: a solid black void on the left, the warm-paper content panel on the right. The void carries no text — it's a held-empty media/loading bay where work (and here, a single orange disc) appears. Splitting the canvas by *value* (black vs paper) rather than by a rule line gives an instant two-zone hierarchy: one zone performs, one zone speaks. Transferable to any agency/portfolio hero that wants drama without filling the screen with ornament — commit half the canvas to emptiness and let it read as confidence.
- **Hierarchy is scale + family, never weight or colour.** A ~5.4rem (86px) grotesk display headline sits above a small *serif* subhead and 16px UI, all in one or two near-identical weights (400/500). There is no grey and no bold: rank is set by a ~5× size jump, and *tone* is handed to a second typeface. Transferable: when you have a real display scale, you can drop weight and colour as hierarchy tools entirely, which keeps a dense hero calm.
- **A single serif line does all the "human" work.** Everything is a neutral Swiss grotesk except the one subhead, set in a book-weight serif ("We shape the future of commerce…"). Rationing the expressive face to *one short paragraph* makes the page feel authored without the headline turning decorative. Transferable to any sans-led site that reads cold — add one small serif voice line, not a second display face.
- **One warm accent, deployed as a single object.** The entire palette is paper + black + one ochre disc. That disc is the only saturated, only curved, only "alive" element, so it owns all the attention the restrained type deliberately gives up. Transferable: spend your one accent on a single hero object rather than spreading it across buttons, links, and underlines — scarcity is what makes it read as art-directed.
- **Paper, not white.** The light half is `#F2EFE6`, a warm off-white; pure white appears only on the small button/tile fills. The slight warmth reads as premium/editorial where flat `#fff` reads as default. Transferable to any minimalist surface: shift the page background a few points off pure white and reserve `#fff` for raised elements, so "white" cards actually look raised.
- **Chrome legibility across the value seam is solved with a blend mode, not two colour states.** On a canvas split into a black stage and a paper half, anything that can cross the seam — the wordmark bar, the custom cursor — would normally need per-zone colour logic. Instead both carry `mix-blend-mode: exclusion` (the site runs two such layers, incl. a full-viewport cursor plane at `--z-cursor:7`), so *one* ink auto-inverts to read correctly over black **and** over paper. Transferable to any value-split or dark/light-zoned layout: give crossing chrome (cursor, sticky header, scroll-progress bar) a single colour plus an exclusion/difference blend rather than measuring which zone it currently overlaps.
- **The split survives the breakpoint by re-stacking, not collapsing.** Mobile doesn't drop the black panel — it moves the speech above and turns the stage into a slab below, with the disc straddling the seam and project tiles bleeding off the right edge. The two-zone idea is preserved vertically. Transferable: when a side-by-side concept reflows, restack the *same two zones* top/bottom rather than discarding one, so the art direction reaches small screens.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Two families. Neue Haas Grotesk (sans — `NeueHaasGrotesk-Roman` + the `TXPro`/`DSPro` optical cuts) carries the display headline *and* all UI; Reckless Neue Book (serif) is reserved for the single subhead. Scale is wide and sparse — ~16px body to ~5.4rem (86.4px) display (~5× jump), with display at very tight ~0.85 leading. **Tracking scales with size** (`tracking_by_size`): the 86.4px display tightens to **-3.456px (-0.04em)** while the 24px subhead tier carries a gentle **-0.24px (-0.01em)** — bigger = tighter, the engineered-density move. The display also turns on stylistic sets **ss04/ss06/ss07/ss10/ss14** (alternate grotesk forms — part of why the headline reads bespoke, not default Helvetica). Hierarchy = **scale + family swap**; weight stays at 400/500 throughout.
- **Color:** Three values, no greys. `bg` warm paper `#F2EFE6`, `void` near-black `#0A0A0A` (the left stage; sets white ink on itself), `fg` black on paper. One accent: ochre `#C38133` used solely on the disc. A blue `#3C4CC7` exists in the stylesheet but is below the fold. Secondary text is *not* a muted grey — it's the same black ink, demoted by serif family + size.
- **Spacing rhythm:** ~8px-derived, scaled generous. The content half holds large vertical gaps (eyebrow → display → serif → buttons each separated by ~2–4rem) and the black half is mostly empty space — whitespace (and blackspace) is the primary separator.
- **Layout system:** Desktop is a hard vertical split (~50/50, content right-aligned within its half). Top bar spans the paper half only (wordmark left, partner badge right). Mobile restacks to: speech block on paper → black slab with disc + horizontally-bleeding tile row → social squares. Observed breakpoint behavior preserves both zones.
- **Layering / page architecture:** Depth is **flat, not parallaxed** — `has_static_backdrop:false`, so opaque paper/void content scrolls under a thin fixed-chrome set (nav + footer-CTA at `--z-menu:8`, wordmark bar at `--z-top-bar:3`); nothing sits at negative-z and no section is transparent to a world beneath, so this is **not** a backdrop-reveal page. What *is* load-bearing is blend: **two `mix-blend-mode: exclusion` layers** (`blend_glass` census). The flagged one (`has_blend_overlay:true`) is a **full-viewport `position:fixed`, pointer-events:none plane at z-index 7 — exactly the site's own `--z-cursor:7` token — riding above all content**: the custom-cursor layer (the likely home of the **14 mousemove listeners** flagged in *Interaction & motion*). Its background is transparent (`alpha:0`, no image), so exclusion is identity over empty space and touches only its own child — which is *why* every frame's surfaces stay dead-flat with **no grain/noise/texture**; the blend exists to make a single cursor ink invert and stay legible over *both* the black stage and the paper half. The second exclusion element is the wordmark lockup (`--z-top-bar:3`) — the same auto-invert insurance for chrome that can cross the black/paper value seam. Depth here is engineered by **blend + value zoning**, not by z-stacked translucency.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Two real curves drive everything (`motion.params`): the signature `cubic-bezier(0.45, 0.02, 0.09, 0.98)` — a fast-out, long-settle ease that reads as crisp and engineered (a Shopify-build studio signalling precision) — plus a steeper `cubic-bezier(0.87, 0, 0.13, 1)` (near-`easeInOutQuint`) for the scroll-pinned reveals, with bare `ease` / `ease-in-out` as fallbacks. Durations cluster at **100 / 150 / 200 / 250 / 400 / 500 / 650ms** and entrance items stagger **~700ms** apart. Distinct from the soft ~1s ease-outs other expressive studios favour. Full choreography, evidence, and per-technique safety in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** Low above the fold (one headline, one line, two buttons, one object), rising on mobile where the tile strip introduces a denser gallery band. Stays legible because the dense band is isolated on the black slab.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **scale** (display vs body), reinforced by **value zoning** (the black half vs the paper half partitions the page before a single word is read).
- **Restraint:** No gradients, no drop-shadows (except the disc's soft cast across the seam), no grey text, no chromatic UI accents, no second display face, no rounded cards — buttons and tiles are near-square (4px). Drama is one disc and half a black screen.
- **Grid / container:** Measured column counts are **1 / 2 / 6** on desktop, collapsing to **1 / 6** on mobile (`.craft.grid`) — i.e. the page mixes a full-bleed single column (the hero halves, a hard ~50/50 viewport split with content right-aligned in the paper half — confirmed in s00/s06), a 2-up split (the OUR WORK / CONTACT US button pair), and a **6-column** track that the project-tile strip rides on (the "6" survives into mobile as the horizontally-bleeding tile row). Representative gutter **~16px** (up to ~35px on the looser bands). No max-width pixel value was captured — the split runs edge-to-edge, so the container is full-bleed (viewport 1440px) rather than a centered column.
- **Components (from `.craft.states`):** Mostly **not captured** — the profiler logged only a **focus** state and an empty **disabled** set; there are **no resting/hover/active deltas for button, card, nav, or input** in the data (this is a near-static hero capture, so interactive states beyond focus weren't exercised).
  - *Button* — two outline pills (`OUR WORK`, `CONTACT US`): `#fff` fill, 1px black hairline border, ~4px radius, mono-ish UI caps. **Hover/active not captured** (the static read's "hover rides the house curve" is inference, not measured).
  - *Card / tile* — project tiles are near-square (4px radius), paper-fill, edge-bleeding off the right on mobile. **No hover/elevation state captured**; site is shadowless (`elevation.shadows:[]`).
  - *Nav* — 10 visible links; desktop and mobile both expose 10 (`likely_hamburger:false`), so the menu does **not** collapse to a hamburger — it's the disc-anchored "Menu" affordance instead. **No nav hover state captured.**
  - *Input* — **none present** on the hero; no form fields captured.
  - *Focus (the one real state):* links take `outline: 1px rgb(0,0,0)` — a genuine visible focus ring (`focus_visible:true`), good for keyboard a11y on this light surface (verify the same ring is legible against the black void half).

## Interaction & motion

**The frame sequence did NOT capture this site's motion.** All 11 scroll stills (`frames/s00–s10`) are byte-near-identical (60.7K→59.0K) because the page is **scroll-hijacked / smooth-scrolled** — the hero is held pinned in the viewport while the document scrolls beneath it, so even-depth stills show the same composition. The *only* visible delta across the whole sequence is the orange disc's curved **"Menu"** label, present in s00–s06 and rotated off-frame by s09 — direct corroboration of the one rotate effect. So motion below is grounded in the probe counts, the `@keyframes` presence, the `scroll_effects` table, and the DOM markup, not in watched frames. **Notably for an expressive/agency site, reduced motion IS respected** (`respectsReducedMotion:true`, **11** `prefers-reduced-motion` rules) — the opposite of teak. Stack is **hand-rolled** — `0` libraries detected, `0` CSS-animation rules, motion driven by `17` transition rules + **6** `IntersectionObserver`s (37 targets, threshold `0.25`, `rootMargin 200px`) + **295** `requestAnimationFrame` calls in one scroll pass + **3** `@keyframes` (minified names `dSRjOm`/`gzjafi`/`gneoFG` — no semantic clue, unlike a site that names them). Only **6** scroll effects total, **5 of them pins** — this is a *pin-heavy, drift-light* signature, far calmer than teak's 48-effect storm.

- **Scroll-hijacked pinned hero (smooth-scroll stage)** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the split-stage container, header, and nav all hold `position: sticky/fixed` (5 of 6 captured `scroll_effects` are `pin`) while a smooth-scroll engine drives the document; the first viewport stays composed as a stable stage as you scroll. *Reads as:* a held, cinematic frame — the page feels "mounted" rather than scrolling, signalling control. *Transfers to:* agency/portfolio heroes that want the opening composition to persist while sections cross-fade or assemble beneath it. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — scroll-hijack overrides native scroll velocity (a motion-sickness + scroll-jank vector; `295` rAF/pass is the cost), and it breaks scrollbar-position expectations; ship a non-hijacked fallback and honour reduced-motion (this site does). The *pin* primitive itself is cheap and safe. → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Scroll-progress rotation of the disc's orbiting label** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the curved "Menu" wordmark wrapping the ochre disc (`g "Menu"`, `property: transform`) rotates **108.2°** as a function of scroll — the single non-pin scroll effect, and the only delta the frames actually caught (label present at s06, gone by s09). *Reads as:* the one accent object is alive and reader-driven; turning the *menu affordance* into the moving part ties navigation to scroll. *Transfers to:* a single decorative/wayfinding accent (a disc, a seal, a badge) you want bound to reader motion. *Safety:* **production-safe** (compositor-only `transform` on one tiny element; cap the angle — 108° is within taste, well short of teak's near-180° swings). → `scroll-progress-rotate`.
- **Staggered entrance on load** — *Trigger:* load / in-view (IO, threshold `0.25`, `rootMargin 200px` pre-roll). *Mechanism:* hero elements reveal in sequence (`staggeredEntrance:true`, stagger `~700ms`; entrance entries fire at `150ms` for the SVG disc and `1200ms` for the copy block). *Reads as:* the composition assembles itself — eyebrow, then display, then serif line, then buttons — a deliberate "curtain-up." *Transfers to:* any hero that should build rather than appear. *Safety:* **production-safe** (one-shot opacity/transform, reduced-motion-gated). Caveat: the captured entrance deltas were `from==to` (`1.00→1.00` / `0.00→0.00`) — representation noise dropped per method — so the *technique* is confirmed by the stagger/IO probes, not by a measured transform range. → `scroll-reveal-in-view` (group variant).
- **Pointer-reactive ambient (probable, unconfirmed)** — *Trigger:* mousemove. *Mechanism:* **14** `mousemove` listeners are bound, yet the profiler classified **0** cursor-followers and **0** hover effects — so pointer input is wired but its visible target isn't captured (likely a subtle disc/parallax nudge or a custom cursor on inner pages, possibly the showreel hotspot). *Reads as:* unverifiable from this capture. *Transfers to:* — (don't reuse what we can't see). *Safety:* **flagged as thin data** — 14 listeners is real main-thread pointer work to budget for, but with no observed effect we can't tag the technique; treat as "instrument, don't replicate." No matching pattern.

## Production-safety note

- **Split-stage hero (value-zoned canvas)** — *production-safe.* Pure CSS grid/flex with two background colours; crawlable, no JS, reflows by restacking. Cost: near-zero. Watch-items: (1) ensure text lives only on the legible (paper) half so contrast is never at risk over the black void; (2) the empty black half is "wasted" first-viewport real estate — fine for a brand/agency hero, risky for a conversion landing where above-fold content matters, so treat the emptiness as a deliberate brand cost.
- **Endorsed wordmark lockup ("BiA. | Powered by FRONT ROW")** — *production-safe.* Static text + a hairline divider; trivial cost. Watch-item: keep the endorser type small and the divider a real `border`, and mark it up as text (not an image) so it stays accessible/crawlable.
- **Serif voice line inside a sans system** — *production-safe.* One extra webfont. Cost: a second font request (subset it / `font-display: swap`). A11y-neutral.
- **Warm-paper background instead of #fff** — *production-safe.* A single token change. Cost: none; just re-check that text contrast still clears AA on `#F2EFE6` (black ink is fine).
- **Giant display at ~0.85 leading + ~-0.04em tracking** — *production-safe with care.* Cost: very tight leading can collide ascenders/descenders between stacked lines and tight tracking hurts small-screen wrapping; cap the negative tracking and test the multi-line stack on narrow mobile (this hero wraps to 5+ lines on phones).
- **Single ochre disc as ambient/floating object** — *spectacle-only (mild).* A decorative moving element; adds a little JS/animation and a moving target that can distract or trigger motion sensitivity. Cost: minor perf + a11y — gate any float/parallax behind `prefers-reduced-motion`; it must stay purely decorative (no information encoded in it).
- **Edge-bleeding tile strip (mobile)** — *production-safe.* Horizontal overflow scroll/peek; cost low. Ensure it's keyboard/touch scrollable and that clipped tiles aren't the only way to reach those projects.

- **Accessibility read (honest, incl. defects).** **Wins:** `prefers-reduced-motion` IS respected (**11** PRM rules — rare for an award/agency site, and the headline differentiator vs teak, which respects none); a real **focus-visible** ring (1px black outline) on links; and a **perfect alt-text ratio (1.0 across 30 images)**. **Defects:** **5 small tap targets** flagged (of 21 interactive elements — the near-square 4px outline pills + social squares likely fall under the 44px min, a touch-ergonomics miss); the **scroll-hijack** is itself an a11y liability (overrides user scroll velocity, hostile to motion-sensitive and switch/keyboard users even with PRM gated — the resting non-hijacked DOM must remain navigable); and `theme.supportsDark:true` is a **false positive** (reported dark values are transparent/`rgb(0,0,0)` artifacts of the black void panel, not a real dark-mode system — don't treat it as one).

Capture is a clean render of the live split-screen homepage hero (desktop) and its restacked mobile equivalent — **not** a cookie wall, preloader, or WebGL gate. The black half is intentional design, not a failed/blank load.

## Extracted patterns

- **split-stage-hero** — a hero whose viewport is split by value into a held-empty "stage" half and a typographic "speech" half; restacks (not collapses) on mobile. Reuse for agency/portfolio/brand heroes that want drama from emptiness rather than ornament.
- **endorsed-wordmark-lockup** — a primary wordmark paired with a parent/endorser brand via a hairline divider ("X | Powered by Y"), plus a separate trust badge. Reuse in nav/footer for sub-brands, white-label products, or partner-certified studios.
- **sticky-pin-scrollytelling** — the pin primitive behind the held hero (5 of 6 scroll effects pin); reuse to keep an opening composition mounted while sections cross beneath it. (motion)
- **scroll-progress-rotate** — the disc's "Menu" label rotating 108.2° with scroll; reuse to bind one decorative/wayfinding accent to reader motion (cap the angle). (motion)
- **scroll-reveal-in-view** — the staggered hero entrance (group variant, ~700ms stagger, IO threshold 0.25); reuse to make a hero assemble rather than appear. (motion)

`patterns_to_build:`
- **scroll-hijack-pinned-stage** — a *smooth-scroll engine* that overrides native scroll to hold the first viewport composed as a stage while the document scrolls beneath. Not yet a corpus pattern, and deliberately so: it's **spectacle-only** (scroll-velocity override = motion-sickness + jank + scrollbar-expectation break). If built, the recipe must wrap the hijack behind `prefers-reduced-motion` and keep the un-hijacked DOM fully navigable — distinct from plain `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`, which pins *within* native scroll and is production-safe. (motion, candidate)
