## What works
- **Media carries the page; type recedes to instrument labels.** ~90% of the surface is full-bleed cinematic photography / 3D render (silhouetted hero, the product mosaic, the aerial megafactory). Typography is almost entirely demoted to a single register: ~12px uppercase HelveticaNow tracked out to +0.48px (≈+0.04em). Hierarchy is created by *image scale and ground color*, not type scale — the largest "headline" most of the page ever shows is a product name set as a small caption. The transferable rule: when the assets are genuinely strong (hardware, renders, real photography), let media own the hierarchy and let type fall back to captioning; the page reads cinematic instead of slideware. The failure mode it avoids is the generic "big headline + three feature cards."
- **Tracked-out micro-caps as the *only* UI type register.** Every label — product categories, `ARSENAL-1`, `DESIGNED BY ANDURIL / BUILT IN OHIO`, `PT — 01/02` — is the same tiny tracked uppercase. Positive tracking on small caps reads as instrumentation / spec-sheet / HUD annotation; that one typographic decision is most of the military-industrial signal, achieved with zero illustration. Pair it with the inverse on the few large heads (negative tracking that tightens with size: -0.176px at 18px → -0.8px at 40px). Transferable optical law: track *out* below ~14px caps, track *in* above ~32px display.
- **Sectional ground-color inversion as narrative pacing.** The page cycles grounds like chapters: black (hero + product mosaic) → mid-grey (Arsenal-1 factory) → warm off-white #f1f0ea (News, a light editorial interlude) → an olive-green + grey *split* band (the two Arsenal essays) → black (footer). Each act gets its own full-bleed background, so scrolling feels like moving through rooms, not down one scroll. Transferable: chapter a long landing page with edge-to-edge background-color shifts instead of hairline dividers — cheaper and more dramatic than section borders.
- **Rationed dual accent on a greyscale base.** The palette is white-on-near-black greyscale; the only chroma is an acid chartreuse (#dff140) and a saturated electric blue (#0000ee). The chartreuse appears almost nowhere as a fill — it is a thin edge/stripe and a literal glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 10px #dff140`), reading as "powered / armed / active." Scarcity is the mechanism: a hue touching <2% of pixels brands harder than a saturated section. Transferable to any restrained dark theme — reserve one accent for *energy* states only.
- **Asymmetric cinematic bento for a product family.** Ghost / Barracuda / Lattice / Fury / Roadrunner / Menace / Dive-XL is a deliberately uneven mosaic — one tall tile (Roadrunner) anchoring a field of varied sizes, each a full-bleed still with a bottom-left name + one-line tagline and a corner ↗. It reads as a catalogue of capabilities, magazine-like, and lets each product's own footage supply the color while the chrome stays monochrome. Transfers to any product line, portfolio, or capability index. → `cinematic-media-mosaic`.
- **"Machined" chrome: 2px radius + hairlines, no soft UI.** Corners are 2px everywhere — not sharp brutalism, not soft cards; it reads as milled metal. No drop shadows (the single shadow in the whole capture is the chartreuse glow), no decorative gradients beyond the legibility scrim under labels. Restraint *is* the aesthetic — the austerity is what makes the cinematic media feel expensive.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** HelveticaNowDisplay is the near-sole visible family (a neutral, slightly humanist grotesque — "default but premium"); `Elios` is a display/brand face surfaced sparingly. Scale is bimodal: a dense floor of 12–18px UI/body and a thin ceiling of 32/40/64px display — hierarchy = scale jump + position, not a weight ramp (weights stay 400, with 500/700 rare). The signature is the *tracking*: +0.48px on 12px caps, sliding negative as size grows (-0.144 to -0.8px). (Raw also reports a large `Times New Roman` count — treated as fallback / unstyled CMS DOM, not a visible role; the capture also caught unrendered template tokens like `@[title]`.)
- **Color:** Inverted greyscale narrative. Ground #010101; fg #fff; a ladder of greys for the mid-page bands (#8e9291, #6c6e6b, the #505544 olive); a warm off-white #f1f0ea for the light editorial chapter. Two rationed accents: chartreuse #dff140 (edge/glow/"active") and electric blue #0000ee (link/interactive). Roles are value-and-chapter driven, so the brand chroma stays free for energy cues.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base. Tight 8–18px gaps cluster the mosaic and label stacks; a hard jump to 96–160px block padding separates chapters. Two-speed rhythm — dense media packed inside very large negative space.
- **Layout system:** 1440 desktop on a 12-column grid; the product mosaic is an asymmetric span layout (tall tile + varied cells), not a uniform card grid. Mobile (390) collapses the mosaic to a single stacked column and folds the nav (3 visible links → hamburger; `Company +` disclosure). Gutters 8–16px; section bands run full-bleed edge to edge.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Lenis-driven smooth/inertial scroll over the whole page; in-view reveals (the `inView` keyframe, 88 IntersectionObserver targets) and an animated link underline (`linkbutton-underline-in`). House easings are `cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1)` (expo-out, long & decelerating — "heavy machine settling") and `cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1)` (cubic-out), durations 125–1000ms. Communicates "weight and precision." Detail + per-technique safety below.
- **Density:** High locally in the mosaic (tight gaps, many tiles) but chunked by large chapter padding, so it never feels cramped — density is packed, then released.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **image scale + ground color**; type is a near-flat caption register and position (corner arrows, bottom-left labels) does the rest.
- **Restraint:** No rounded cards, no shadows (bar one glow), no gradient decoration, no weight ramp, accent never used as a fill. The expressiveness is concentrated entirely in the cinematic media and the chapter grounds; the chrome is austere on purpose.

## Interaction & motion
Anduril is a **smooth-scroll cinematic page, not a scroll-scrubbed spectacle.** The capture is blunt about where the motion lives: **Lenis** is the one library (`lenis.*.min.js`), driving inertial scroll (~399 `requestAnimationFrame` calls/pass), with **88 IntersectionObservers** (thresholds 0.05 and 0, several with `rootMargin: 250px`) gating in-view reveals and lazy media. Five `@keyframes` name the intent: `inView` (entrance reveal), `bar` (a loading/progress bar), `linkbutton-underline-in` / `-replay` (the animated link underline), `slider`. Only three scroll-driven effects diffed: the **nav pins** (`position: fixed`), a **`<canvas>` pins** (`position: fixed` — the hero render/video staying put as content scrolls over it), and a **parallax transform** on the wordmark/marquee div. There is 1 `<canvas>` + 1 `<video>` and 11 disclosure widgets (the `Company +` expander, mobile footer accordions). The style-diff probe reported `0` entrance reveals and `0` hover effects, but the `inView` keyframe + 88 IO targets + the underline keyframes contradict that — the probe samples at fixed scroll stops and misses reveals that already completed, so I trust the keyframe/IO evidence. **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`, `reduced_motion: false`) — the headline production liability, since Lenis hijacks native scroll for everyone. Frames cited: `corpus/expressive/screenshots/anduril/frames/`.

- **Lenis inertial smooth-scroll** — *Trigger:* scroll (continuous rAF). *Mechanism:* native scroll is intercepted and eased via a rAF loop, adding momentum/glide to the whole document. *Reads as:* weight and polish — the page moves like heavy hardware, not a spreadsheet. *Transfers to:* premium brand/landing pages wanting a "filmed" feel. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — hijacks native scroll, fights trackpad/keyboard/`scroll-into-view` expectations, adds main-thread cost, and here runs **even under reduced-motion** (the defect). Must gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference` and ship a native-scroll fallback.
- **In-view fade-rise gated by IntersectionObserver** — *Trigger:* in-view. *Mechanism:* the `inView` keyframe fires when a tile crosses a low threshold (0.05) with a 250px `rootMargin` pre-trigger, so media has already begun resolving before it reaches the viewport — fade + small rise. *Reads as:* the catalogue assembling itself as you descend. *Transfers to:* any media grid / long marketing page. *Safety:* **production-safe** when it animates only `transform`/`opacity` and the reduced-motion branch jumps to the end state — Anduril omits that branch, so copy the technique, not the omission. → `inview-media-reveal`.
- **Sticky-pinned hero canvas** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* a `<canvas>` (the hero render/video) holds `position: fixed` while the foreground content scrolls over it, with a parallax `transform` on the wordmark layer — a cheap depth illusion without true scroll-scrubbing. *Reads as:* the world sits behind a moving HUD. *Transfers to:* a fixed cinematic backdrop under a scrolling first section. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — a pinned canvas/video is GPU + decode cost; pause off-screen, supply a static poster, and freeze under reduced-motion.
- **Animated link underline** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* `linkbutton-underline-in` draws an underline from one edge on hover and `-replay` re-runs it; on the nav links and every `Read More ↗`. *Reads as:* crisp, deliberate affordance — the one place the austere chrome comes alive on pointer. *Transfers to:* any text-link nav. *Safety:* **production-safe** (animate `transform: scaleX` / `clip` on a pseudo-element; cheap, degrades to a static underline under reduced-motion).

## Production-safety note
- **Media-first hierarchy + tracked micro-caps + chapter grounds** — production-safe; the whole static identity is portable with no perf or a11y cost. Ensure label-on-image contrast holds (Anduril uses a bottom scrim — keep it).
- **Rationed chartreuse glow / blue accent** — production-safe. Cost: none. Don't let the accent be the *only* signifier of an interactive state.
- **2px radius + no shadows** — production-safe; recommended for a "machined" register.
- **Lenis smooth-scroll** — **spectacle-only.** Cost: scroll hijack, input-expectation breakage, main-thread; **measured defect: ignores `prefers-reduced-motion`.** Gate it and keep a native-scroll fallback.
- **Pinned hero canvas/video** — spectacle-only. Cost: GPU/decode; needs a poster fallback + off-screen pause + reduced-motion freeze.
- **a11y (measured, `craft.a11y`) — mixed.** *Good:* `focus_visible: true` (1px focus outline on nav links), `img_alt_ratio: 1` (all 11 images carry alt). *Fail:* `reduced_motion: false` — not respected anywhere, compounded by Lenis. *Watch:* 28 sub-44px tap targets (the micro-caps doubling as links) — bump hit areas on touch; and the raw electric-blue `#0000ee` link on near-black is a borderline-contrast / "unstyled default" smell — verify or restyle.
