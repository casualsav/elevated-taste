## What works

- **Spectacle from restraint, not decoration.** The whole site is two colors (white page, near-black stage) and one typeface. The "expressive" feeling is carried entirely by *scale*, *motion*, and *value inversion* — there is no gradient, no shadow, no chromatic accent. Transferable: you can read as a high-end studio on a strictly monochrome palette if you commit to one dramatic display scale and one signature transition; color restraint reads as confidence.
- **One typographic gesture sets the tier.** A ~6.75rem (108px) display headline in a single sans, with tight negative tracking (~-0.03em) and ~1.0 line-height, does all the brand work; everything else is quiet 16–18px body. The 6×+ jump from body to display is the hierarchy. Transferable to any landing hero that wants gravity without ornament.
- **Imagery is the only color source.** Project cards (bright product UI, renders) are the sole saturated elements, and they're isolated on the black stage so they pop. The chrome stays neutral so the work is the loudest thing on the page — the correct priority for a portfolio. Transferable: let content imagery be the palette; keep system color near-zero.
- **Section = grounded panel, not a divider.** The projects + footer live on one continuous dark slab with curled top corners (see `curved-stage-section`); the seam, not a rule line, marks the boundary and switches the theme. Transferable to galleries, pricing, testimonial walls — any block that wants a different mood from the page.
- **Secondary actions are hairlines until touched.** Outline pills (1px, fully rounded) stay subordinate to the type at rest and fill from the inside on hover (see `outline-pill-fill-button`). Transferable: spend interaction energy on the hover, keep the resting state the lightest possible mark so the page stays calm.
- **Long, eased reveals as the house motion.** Everything enters on a ~0.8–1.2s `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` ease-out. The slowness is the brand — it telegraphs "crafted, unhurried." Transferable as a single motion token applied uniformly so the whole site feels authored by one hand.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Single family ("Suisse Intl"); a wide ratio scale from ~16/18px body to 5rem and 6.75rem display (a ~6× range). Hierarchy is created purely by **scale + a size-graded tracking flip**: tracking goes *negative and tightens as type grows* (captured 81px → -1.62px ≈ -0.02em, 108px → -2.16px ≈ -0.02em) and *crosses slightly positive on the mid-size lead-in copy* (21.6px → +0.432px, 23.4px → +0.468px, ≈ +0.02em — a deliberate loosening on the intro paragraph), with body near neutral and ~1.4 leading. Captions/labels are ~12.6px in grey. No second family does the work — contrast is size, not pairing.
- **Color:** Two-tone. `bg` white, a single `stage` near-black (`#161616`), and one muted grey (`#888`) for captions/labels. **There is no accent hue** — the "accent" is the black inversion itself. Roles: page bg (white) / stage bg (black) / fg (near-black on white, white on stage) / muted grey for metadata. Saturation enters only through project thumbnails.
- **Spacing rhythm:** Generous, ~8px-derived but scaled large; sections breathe with ~5–11rem vertical padding and the hero leaves most of the first viewport empty. Whitespace is the primary separator (the hero copy floats in a sea of white; the "what we do" block is offset right with acres of margin).
- **Layout system:** Centered editorial hero, then an asymmetric two-column "since 2010 / what we do" block pushed to the right, then a project grid on the dark stage (a loose 2-up of large 18px-radius cards staggered vertically). Footer is a multi-column address/nav block on the same dark slab. Mobile collapses to a single column with a hamburger; the display type stacks and stays huge.
- **Grid / container:** content sits in a **wide ~1600px max-width container** (re-probed); the structural grid is just a **2-column → 1** asymmetric two-up content band (`gridCols ["2","1"]`, the band measured as a 2-child flex row) at a representative **~90px gutter**, not a fine 12-col system — the gallery is a staggered 2-up rather than a strict track grid, and most of the page is single-column whitespace, so there is no dense column rail. (The narrow ~756px figure is a component measure, not the container.) Mobile (390px) collapses 2→1 and reorders (`["1","2"]`), nav → hamburger (6 links → 1).
- **Motion / easing intent:** Two real easings carry the site — `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` (the signature long soft settle) and `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0,1)` (a decisive ease-out for snappier state changes) — over durations that cluster at **200 / 300 / 400 / 500 / 600 / 800 / 1200ms** (plus a `15000ms` outlier = the slow continuous badge spin); bare `ease` appears only as a fallback. The intent is "unhurried craft" — nothing snaps. Full choreography, evidence, and per-technique safety are in **Interaction & motion** below; the headline fact is that **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules:0`).
- **Density:** Very low. Each viewport carries one idea. The dark gallery is the densest region and stays legible because cards are large, captions are tiny and greyed, and there's heavy gutter between them.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant ordering device is **scale** (display vs body), reinforced by **value** (the dark stage promotes the gallery). Weight and color barely vary.
- **Restraint:** No gradients, no drop-shadows, no second typeface, no chromatic brand color, no borders except the hairline pills. Depth is implied (imagery, the dark panel), never drawn.

## Interaction & motion

Cuberto is a **studio-portfolio spectacle**: its reputation rides on a morphing custom cursor + slow, value-led reveals, but the actual motion budget is *far lighter than a teak/WebGL site* — there is **no canvas, no library, no IntersectionObserver, no draggable carousel, and only 5 `@keyframes`**. Evidence from the capture: 7 scroll effects (2 `pin`, 3 `parallax`, 1 `rotate`, 1 `scale`), 1 cursor-follower fed by **9 `mousemove` listeners**, **10 `scroll` listeners**, **2137 `requestAnimationFrame` calls** in one scroll pass (the cursor + scroll math run hot per-frame; no canvas to justify it), `@keyframes` = `cb-fx-blink` · `cb-fx-rotating-360` (the contact badge) · `cb-btn_menu-open-top`/`-bottom` (hamburger morph) · `cb-overview-icon-stroka`, 20 `<video>` (project preview loops), 0 `<canvas>`. Stack is **hand-rolled** (no GSAP/Lenis/Framer/Three globals) — bespoke scroll + pointer JS, 57 transition rules + 7 animation rules. The frames *did* capture the page well (s00 hero → s03 dark-stage project grid → s06 the curved seam + "Our services" → s09 footer), and the faint rotating "contact" badge is visible bottom-right in every frame; what frames can't show is the per-frame cursor morph, so that one is grounded in the probe counts + the `cb-cursor` matrix + `cb-fx-rotating-360`. **Critically, `prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected (`prmRules:0`, `a11y.reduced_motion:false`)** — the single biggest production liability here.

- **Morphing custom cursor (mouse-follower)** — *Trigger:* hover / mousemove (1 `cb-cursor -inverse -pointer` node, **9 `mousemove` listeners**, much of the 2137 rAF). *Mechanism:* a replacement cursor element lerps toward the pointer every frame, and its `transform` is a full `matrix(...)` (rotation + skew + scale + translate, captured drifting from `matrix(-0.45,-0.93,0.87,-0.42,…)` to `matrix(1.07,0.52,-0.36,0.73,…)`), so it *rotates and squashes* with velocity and inverts/grows over interactive targets — not a static dot. *Reads as:* the signature "alive, crafted" pointer that defines the Cuberto house style. *Transfers to:* brand-experience / agency heroes on fine-pointer devices only. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — pointer-only (does nothing on touch), per-frame `mousemove`+rAF main-thread cost, overrides the native cursor (an a11y + predictability risk), and must be the first thing dropped under a reduced-motion or perf budget. (No corpus recipe — `cursor-follow` is rare/spectacle; reuse the *principle* sparingly, fine-pointer-gated, never the only cursor.)
- **Pinned ambient rotating "contact" badge** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress + continuous loop (the `a/svg "contact-contact-…"` node: 1 `pin (fixed)` + 1 `rotate 118°` on scroll, plus the `cb-fx-rotating-360` keyframe; visible in s00/s06/s09). *Mechanism:* a circular-text "contact" SVG is `position: fixed` to the corner and its `transform: rotate()` is driven both by a slow continuous spin (the `15000ms` duration outlier) and nudged by scroll progress (~118° over the page). *Reads as:* a persistent, hypnotic "talk to us" affordance — ambient motion that never blocks content. *Transfers to:* a persistent contact/back-to-top affordance on any long brand page. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — decorative perpetual motion is a classic vestibular trigger; gate behind reduced-motion (it isn't here) and ensure the real contact link works with the spin frozen. → reuses the principle behind `scroll-progress-rotate` (cap/lock the angle), but the *perpetual* spin half is spectacle.
- **Layered parallax drift of media + headline words** — *Trigger:* in-view (3 `transform/parallax`: `div.cb-preview-media`, the nav cluster `"Services…Contacts"`, and the word-span `"Have"`). *Mechanism:* project-preview media and individual headline words translate at rates different from the page as their section enters, separating foreground media from background type into depth planes. *Reads as:* the work "floats" forward and the type assembles with a gentle drift — dimensional, not pasted-on. *Transfers to:* portfolio media and oversized headline words you want to feel layered. *Safety:* **prod-safe if subtle** — Cuberto's amount is restrained (only 3 layers vs teak's 14), compositor-only `transform`; keep it light and it ships. → `parallax-drift`.
- **In-view scale-up of headline copy** — *Trigger:* in-view (1 `scale`, captured `0.5→0.874` on span `"Tell us"`). *Mechanism:* a CTA/headline phrase scales from ~0.5 up toward full size as it crosses the viewport, settling on the `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` ease. *Reads as:* the closing "Tell us / Have an idea?" line *arrives* and asserts itself, reinforcing the slow-reveal house feel. *Transfers to:* a single hero/closing line you want to feel reader-summoned (pair with the long ease). *Safety:* **prod-safe** (compositor `transform`, one-shot); cap the start scale so text never renders sub-pixel/blurry mid-animation, and ship the resting size in the DOM. → `scroll-scale`.
- **Sticky nav fill on scroll** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (`div.cb-navbar-fill`, 1 `pin (fixed)`). *Mechanism:* the nav bar's backing fills/pins (`position: fixed`, paired with the `nav-scrim rgba(255,255,255,0.95)` role) so the menu stays reachable and legible once the dark stage scrolls under it. *Reads as:* a stable frame the monochrome page passes behind — the inverse of teak's pinned bar. *Transfers to:* persistent nav on any long single-page scroll, especially when the background switches light↔dark mid-page. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native fixed/sticky, no per-frame JS). → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Hover micro-states (card zoom + link wipe + label stretch)** — *Trigger:* hover (4 captured). *Mechanism:* project cards zoom their media `transform: scale(1.0288)` (the `cb-card-preview-media` image grows inside its 18px-radius clip); the "What we do" label stretches horizontally `scaleX(1.055)`; the contact link drops its underline (`underline→none`). *Reads as:* responsive, intentional chrome — the work nudges toward you on hover, the resting state stays calm. *Transfers to:* portfolio thumbnails (zoom-in-clip), text CTAs (underline/stretch). *Safety:* **production-safe** — `transform`/`text-decoration` only, GPU-cheap, reflow-free; keep the zoom subtle (~3%) so layout doesn't shift.

## Components

Resting-state component styles are **only partially captured** — the profile exposed focus and nav states richly but not full button/card/input resting tokens (`states.disabled` empty; no input nodes captured). Honest read:

- **Button:** two shapes seen, both **outline stadium pills** (1px hairline, fully rounded `1000px`/`9999px`): the white-on-dark "View all projects" / "Visit our blog" on the stage, and the inverse on white. Resting fill is transparent; the documented interaction is the **fill-from-inside on hover** (see `outline-pill-fill-button`) — but the *raw `states` block did not capture the hover fill tokens*, so exact fill color/timing is inferred from the pattern, not measured. No solid/primary button exists (the design has no accent to fill one). *Tap targets:* **7 flagged below minimum** — a real defect.
- **Card (project tile):** large 18px-radius media tile on the dark stage, **no border, no shadow** (`shadows:[]`), depth implied by the bright imagery against black. Resting state = flat; **hover = media `scale(1.0288)` zoom inside the clip** (captured). Caption is tiny (~12.6px) grey metadata beneath. No card padding/background token captured beyond the radius.
- **Nav:** 6 text links (`Services / Projects / Company / Blog / Contacts`) + wordmark; **resting = plain text, no pill/underline**; **hover** removes underline on the contact link and the label stretches; **focus = 1px solid `rgb(16,16,16)` outline** on every link (`focus_visible:true` — good). Collapses to a **hamburger** on mobile (the `cb-btn_menu-open-top/-bottom` keyframes morph the icon). The `cb-navbar-fill` adds a `rgba(255,255,255,0.95)` scrim on scroll.
- **Input:** **not captured** — no form/input nodes in the profile (the page surfaces contact via the rotating badge/link, not an inline form). State unknown; do not infer.

## Production-safety note

- **Curved-corner dark stage** — *production-safe.* Pure `background` + `border-radius` on a block; crawlable, keyboard-neutral, no JS. Cost: near-zero (one extra radius token). The only watch-item is contrast — keep captions above grey `#888` on black for AA on small text.
- **Outline pill with grow-from-inside fill** — *production-safe.* Pseudo-element + `transform: scale()`, GPU-cheap, reflow-free, reduces to an instant background swap under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Cost: trivial; mirror `:focus-visible` to `:hover` (done in the pattern) or keyboard users lose the affordance.
- **Giant display type at ~1.0 leading + negative tracking** — *production-safe*, with care. Cost: tight tracking + tight leading can clip descenders/ascenders and hurt legibility at very large sizes; cap the negative tracking (~-0.02 to -0.03em) and test wrapping on narrow mobile so words don't collide.
- **Uniform 0.8–1.2s ease-out reveals** — *production-safe but borderline.* Long entrance animations can feel sluggish and delay perceived load; gate them behind `prefers-reduced-motion` and ensure content is present in the DOM (not animation-dependent) for crawlers and no-JS. Cost: perceived-latency risk if overused above the fold.
- **Rotating circular "Contact" text badge / custom cursor** — *spectacle-only.* Decorative ambient motion; adds JS and a moving element that can distract or trigger motion sensitivity. Cost: minor perf + a11y; must respect reduced-motion and never be the only path to the contact action. Purely optional flourish.
- **A11y scorecard (measured, honest):** **Wins** — `img_alt_ratio: 1.0` (all 18 images carry alt text) and `focus_visible: true` (every nav link shows a 1px `rgb(16,16,16)` focus outline). **Defects** — (1) **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules:0`): the perpetual badge spin, the morphing cursor, the in-view scale/parallax and the long reveals all keep firing for motion-sensitive users — the expected expressive-site failure, and the top thing to fix before shipping this pattern. (2) **7 small tap targets** below the ~44px minimum on a 51-interactive-element page — tighten hit areas on mobile. (3) Custom cursor **overrides the native pointer** (predictability/visibility risk) and is fine-pointer-only. Borderline: grey `#888` captions on the black stage sit near the AA floor — keep them ≥ caption size.

Capture is a clean full-page render of the live site (hero, intro, projects on the dark stage, footer). **Not** a cookie wall or preloader.

## Extracted patterns

- **curved-stage-section** — a dark, large-top-radius panel the light page curls into; switches theme and marks a section boundary with geometry instead of a divider. Reuse for galleries, pricing, testimonial walls.
- **outline-pill-fill-button** — a hairline stadium button that fills from the inside on hover. Reuse for secondary CTAs on content-led pages where the resting state must stay quiet.
