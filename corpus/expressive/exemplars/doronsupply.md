## What works
- **One genre metaphor, applied to every component.** The whole store is dressed as retro software UI — products are "plugin windows" with title bars and spec strips, categories are a numbered file index, the bundle tiers are a config panel. Because the metaphor is consistent, the brand reads as a *coherent world* rather than decorated chrome. Transferable: pick one real-world schema (terminal, receipt, dashboard, cartridge) and express *every* section through it; consistency, not novelty, is what makes it feel designed.
- **Type does the genre-collision, not color.** A heavy serif/blackletter display ("Design Smarter, Not Harder", section titles like "Beyond The Basics") sits over an all-monospace body at one small size. The serif carries warmth/editorial weight; the mono carries the "instrument/system" register. The tension between the two *is* the personality, achieved with two families and near-zero color. Transferable anywhere you want character without a loud palette: pair a high-contrast display face with a utilitarian mono and let the register clash carry the brand.
- **Hierarchy by position + framing, not by weight.** Mono runs at ~12px nearly everywhere; almost nothing is bold. Order is created by *where* things sit (display headline centered, controls and metadata pinned to edges/corners) and by *typographic frames* (brackets `[01]`, title bars, spec strips). Transferable to dense catalog/dashboard UIs: when you strip scale and weight from body text, structure has to carry hierarchy, which reads calm even at high density.
- **A single rationed accent against near-black.** The page is ~black with one periwinkle/indigo (`#5b66de`) reserved almost exclusively for primary CTAs ("Explore Products", "Subscribe Now") and a faint hero glow. Everything else is greyscale + muted lavender. One saturated hue on a dark ground means every CTA is unmissable with zero extra emphasis. Transferable: ration your accent to *actions only* and the dark field does the rest.
- **The art lives in the product imagery, the chrome stays disciplined.** Glitch/dither/halftone thumbnails are wild; the frames around them (mono labels, hairline rules, bracket indices) are rigid and quiet. The contrast — chaotic content inside orderly chrome — is what keeps a maximal aesthetic from reading as noise. Transferable: when content is busy (3D, generative, photography), make the surrounding UI maximally restrained so the eye knows where the subject is.
- **Art direction survives the breakpoint.** On mobile the serif headline stays oversized (fills two lines edge-to-edge), the numbered index reflows from a horizontal rail to a 2-col grid keeping its `[0n]` markers, and the plugin-window card keeps its title bar + spec strip. Nothing collapses to a generic stacked-card mobile layout. Transferable: design the *components* to reflow (grid → fewer columns) rather than swapping in a separate mobile treatment, so the identity holds at every width.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** Three roles. (1) Display: heavy serif / blackletter-adjacent face (raw faces `ModelStandard Mono Trial Medium` / "Fuck Regular") at ~40–67px desktop with tracking that **tightens hard as it grows** — captured `40px → -3.6px` (~-0.09em) and `50px → -2px` (~-0.04em) — used only for hero + section titles. (2) Body/UI: `Space Mono` at ~11–13px, weight 400, for *everything* else (labels, prices, nav, spec strips, body copy), carrying a near-neutral negative tracking (`13px → -0.26px`, `12px → -0.24px`, ~-0.02em) plus `tabular-nums` so price/spec columns auto-align. A stray `16px → -0.96px` (~-0.06em) tightens the few mid-size mono lines. (3) Inter as a quiet sans fallback. Hierarchy is a ~4–5× jump from mono body straight to the serif display, with no mid-scale sans steps — the gap itself is the drama. (`fluid_type: false` — sizes step at the breakpoint, they don't `clamp()`.)
- **Color:** Near-black ground (`#0a0a0c`) with a subtle indigo bottom-gradient on the hero. Recurring dark card surface `#35363e`. Muted lavender-grey (`#b3b9c9`) for secondary surfaces (folder tabs) and dimmed text. One accent: periwinkle indigo `#5b66de` (+ a soft `rgba(110,104,222,0.4)` glow) on CTAs and small "SAVE $XX" badges. Effectively monochrome + one hue + one accent — semantic colors are absent (this is a vibe-led store, not a data UI).
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base. Tight inside components (4/8/12/16px gaps, mono padding ~14px inline) but very generous *between* sections (~80–120px block padding), so dense terminal panels still breathe. The density contrast — packed cards, airy section gaps — is deliberate pacing.
- **Layout system:** Full-bleed dark sections stacked vertically; content held to a wide centered container with large side padding (~80px). Products in a 4-up row (desktop) → 1-up (mobile). Recurring small dotted-square corner ticks (`⠿`) act as decorative section registration marks, reinforcing the "technical document" feel. Breakpoints observed: ~4-col desktop, 2-col index / 1-col products on mobile.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Restrained, snappy. The captured params are durations **240 / 100 / 4500ms** and easings `ease` · `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` (fast-out overshoot) · `ease-in-out`: the 100/240ms pair drives hover/interaction feedback, the lone 4500ms with `ease-in-out` is a slow *ambient loop* (the idle folder-tab wiggle / continuous spin), and a `400/800/1200/1600ms` ladder reads as a one-shot load stagger. Despite a Framer runtime (`script_main.D5qk1UWr.mjs`, 988 rAF/scroll), the actual *scroll* choreography is minimal — **1 scroll effect (the pinned nav), 0 entrance reveals, 0 cursor-followers, 0 canvas.** Motion communicates *responsiveness of an instrument*, not spectacle — short and mechanical, in keeping with the software metaphor. Full per-technique breakdown + safety in **Interaction & motion** below.

## Interaction & motion

Counter to its maximal *look*, doronsupply is motion-light where it counts: the capture shows **1 scroll-driven effect (a pinned nav), 0 entrance reveals, 0 cursor-followers, 0 `<canvas>`**, against 8 marquees, 6 `@keyframes` (`folderIdleWiggle_0..4` + `ds-i8nxrp-spin`), 12 hover effects, 10 `draggable` nodes, and 3 IntersectionObservers (threshold `0`, rootMargin `100px` — wired but driving no captured reveal). The 11 scroll frames are **distinct and clean** (normal scroll, no hijack), so the read is trustworthy: the "energy" is pre-baked into the glitch/dither art and a couple of looping flourishes, not a scroll engine. Stack: **Framer** (`script_main.*.mjs`) — JS-driven, but only **6 CSS `@keyframes`** and **0 CSS animation/transition rules** exposed. **Critically: `prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`, `a11y.reduced_motion: false`) — the marquees and the 4.5s idle wiggle never stop for a sensitive user.

- **Pinned utility nav** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (the single `pin (fixed)` effect on the `Shop deals MORE GET HELP PORTAL CART` bar). *Mechanism:* the top chrome holds `position: fixed` while every section scrolls beneath it — visible across all frames (s00→s09 keep the identical bar + the orange "[NEW DROPS]" feature plate top-right). *Reads as:* a persistent instrument panel framing the store; CART/PORTAL always reachable. *Transfers to:* any long catalog/storefront that wants a constant command bar. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky/fixed, no per-frame JS). → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Hover lift on index + nav links** — *Trigger:* hover (7 of the 12 hover effects: `[01]mockups`→`translateY(-6.97px)`, `[02]Textures`→`-6.50px`, `[03]tools`→`-6.40px` … each bracket-index cell nudges up ~6–7px; the `15% OFF / 3–5 Items` tier slides `translateX(-6.95px)`). *Mechanism:* a small compositor-only `transform` on pointer-enter, eased by the `cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1)` overshoot at ~100–240ms. *Reads as:* mechanical, tactile "key-press" feedback — the chrome behaves like instrument keys, matching the software metaphor. *Transfers to:* any list/nav of discrete targets where you want crisp, non-decorative hover affordance. *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform-only, cheap); no corpus recipe — reuse the principle (translate ≤8px on `:hover`/`:focus-visible`).
- **Glow-bloom hover on secondary button** — *Trigger:* hover (the `;IEW ALL` button: `bg rgb(179,185,201)→rgb(247,248,249)` lavender-grey→near-white, shadow `rgba(255,255,255,0)→rgba(255,255,255,0.27) 0 1px 18.8px 4.9px`). *Mechanism:* background lightens and a soft white outer glow blooms in on enter. *Reads as:* the glassy/holographic surface "powering on." *Transfers to:* secondary CTAs on dark glass panels. *Safety:* **production-safe** (bg + box-shadow; keep a non-glow focus ring for a11y — glow alone isn't a focus signal). No recipe.
- **Idle folder-tab wiggle** — *Trigger:* load/ambient loop (5 distinct `@keyframes folderIdleWiggle_0..4` — one per tab — running on the 4.5s `ease-in-out`). *Mechanism:* each "Shop By Type" folder card holds a small, perpetually-looping rotate/translate so the stack subtly breathes at rest (s05 shows the five tilted cards fanned in a perspective row: psychedelic tile · "DRK TOOL textures" · halftone tee · circular-type disc · "DGLITINT"). *Reads as:* alive, tactile shelf of physical media — folders quivering as if hand-held. *Transfers to:* a hero/section centerpiece you want to feel animate without scroll. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — perpetual main-thread loop, no reduced-motion gate (a motion-sickness + battery vector); cap amplitude, pause off-screen, and *must* stop under `prefers-reduced-motion`. → genuinely new (`patterns_to_build`).
- **Perspective folder-tab carousel** — *Trigger:* drag/tap (10 `draggable` nodes; the same "Shop By Type" stack). *Mechanism:* large image cards laid on a `perspective` stage, draggable laterally with per-card rotate/Z so they recede into depth (s05 = front card upright, neighbours tilted back). *Reads as:* riffling through a physical folder rack — the spectacle centerpiece. *Transfers to:* a tactile category/collection browser on a brand-experience page. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — 3D transform-heavy, drag-only (no obvious keyboard/touch-tap path observed), easy to jank; ship a flat scrollable grid fallback + reduced-motion off. The corpus `perspective-curved-ticker` is a *vertical receding text crawl* — a different mechanism — so this is **not** that; → `patterns_to_build`.
- **Marquee tickers** — *Trigger:* continuous/ambient (8 `marquee` instances). *Mechanism:* infinite horizontal scroll of short labels/wordmarks (the page's mono "technical-document" registration strips). *Reads as:* a live data feed / terminal scroller reinforcing the instrument metaphor. *Transfers to:* announcement bars, logo/credit strips, status rails. *Safety:* **production-safe** *if* it pauses on hover/focus and honors reduced-motion — here it does **neither**, so as shipped it's a liability. → `announcement-ticker-marquee` (the corpus recipe adds the pause + reduced-motion the site omits).
- **Density:** High inside cards (title bar + thumb + 3-cell spec strip + wordmark plate + price + add-item, all in one tile) yet legible because mono auto-aligns columns and frames segment the information. Section-level density is low (lots of black). The page feels rich but never cramped.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device = **typographic framing + position**. Brackets, title bars, and spec strips rank elements; scale jump (serif display vs mono) marks the top of the page; the lone accent marks actions. Weight is barely used.
- **Restraint:** No drop shadows (only faint white glows on glassy panels), no rounded-card softness (radii are tiny, 3–8px), no gradient buttons, no second accent hue, almost no bold weight, no semantic color system. The maximalism is entirely in *content imagery and the metaphor*, not in the UI primitives.
- **Grid / container:** content sits in a **wide ~1600px max-width container** (re-probed; Framer flex/absolute layout, not CSS Grid tracks, so the bands are flex rows rather than grid columns). The re-probe measures the content bands as flex rows of **2 / 3 / 5 / 7** children: the **3-up bundle-tier panel** (s06: 15% / 20% / 25% OFF) and the **5-card** "Shop By Type" perspective carousel (s05) are now corroborated by the flex counts, while the **4-up product row** (s03) and the closing **4-up** "Professional Design" mosaic (s09) remain a frame read. Mobile collapses to `["2"]` (index → 2-col) and 1-up products.
- **Components** (from `.craft.states` — only `focus` was captured; the rest are inferred from hover deltas + frames and flagged):
  - **Button** — primary = solid periwinkle `#5b66de` fill, mono uppercase label, tiny radius (3–8px) ("EXPLORE PRODUCTS", "Subscribe Now"); secondary = lavender-grey `#b3b9c9` "VIEW ALL" that lightens to near-white `#f7f8f9` + white glow-bloom on hover. **Resting/active/disabled fills not captured** (`states.disabled: []`); `:focus` = **1px `rgb(16,16,16)` outline** (near-invisible on the near-black bg — an a11y defect, see below).
  - **Card** — the terminal/plugin-window tile (title bar + glitch thumb + 3-cell mono spec strip + wordmark plate + price + Add row), tiny radius, hairline border, faint white inner/outer glow instead of a drop shadow. No captured hover state on the card body (hover deltas land on its inner links, not the tile) — **card hover not captured**.
  - **Nav** — pinned top bar of mono uppercase links; per-item **hover = `translateY(-6…-7px)` lift**; `:focus` = same 1px `rgb(16,16,16)` outline. No background/active-route state captured. (`navVisibleLinks: 0` in raw — the crawler didn't resolve the labels; the frames show them clearly.)
  - **Input** — **not captured** (`states` has no input entry; no visible text field in the frames — search/forms likely live behind GET HELP / PORTAL). Treat as unknown; if rebuilt, give mono inputs a real ≥16px size to dodge the body-mono legibility trap.
- **A11y:** Mixed, with the expected expressive defects. **Good:** `focus_visible: true` (focus rings present) and `img_alt_ratio: 1` (**all 53 images carry alt text** — unusually thorough for a vibe-led store). **Bad:** (1) `reduced_motion: false` — **`prefers-reduced-motion` is not respected**; the 8 marquees + 4.5s idle wiggle run regardless (motion-sickness + battery liability). (2) The focus outline is **1px `rgb(16,16,16)`** — dark grey on a `#0a0a0c` near-black ground, so the ring is essentially invisible; functionally focus is *declared but not perceivable*. (3) **33 of 70 interactive targets are below comfortable tap size** (`small_tap_targets: 33` ≈ 47%) — the mono micro-labels and bracket-index cells are finger-hostile. (4) Body copy at **12px Space Mono** sits under the comfortable legibility floor. Net: visually fastidious about alt text, but fails reduced-motion, focus-contrast, and tap-target guidance — typical expressive trade-offs.

## Production-safety note
- **terminal-window-product-card** — *production-safe.* Pure HTML/CSS (border, title bar, grid spec strip). Cost: dense decorative chrome adds DOM nodes and the tiny mono labels (~11–12px) are below comfortable a11y minimums — keep *essential* text (price, product name, Add button) at a real readable size and treat the decorative spec strip as supplementary. Crawlable, keyboard-fine, degrades to a plain bordered card.
- **bracketed-index-rail** — *production-safe.* A flex/grid of numbered links; reflows to a 2-col grid on mobile. Cost: none of note; the `[0n]` numerals are decorative, so ensure each cell's *label* is the real link text for screen readers.
- **All-monospace body at ~12px** — *spectacle-only (use with care).* It nails the aesthetic but monospace at 12px is slower to read and fails comfortable contrast/size guidance for long copy; fine for labels/prices/short marketing lines, risky for paragraphs or checkout/legal text. Cost: legibility + a11y. In production, switch body copy and any transactional flows (cart, forms, errors) to a readable size/face and reserve the mono purely for chrome.
- **Heavy serif/blackletter display headline** — *production-safe with a caveat.* Visually load-bearing and cheap (one webfont). Cost: blackletter-adjacent faces have low readability — keep them to a few large words (headlines only), never body or buttons; ensure a real font fallback so the page isn't unreadable if the font fails.
- **Glitch/dither product thumbnails + faint glows** — *production-safe.* These are static images, not runtime effects, so no perf cost; the glows are CSS box-shadows. Spectacle is pre-baked into the art, which is the cheap, robust way to look maximal.
- **Perspective folder-tab carousel ("Shop By Type")** — *spectacle-only.* A 3D/parallax card stack is JS + transform-heavy and easy to make janky or unusable on touch/keyboard. Cost: perf + a11y + interaction complexity. Provide a flat scrollable/grid fallback for reduced-motion and small screens.
