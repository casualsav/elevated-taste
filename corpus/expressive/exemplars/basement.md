## What works
- **A single fixed body size + scale-only hierarchy.** Body text is locked at 16px/1.5 across essentially the whole page (~334 instances); the only escalation is raw scale — 38px section heads, 76px display. There is no weight ramp doing the work (it stays 400/600). On a dark, dense page this keeps the texture calm and uniform while letting the few large moments hit hard. Transfers to any content-heavy site: pick one body size and earn hierarchy through deliberate size jumps, not five weights.
- **Optical letter-spacing that tightens as type grows.** Tracking ramps negative with size — body ~0, 38px heads at -1.52px, 76px display at -3.04px. Big grotesque type left at default tracking reads loose and cheap; pulling letters together at display scale is what makes it look set, not typed. A transferable rule: scale your negative tracking with font-size.
- **One rationed accent, used only as edge + glow.** The entire palette is greyscale-on-black except a warm orange (#ff4d00) that appears *only* on hairline borders and a faint glow — never as a fill or large area. Scarcity is the mechanism: a single hue touching <2% of pixels carries more brand signal than a saturated section would. Directly portable to any restrained dark theme.
- **The literal brand as the hero scene.** The hero is a 3D wireframe of an actual basement (staircase, framing, room) rendered in white line-art on black — the studio's name made spatial. The principle (not the asset): when a brand has a concrete noun, render that noun as the hero instead of an abstract gradient; it's memorable and self-explaining. Spectacle-only, but the *idea* is cheap to adopt with a static illustration.
- **Off-white body, not pure white.** Body copy is #e6e6e6, with #fff reserved for wordmarks/emphasis. On a true-black ground, pure-white body vibrates and fatigues; dropping to ~90% grey reduces glare and quietly creates a second emphasis tier (#fff = "louder"). A near-universal dark-mode fix.
- **Editorial case rows over a card grid.** Featured work is a stack of full-bleed media rows with a right-aligned title + tiny meta + one-line outcome, not a uniform card grid. The asymmetry (huge image vs. terse text column) reads like a magazine spread and lets each project's own colour be the variety, so the chrome stays monochrome. Transfers to any portfolio/case index.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** Geist (grotesque sans) as the near-sole family; a `flauta` serif/display face is loaded but barely surfaced. Scale is wide-range, ~6.5× from 13px micro-labels up to an 87px H1 (the captured top heading; 76px is the secondary display tier), with the body pinned at 16px — a *bimodal* scale (lots of 16px, a few large) rather than a smooth modular ramp. Hierarchy = scale + position; weight only toggles 400↔600. Numeric-tabular + `ss01` font-features are enabled (the count badges / `BSMNT.26` read as set type, not default).
- **Tracking (measured, `type_detail.tracking_by_size`):** negative tracking scales with size — `20px → -0.4px`, `24px → -0.72px`, `76px → -3.04px`, `87px H1 → -3.48px` (a steady ~-0.04em ratio held across the display tier). Body (16px) sits at ~0. So the optical-tightening rule is literal and proportional: roughly -4% of font-size at every step up. (No positive-tracked uppercase eyebrow surfaced in the capture; `letter-spacing` is the *tightening* lever only.)
- **Color:** Inverted. #000 ground, #e6e6e6 body, #fff emphasis, #757575 muted meta, #c4c4c4 secondary. Single accent #ff4d00 as border/glow only. Roles are value-driven (black→white = quiet→loud), so brand colour stays free for one job.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 4px base for micro-gaps (labels, nav), then a hard jump to a 56–128px macro band for section separation. The page is rhythmically two-speed: tight clusters set inside very large negative space.
- **Layout system:** 1440 desktop. Asymmetric editorial grid — left-weighted hero copy, full-bleed media with a right-rail text column for cases, and a footer that pairs a giant wordmark with a stacked nav index. Mobile collapses to a single column; the basement 3D scene survives as the hero.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Two transition registers (`motion.params`): `60ms linear` = instant micro-feedback (hover, 39 uses) and `300ms cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` (Material standard ease, 46 uses) for the few larger transitions, with `100/200ms` of the same curve as intermediates (4 uses each) and a bare `ease` default. Communicates "responsive and precise" (snappy hover) plus "considered" (the slower eased curve). But the *body* barely animates on scroll — `0` entrance reveals, `0` stagger, only `4` `pin (fixed)` scroll effects; the real continuous motion is **three always-on `<canvas>` loops** (~923 rAF/scroll-pass), not transitions. Full breakdown, evidence, and per-technique safety in **Interaction & motion** above.
- **Density:** High locally (tight 4px clusters, dense logo grid of ~30 marks) but spaced by large section gaps, so it never feels cramped — density is chunked, not uniform.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **scale**, reinforced by **position** (right-aligned project titles, edge-pinned nav). Colour and weight are secondary levers.
- **Restraint:** Zero border-radius (everything squared), no real shadows (only one micro-glow), no gradient backgrounds, no multi-weight type ramp, accent never used as a fill. The expressiveness is concentrated entirely in the 3D hero and the per-project media — the chrome is austere.
- **Grid / container:** 1440 desktop; a **12-column** editorial grid (`craft.grid.columns: [1, 12, 8, 3]` — i.e. spans of 1 / full-12 / an 8-col media block + a 3-col text rail, the case-row split visible in s03/s06). On mobile (390) it collapses to a **4-column** grid (`[1, 4, 3, 2]`) and the 6 nav links fold into a hamburger (`likely_hamburger: true`, `mobile_links: 0`). No fluid type (`fluid_type: false`) — H1 steps 87px desktop → 46px mobile, body holds 16px at both. Measured gutters run **~4px** for the micro-clusters up to ~8–12px between blocks. The only container candidate measured was **1920px** — i.e. the layout runs full-bleed/edge-to-edge (no real centered max-width), with the case rows bleeding to the viewport edge in the frames.
- **Components (from `craft.states` — thin; most rest/hover states were not isolated):**
  - **Button** — *rest not captured* as a styled fill (the only `disabled` snapshot shows `bg: transparent`, `color: #fff`, `cursor: default`, `opacity: 1` → buttons are effectively text/links, not filled pills, consistent with the zero-radius austere chrome). *Hover:* text → accent `#ff4d00` (`Contact Us`). *Focus:* a **doubled box-shadow ring** — `0 0 0 2px #000, 0 0 0 3px #ff4d00` (a black inset gap then a 1px orange ring), with the native `outline` made transparent. Same focus ring on every nav link and button.
  - **Card** — *not captured as a discrete component.* The "cards" are the case-study split rows (full-bleed media + right text rail) and the showcase/logo plates, which carry **no border, radius, or shadow** — they rest at `opacity: 0.5` and reveal to `1` on hover (the dim-to-reveal gallery behavior). Grouping is by whitespace + the per-project media color, not chrome.
  - **Nav** — fixed top bar, 6 text links + `Contact Us` + a `·····` glyph cluster; counts inline as superscript-style badges (`Showcase (25)`, `Blog (28)`). *Hover:* `color #e6e6e6 → #ff4d00`, some links also `opacity 1 → 0.5`, `0.06s linear`. *Focus:* the doubled black+orange ring above. Pins on scroll (see motion).
  - **Input** — *not captured.* A contact form exists (the pinned `CONTACT US … SEND MESSAGE → X (Twitter)` modal, an `accordion-up/down` disclosure), but no field rest/focus/error styling was probed; treat input styling as **unknown**.

## Interaction & motion

basement is an **agency/spectacle site whose spectacle is GPU canvas, not scroll-choreography** — closer to "always-on WebGL ambience + austere DOM chrome" than to teak's Framer fling. The motion capture is blunt about where the heat is: **3 `<canvas>` nodes and ~923 `requestAnimationFrame` calls in one scroll pass** (the continuous render loops — the wireframe-basement hero in s00, the dithered point-cloud "Vercel Ship" plate in s03), against **0 entrance reveals, 0 staggered entrances, and only 4 scroll-driven effects — all of them `pin (fixed)`** (the nav bar, a `pointer-events-none` overlay, and two modal shells: `Close [ESC]` and the `CONTACT US … SEND MESSAGE` form). So the *page body* barely animates on scroll; the liveliness is canvas ambience plus crisp hover feedback. Hand-rolled, **no library** (`libraries: []`, `scriptHits: []`): 12 `@keyframes` (`bounce`, `fade-in`, `fade-in-out`, `fade-out-in`, `pulse`, `spin`, `actionable-blink`, `diagonalPatternAnimation`, `marquee-translate`, `subtle-pulse`, `accordion-up`, `accordion-down`) + 10 CSS animation rules + 1 IntersectionObserver (`rootMargin: 200px`, prefetch-style, 0 observed targets at capture). 13 `mousemove` listeners are registered but **0 cursor-followers** fired — they likely feed the WebGL camera/parallax, not DOM garnish. **Critically: `prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`, `craft.a11y.reduced_motion: false`) — the headline production liability, compounded by three always-running canvas loops. Frames cited below are `corpus/expressive/screenshots/basement-studio/frames/`; they captured the page well (s00 hero, s03 logo-wall + first case, s06 hot-pink "Shop MrBeast" case, s09 contact + `BSMNT.26` masthead) — the *continuous* canvas motion is the part the stills can't show, so it's grounded in the rAF/canvas probe counts and `@keyframes` names below, not invented.

- **Always-on WebGL ambient hero** — *Trigger:* page-load / continuous (and `mousemove`, 13 listeners feeding camera). *Mechanism:* a `<canvas>` rAF loop (~923 calls/scroll-pass across 3 canvases) renders a white wireframe of a literal basement — staircase, framing, roof trusses — on `#000`, with drifting dithered-pixel particles (s00). *Reads as:* you've stepped *inside* the studio; the brand-noun made spatial and alive rather than a static illustration. *Transfers to:* a brand with a concrete noun that wants a signature hero. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — GPU/CPU + bundle weight, invisible to crawlers/keyboards, and **runs even under reduced-motion** (the defect). Must layer over a real DOM hero with a static poster fallback and a `prefers-reduced-motion` freeze. → `rim-lit-void-stage` (the white-line-on-void staging) / `inset-canvas-stage` (canvas-as-figure).
- **Point-cloud / dithered render as case media** — *Trigger:* in-view → continuous canvas. *Mechanism:* a project plate is itself a live `<canvas>` particle render (the grayscale point-cloud swelling into frame on the "Vercel Ship" / Websites & Features row, s03), not a flat thumbnail. *Reads as:* "this studio renders things in real time" — the medium is the proof. *Transfers to:* a single hero/case asset for a 3D/creative-dev shop. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — another rAF canvas; lazy-init on in-view, pause off-screen, and ship a static image fallback (none captured). → `ascii-particle-ring` (particle-field-as-figure on void).
- **Pinned nav + pinned modal shells** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (the 4 captured `pin (fixed)` effects). *Mechanism:* the `Home / Services / Showcase (25) / People / Blog (28) / Lab / Contact Us` bar holds `position: fixed` while the page (and canvas) scroll beneath it (visible identically pinned in s00, s03, s09); the `Close [ESC]` and `CONTACT US … SEND MESSAGE` overlays pin as full-viewport modal layers. *Reads as:* a stable HUD/frame over a moving stage — the studio's chrome stays put while the work performs. *Transfers to:* persistent nav/CTA and modal layers on any long animated page. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native fixed, no per-frame JS). → `wordmark-nav-index` (the index-style nav with count badges) / `monospace-hud-chrome` (the `[ESC]`-tagged overlay register).
- **Hover: link → accent + half-fade** — *Trigger:* hover (12 captured effects). *Mechanism:* nav links flip `color rgb(230,230,230)→rgb(255,77,0)` and several drop `opacity 1→0.5` on the same gesture, over the house `0.06s linear` (39 uses) — instant. *Reads as:* snappy, precise, "responsive" — the one place the austere chrome comes alive on pointer. *Transfers to:* any dark-theme nav wanting a single-accent hover. *Safety:* **production-safe**, but the a11y catch the static read already flagged bites here: orange + a 50% opacity drop is the *only* hover signifier, and orange-on-black hover state shouldn't be the sole affordance — pair with underline/weight for color-blind users.
- **Hover: case thumbnail 0.5 → 1 opacity (gallery dim-to-reveal)** — *Trigger:* hover (the `a.aspect-[202/110]` thumbnails). *Mechanism:* showcase/logo plates rest at `opacity: 0.5` and resolve to `1` on hover, over `0.06s linear`. *Reads as:* a dimmed contact-sheet that lights the item under the cursor — focus by subtraction. *Reads in frames:* the logo wall in s03 sits muted; per-project full color (the hot-pink "Shop MrBeast" plate, s06) is what carries variety once lit. *Transfers to:* any portfolio/logo grid wanting hover focus without scale or shadow. *Safety:* **production-safe** (opacity is compositor-cheap); ensure resting items stay ≥ a readable contrast even at 0.5 so the grid isn't unreadable pre-hover. → `dimmed-color-case-stack`.
- **Diagonal-hatch panel + marquee/blink accents** — *Trigger:* continuous CSS animation. *Mechanism:* `@keyframes diagonalPatternAnimation` drifts the striped hatch behind the Contact block (the angled cross-hatch fill behind "Let's make an impact together", s09); `marquee-translate` scrolls a logo/label ticker; `actionable-blink` / `subtle-pulse` blink small "live"/actionable cues. *Reads as:* low-key industrial texture — the surface is never fully dead even where the DOM is static. *Transfers to:* footer/contact texture, status ticker. *Safety:* **production-safe if paused under reduced-motion** (currently it is *not* — these loops ignore `prefers-reduced-motion`); cap to a slow, low-contrast drift to avoid a shimmer/motion-sickness vector. → `announcement-ticker-marquee` (the marquee), with the hatch drift as a `patterns_to_build` candidate.

## Production-safety note
- **Scale-only hierarchy with a fixed body size** — production-safe. Cost: near-zero; improves consistency. Adopt freely.
- **Size-scaled negative letter-spacing** — production-safe. Cost: none; a few `letter-spacing` values per heading tier. Verify legibility isn't lost at the tightest setting.
- **Rationed accent as border/glow only** — production-safe. Cost: none. Ensure the accent isn't the *only* signifier of an interactive state (a11y: don't rely on the orange hairline alone for focus/active).
- **Off-white (#e6e6e6) body on black** — production-safe; recommended. Cost: none; better contrast comfort than #fff.
- **3D wireframe basement hero + canvas case media (WebGL)** — spectacle-only. Cost: GPU/CPU + bundle weight; invisible to crawlers and keyboards. **Measured defect:** 3 `<canvas>` loops (~923 rAF/scroll-pass) run **even under reduced-motion** (`prmRules: 0`). Must layer over a real DOM hero with a static fallback and a `prefers-reduced-motion` freeze/poster — basement does *not*, so copy the look, not this behavior.
- **Pure-black #000 ground** — mostly safe, mild caveat: true black raises perceived contrast and can cause halation with thin white text; basement mitigates by using #e6e6e6 body. On OLED it also induces smearing on scroll. A very dark grey (#0a0a0a) is the safer default; #000 is a deliberate brutalist choice here.
- **Case-study split rows** — production-safe. Cost: none; just an asymmetric grid. The full-bleed media should be lazy-loaded/responsive.
- **a11y (measured, `craft.a11y`) — mixed, with one hard failure.** *Good:* `focus_visible: true` and a strong, deliberate focus ring (doubled black+orange box-shadow on every link/button); `img_alt_ratio: 1` (all 36 images carry alt text). *Fail:* `reduced_motion: false` — `prefers-reduced-motion` is **not respected** anywhere (canvas loops + `diagonalPatternAnimation`/`marquee-translate`/`actionable-blink` keep running) — the site's biggest accessibility liability and a motion-sickness vector. *Watch:* 30 of 89 interactive targets are flagged **small tap targets** (sub-44px) — the index-nav links and micro-meta; bump hit areas for touch. *Color-only state:* hover relies on orange `#ff4d00` + opacity alone (no underline/weight) — add a non-hue affordance.
