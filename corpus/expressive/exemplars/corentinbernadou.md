## What works (transferable principles)

- **One loud accent on a near-black canvas = all the energy, one decision.** The whole page is `#070304` black with grey body text; a single saturated orange-red (`#ff4401`) carries the giant wordmark and the rare highlight (one cube tile, one ticker accent). Mechanism: a monochrome-plus-one-hue system means the accent is *never* in competition, so it reads as pure emphasis wherever it lands — the eye goes straight to it. Transfers to any dark UI that needs a brand "shout" without a rainbow: pick one chromatic accent, keep everything else neutral, and spend the accent only on the 1–2 things that must win. Production-safe, and cheaper to maintain than a multi-accent palette.

- **Tiny mono-spaced-feeling chrome makes the work look "published," not posted.** Corners carry magazine apparatus — a `1440px × 900px` dimension readout, `Issue N°003 / Coll. 2026`, `Ref. EDI-032026-R02`, `Available July 2026` — all in ~11–13px tight-tracked uppercase-ish grey. Mechanism: editorial framing (issue/edition/reference numbers, availability) borrows the authority of print and signals craft *before* the user reads a single project. Transfers to any portfolio, launch page, or product "about" surface that wants gravitas — the metadata is nearly free to add and recolors the whole tone.

- **Two type sizes do the hierarchy; weight stays flat.** Body and nav sit at ~11–13px in a single weight (600); the only big thing is the display wordmark, which is enormous (a ~25–40× jump). Mechanism: when the size ratio is extreme, you never need bold/light to rank things — scale alone orders the page, and weight is freed for state (active nav is brighter, not heavier). Transfers broadly; in product UI cap the ratio nearer 4–6× so mid-level text (sub-nav, captions) still has a legible rung.

- **Active state = brightness, not decoration.** In the nav (`Featured Works, Archive, About`) the current item is near-white while the rest are mid-grey — no underline, pill, or box. Mechanism: on a monochrome system, a single step up the value scale is a complete, unambiguous "you are here" signal that adds zero new material. Transfers to any minimalist nav/tab/menu; pair it with a real focus ring for keyboard users since brightness alone isn't an accessible focus indicator.

- **Margins hold metadata, center holds spectacle.** The live content (wordmark, rotating image cube, ticker) owns the optical center while all chrome is pinned to the four corners and edges. Mechanism: separating navigation/credits (margins) from the hero object (center) lets each be dense without crowding — the page feels minimal yet is information-rich. Transfers to dashboards, editorial layouts, and any hero that needs chrome without clutter.

- **Motion is the medium: a 3D image-object + a curved ticker carry the "wow."** The hero is a rotating Rubik's-cube of project thumbnails; a project list is mapped onto a receding 3D path so it bends and shrinks into the void. Mechanism: the expressiveness is bought with *spatial motion and scale*, not with color, gradients, or ornament — so the underlying system stays clean and the spectacle is isolated to a couple of GPU-driven objects you can lazy-load or disable. Transfers as a strategy (concentrate the spectacle, keep the chrome plain); the specific 3D devices are spectacle-only (see safety note).

## Design-system read

- **Type:** One family — a Helvetica-class grotesque (`H, HelveticaNeue, Helvetica, sans-serif`), **single weight 600** (a stray 700 on a couple of nodes). Effectively **two sizes**: ~11.4px (0.71rem) and ~13.3px (0.83rem) for body/nav, plus a huge display wordmark (`FW`) far off the body scale. Only one size was profiled for tracking — **11.4px carries -0.229px (≈ -0.02em)**, a deliberate tightening at small sizes (the off-scale display wordmark is WebGL-rendered and not in the static profile, but a Helvetica wordmark at that scale would tighten further still). No fluid `clamp()` type; sizes are fixed (h1/body hold ~11.4px desktop → ~11.7px at 390px — essentially no mobile downscale). Hierarchy is **scale + value**, never weight.
- **Color:** Near-black canvas `#070304`; body text two greys (`#767676` primary, `#6d6d6d` secondary) with a brighter `#cacaca` for active/emphasis; **one accent** `#ff4401` (orange-red) for the wordmark + sparse highlight tiles. No semantic palette — this is monochrome-plus-one. Imagery (the cube tiles) is the only other color source.
- **Spacing rhythm:** Small base (~8px). Box padding is tiny (~2–3px) for the brutalist edge-to-edge corner labels; large structural gaps (~32px, ~122px) separate the corner blocks from the center. Contrast of micro-padding vs macro-voids is the rhythm.
- **Layout system:** Corner-anchored frame — masthead top-left, nav top-center, status top-right, dimension readout far top-left; hero object + ticker in the center-right field. Essentially a fixed-corners "instrument panel" around a free-floating 3D centerpiece. (Mobile not capturable — see note.)
- **Motion / easing intent:** Two captured durations — **300ms** (hover/state) and **750ms** (the staged wordmark/loader reveal) — over easings `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)` (ease-out-quad, the house hover curve), a bare `ease`, and `steps(1, start)` (the discrete stepping of the `pulse` keyframe). 16 transition rules vs only 2 `@keyframes` (`pulse`, `nw-marquee`). The short curve = polish; the WebGL cube loop (Three.js, ~4187 rAF/scroll pass) = show. Full choreography, evidence, and per-technique safety in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** Looks sparse, reads dense — full nav, identity, edition metadata, availability, and a project list are all present, kept legible by pushing detail to tiny corner type and letting the center stay open.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **scale** (giant wordmark vs whisper chrome), reinforced by **the single accent hue** and **value steps** for active state.
- **Restraint:** No gradients, no shadows (one ~4px radius on a single node, otherwise square), no second font, essentially one weight, no decorative color beyond the lone accent. The wow is concentrated into two motion objects; everything else is deliberately plain.

## Interaction & motion

This is a **WebGL/scroll-hijack spectacle**, the inverse of an IntersectionObserver reveal page: **0 IO observers, 0 entrance reveals, 0 cursor-followers**, but **Three.js + GSAP loaded, 1 `<canvas>`, ~4187 `requestAnimationFrame` calls in one scroll pass**, 2 scroll listeners, 1 mousemove listener, and only **2 `@keyframes` (`pulse`, `nw-marquee`)**. The rAF flood + single canvas = one continuous 3D render loop (the cube), not a per-element timeline. Easings: `cubic-bezier(0.25, 0.46, 0.45, 0.94)` + bare `ease` + `steps(1, start)`; durations 300 / 750ms. **Critically, `prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected (`prmRules: 0`, `a11y.reduced_motion: false`)** — the single biggest production liability. Stack: **Three.js** (`three-*.js`) · **GSAP** (`gsap-*.js`, ScrollTrigger-style pins) · CSS `@keyframes`. The frame sequence DID capture the cube clearly (`corpus/expressive/screenshots/corentinbernadou-com/frames/`), so the motion below is grounded in what the frames show, corroborated by the probe counts.

- **Auto-rotating 3D thumbnail cube (WebGL)** — *Trigger:* load → continuous loop, nudged by pointer (1 mousemove listener; no DOM cursor-follower). *Mechanism:* a Rubik's-cube of project thumbnails rendered to a single `<canvas>` via Three.js, re-rendered every frame (~4187 rAF/scroll). *Reads as:* the hero IS the portfolio — browsing the work is the landing page; s00 shows a near-flat tilted grid plane, s06 the full 3D box with top + two side faces and one orange accent tile, s09 a fresh rotation presenting new thumbnails. *Transfers to:* a portfolio/gallery hero where you want "look how much work there is" as latent density in one navigable object instead of a 2D grid. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — continuous GPU/CPU/battery cost, large thumbnail payload, invisible to crawlers/keyboard/reduced-motion. Lazy-load, pause off-screen, never block first paint, and layer it over a real crawlable DOM gallery. → `webgl-thumbnail-sphere`.
- **Scroll-pinned chrome + center stage** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (2 `pin (fixed)` effects, GSAP). *Mechanism:* the top header cluster (`1440px × 900px` readout + `Featured Works, Archive, About` nav) AND the center identity block (`Corentin Bernadou — Freelance Developer`) hold `position: fixed` while the page scrolls, so the corner instrument-panel and the giant `FW` wordmark stay locked across every frame. *Reads as:* a stable magazine frame on which the cube performs — the chrome never moves, only the work does. *Transfers to:* any long animated scroll where nav/identity must stay reachable while a centerpiece evolves. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky/fixed once GSAP sets it; no per-frame JS cost of its own). → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Ghost project index, brighten-on-hover** — *Trigger:* hover/focus (12 hover effects, all `opacity 0.25 → 1`). *Mechanism:* the right-edge title list (`Studies in Motion / Ruby Campbell / Shaped by Earth / Echoes in Light / Moving Portraits / Issey Miyake SS25`, repeating) rests at **opacity 0.25** — present but dormant — and the pointed-at item resolves to full opacity over the 300ms ease-out-quad. *Reads as:* a reward loop; the eye goes to the one line it can read, and the list indexes the cube's projects without competing with it. *Transfers to:* archive/portfolio indexes, footer link columns, any "browse by name" list on a dark field. *Safety:* **spectacle-only as drawn** — 0.25 resting contrast fails WCAG and hover-only reveal is invisible on touch + keyboard; ship a legible muted resting token and trigger the brighten on `:focus-within` too. → `ghost-index-hover-list`.
- **Marquee title crawl** — *Trigger:* load → continuous (`nw-marquee` keyframe, `steps`/`ease`). *Mechanism:* the repeating project-title list scrolls vertically as an ambient loop (the duplicated titles in the frames confirm a seamless wrap), in the same spirit as the site's `perspective-curved-ticker` — a crawling list as texture, not a clickable nav. *Reads as:* "the catalogue is alive," ambient editorial motion in the margin. *Transfers to:* decorative credits/now-showing crawls on expressive heroes. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — perpetual motion is a motion-sickness vector and steals focus; pause on hover/focus, gate under reduced-motion, and provide a static readable copy if the list must be functional. → `perspective-curved-ticker`.

**Components** — captured state coverage is **thin** (`craft.states.focus` and `craft.states.disabled` are both empty; no img/elevation/gradient data):
- **Button / CTA:** **not captured** as a distinct component — there is no conventional filled CTA on the hero; the only "actions" are text links (nav + the project index). The accent `#ff4401` lives on the wordmark/tiles, not on a button.
- **Card:** **not captured** as DOM — the project "cards" are thumbnail faces inside the WebGL cube, not styled HTML cards (0 box-shadows, 0 gradients, radius mostly `0` with one stray ~4px node). No hover-lift/elevation system exists to extract.
- **Nav:** top-center 3-row link cluster; **active = brightness** (current item ~`#cacaca`, rest mid-grey), no underline/pill/box. Hover deltas weren't captured for the nav specifically, but the index list confirms the house hover idiom is an **opacity bump** (0.25→1) over 300ms ease-out-quad. No `:focus-visible` ring (`focus_visible: false`) — a keyboard-focus defect.
- **Input / form:** **not captured** — no forms, search, or input fields on the captured page.

**Grid / container** — `craft.grid.columns: ["12"]` (a 12-col desktop grid that collapses to **4 columns at 390px**); the nav does **not** go to a hamburger (`likely_hamburger: false`, 3 links both viewports). In practice (frames) the layout reads as a **corner-anchored frame, not a content grid**: chrome pinned to the four edges, the cube + index occupying the center-right field. So the "12-col" is the underlying scaffold; the visible composition is edge-anchored margins around a free-floating centerpiece (no max-width content column is exercised on the hero).

**A11y (honest, incl. defects)** — this expressive site **fails several gates**: `reduced_motion: false` (the continuous WebGL loop + marquee run regardless of OS setting — the headline defect); `focus_visible: false` (no keyboard focus ring across **184 interactive elements**, **all flagged as small/sub-44px tap targets**); the ghost index rests at opacity 0.25 (sub-WCAG until hovered) and body grey `#767676` on `#070304` at ~11px is already borderline. `img_count: 0` / `img_alt_ratio: null` — the thumbnails live in the canvas, so they carry **no alt text and are invisible to screen readers / crawlers**. Net: beautiful, but it would need a reduced-motion branch, a visible focus ring, larger tap/type, and a real DOM gallery behind the canvas to ship as anything task-bearing.

## Production-safety note

- **Monochrome + single accent on dark** — **production-safe.** Robust, low-maintenance, high-contrast for the accent. Cost: the grey-on-near-black body (`#767676` on `#070304`) is borderline for WCAG AA at ~11px — bump body to ≥14px and/or lighten to ~`#9a9a9a` in production.
- **Editorial corner-metadata chrome** — **production-safe.** Pure text, cheap to add, sets tone instantly. Keep it in logical DOM/reading order and size it accessibly; tiny edge type can fail zoom/contrast and confuse screen readers if positioned out of order.
- **Two-size scale + value-only active state** — **production-safe.** Reduces decisions and stays accessible *if* you add a real `:focus-visible` ring — a brightness change alone is not a keyboard focus indicator, and the size jump shouldn't leave mid-level text homeless (add a rung for product UI).
- **300ms ease-out-quad hover/state transitions** — **production-safe.** This is exactly the right register for interactive feedback; the `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.46,0.45,0.94)` curve and 300ms duration port directly to product UI.
- **Rotating 3D image cube (WebGL/CSS-3D)** — **spectacle-only.** Beautiful hero, but it is continuous animation (battery/CPU/GPU cost), needs many thumbnails (payload), and carries no semantic content. Gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion`, lazy-load, pause off-screen, and never block first paint on it.
- **Perspective-curved scrolling ticker** — **spectacle-only.** The 3D-mapped, shrinking text is decorative and partly illegible by design (far items are sub-pixel) — fine as ambient texture, unusable as real navigation. If the list must be functional, provide a flat, readable copy; respect reduced-motion.
- **Intro wordmark reveal / loader (the "FW" + 49%)** — **spectacle-only.** A staged intro animation adds perceived latency before content; acceptable on a portfolio, costly anywhere users have a task. Keep it skippable and short, and don't hide critical content behind it.
