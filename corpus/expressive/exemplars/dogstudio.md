> **Capture note:** desktop is partly occluded by a functional **cookie wall** (dark card, "Accept / Deny" in coral) docked lower-left over the hero. It is a consent overlay, not a layout element — read past it. The hero itself (3D wolf + nebula + serif display) is the real first screen.

## What works
- **One sans + one serif, split by job, not by hierarchy.** A high-contrast display serif (GT Sectra) owns every *named* thing — the hero line, project titles, the "We're crafting…" statement — while a neutral grotesque (Heebo) carries all body, navigation, eyebrows and the project *index*. The reader learns "serif = the subject, sans = the apparatus," so the page needs almost no weight or color variation to feel ordered. Transfers to any editorial/agency site that wants two registers without a sprawling scale.
- **Spectacle is quarantined to the hero; the rest is near-flat dark.** The expensive moment (animated 3D render over a painterly nebula) lives in screen one. Everything below is a black canvas with type and at most a dimmed photo. Concentrating the GPU/wow budget in one viewport buys "premium" perception cheaply and keeps scroll performance and legibility intact. Transferable rule: budget one spectacle surface, pay flat everywhere else.
- **Depth comes from a fixed world, not from chrome.** Every section is transparent and the whole atmosphere — the nebula wash and the rendered creature — lives on `position:fixed` layers behind them, so scrolling *reveals a still world* instead of pushing opaque panels past each other. The page reads dimensional with zero shadows, borders or the janky `background-attachment: fixed`. Transferable rule: for a layered feel, make sections transparent over one held backdrop rather than stacking elevated cards. (Pattern: `fixed-backdrop-reveal`.)
- **A ghost index that withholds, then rewards.** The "Featured projects" list renders enormous but in a near-invisible dark-on-dark thin weight (~10–15% contrast) — present but dormant — and only brightens + reveals its imagery on hover. Withholding legibility converts a static list into an interaction and makes the reveal feel earned. (Pattern: `ghost-index-hover-list`.)
- **Color is delegated to the content, not the chrome.** The shell is monochrome; *each project* supplies its own saturated identity — a royal-blue case, a crimson case, a teal case — surfaced as a tiny color-coded category eyebrow and as the panel's full-bleed flood on mobile. The palette stays disciplined while the work still feels chromatic and varied. (Pattern: `dimmed-color-case-stack`.)
- **Margin metadata does the indexing.** Date ranges ("2020 – Ongoing", "2012 – Today") and category labels live in tiny tracked caps in a left gutter, freeing the headline column to be pure scale. Separating "when/what" (margin) from "name" (center) lets both be information-dense without collision. Transfers to any list/archive view.
- **A single recurring accent gesture.** One thin coral-red diagonal "laser" line streaks across multiple sections and reappears as the underline on "Discover our values" and the consent buttons. A lone repeating accent in an otherwise neutral field reads as deliberate art direction at near-zero cost. Transferable: pick one accent gesture and reuse it as the through-line.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** Three families captured: display serif **GT Sectra Display**, grotesque **Heebo** (body + giant thin index), and **Gilroy** (geometric sans, incidental). Tracking is **size-bound and inverts across the scale** — the 120px display sets at **−4.8px** (≈ −0.04em, tight), while the tiny tracked caps go the other way: **8px → +4.8px** (≈ +0.6em) and **9px → +3.6px** (≈ +0.4em). So big serif = negative/condensed, micro-eyebrow = heavily tracked-out — the two extremes of the same lever. Body Heebo ~14px / 1.5; the giant ghost index is ~80px at weight 200–300. Range is wide (~8→120px) but the *families* split the work, so hierarchy is mostly **scale + family**, barely weight. `tabular-nums` is enabled (the margin date metadata stays column-aligned).
- **Color:** Near-black canvas (`#0e101a`/`#131419`) with a violet-indigo nebula tint in the hero; fg is white; large dormant type sits at low-alpha lavender (`rgba(160,168,220,.7–.8)`). Accent is coral-red (`#ff4940`) used only for the laser, links and consent CTAs. Per-project accents (gold, teal, pink, royal blue, crimson) belong to content, not the system. Roles: bg=ink, fg=white, muted=lavender-alpha, accent=coral.
- **Spacing rhythm:** Generous; sections separated by tall voids (note ~200–405px block padding in the raw profile). Body uses a small 4–13px micro-scale for chrome, then jumps to huge section gaps — a two-tier rhythm (tight inside components, vast between them).
- **Layout system:** Left-aligned headline column on a ~1440 canvas with a thin left metadata gutter; body sets in 2 narrow muted columns. Footer is a horizontal city/social rail. Mobile reflows the desktop hover-list into a stack of **full-bleed color-flooded case panels** — a genuine treatment change, not a width squeeze.
- **Layering / page architecture:** The page is a stack of **transparent sheets scrolling over two held world-layers**, not a flowing document. `layers_summary.has_static_backdrop: true`: a full-viewport `picture.home-background` sits `position:fixed` at **z-index −3** (100×129% coverage) and never scrolls, while **all four content sections are transparent** (`surfaces.summary.transparent_height_pct: 100`, every block alpha 0) — so the headlines, the ghost index and the footer rail flow *over* a still world. Frames confirm it: the violet nebula wash holds in the upper-left through s00→s03 as the type rises over it, and by s06→s09 the transparent sections have cleared the nebula to sit on near-black. Above the backdrop, the WebGL wolf (`dog-scene-inner`, fixed z:1) is a second held layer the content stacks on top of (index/body at z:2–3). No blend or glass (`blend_glass` empty) and no elevation shadows — depth is built purely from **fixedness + transparency + parallax offset**: the negative-z variant of `fixed-backdrop-reveal` (a rendered nebula here rather than emergent's screen-blend atmosphere). (Pattern: `fixed-backdrop-reveal`.)
- **Motion / easing intent:** Long, slow, expensive easings dominate. Captured durations span `200 / 300 / 400 / 600 / 700 / 750 / 800 / 1200ms` — the cluster sits at the **long end (600–1200ms)**, with `200–300ms` reserved for micro-state. Easings are `cubic-bezier(0.245,0.495,0,0.99)` (the steep cinematic settle), `cubic-bezier(0.165,0.84,0.44,1)` (easeOutQuart), `cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1)` (easeOutCubic), `cubic-bezier(0.25,0.46,0.45,0.94)` (easeOutQuad), plus bare `ease` / `ease-in-out` for trivia. Staggers run `100 / 175 / 200 / 300 / 400 / 500ms` — *long* offsets, not the snappy 60–90ms the corpus favors. Reads as cinematic, weighty, "high-craft": slow scroll/entrance reveals, not snappy UI feedback. Full choreography, evidence, and per-technique safety in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** Low per-screen density bought with darkness and scale; the page feels calm despite carrying a full portfolio because most of it is void or dormant ghost type.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant ordering device is **scale**, reinforced by **family** (serif=subject) and **contrast/value** (dormant→active brightening). Color is almost never used to rank.
- **Restraint:** No card chrome, no shadows of consequence, no borders, no gradients in the UI layer (the gradient is photographic, in the hero art). The whole identity rides on two faces, one accent, and a lot of black.
- **Grid / container:** content sits on a **~1440px max-width canvas** (re-probed) — left-anchored, with a thin left metadata gutter (~5–8% width) for dates/categories. There is still **no declared CSS grid** (`craft.grid.columns` / `layout.gridCols` empty); the content bands resolve to a **2-column flex split** (left serif headline column | right body paragraph beside the pinned scene), with wider 4–6-child flex rows in the footer city/social rail. Treat the container width + the 2-up band as measured; the absence of a fine track grid is intentional (whitespace, not columns).
- **Components (`craft.states`):** **Largely not captured** — `states.focus`, `states.disabled`, and the per-component button/card/nav/input maps are all empty in the raw profile, and `a11y.focus_visible` is **false** (no visible focus ring observed).
  - **Button:** the visible CTAs are the coral consent buttons ("Accept / Deny") and a coral **"Discover our values"** link-button with an underline gesture; fill/hover/active/focus deltas **not captured** (probe recorded 0 hover effects).
  - **Card:** no card component — the site deliberately avoids card chrome (no border/shadow/radius system; only a negligible `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.15)` shadow exists, not real elevation). The closest "card" is the dark cookie-consent panel (radius ~26px pill on buttons, 3px chip elsewhere). **Not a reusable card system.**
  - **Nav:** top bar (wordmark left, "Our Showreel" / project links right) plus a full-screen **`site-menu-panel`** overlay (pinned `picture.site-menu-back` + panel); link hover/active states **not captured** (`navVisibleLinks: 0` at capture — the menu was closed).
  - **Input:** **none on the page** (no forms captured); the only form-like control is the showreel scrubber. **Not captured.**

## Interaction & motion

Dogstudio is **GSAP-driven spectacle quarantined to one pinned stage**. The signature is a **WebGL wolf scene that pins for the whole first act** while headlines and a ghost index scroll past it — the canvas isn't a hero you leave behind, it's a fixed performer the page choreographs *around*. Evidence from the motion capture: **9 `pin (fixed)` effects** (the scene `div.dog-scene-inner`, the `home-background` picture, the `site-menu-panel`/`-back`, the sound toggle, the showreel player, the cookie wall), **3 `transform/parallax`**, **2 IntersectionObservers over 16 targets** (threshold `[0,0.15,1]`, one with `rootMargin:300px` = pre-load before entry), **3 scroll listeners**, **465 `requestAnimationFrame` calls** in one pass (the live canvas loop), **1 `<canvas>`**, **1 disclosure**, library **GSAP (TweenMax)**, **14 `@keyframes`** (11 of them site-specific hashes `u571deb2c`…`udefbcafc`, plus 3 `plyr-*` for the video player). **Critically: `prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`, `a11y.reduced_motion: false`) — the single biggest production liability, same failure mode as teak. **Probe limits to be honest about:** the capture recorded **0 hover effects and 0 cursor-followers** despite this being a hover-reveal site (the `ghost-index` brightens on hover) — the run sampled scroll stops, not pointer events, so hover/cursor motion is **real but un-instrumented here**, not absent. Frames cited are `corpus/expressive/screenshots/dogstudio-co/frames/`.

- **Pinned WebGL scene scrubbed by scroll** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (`div.dog-scene-inner` + `picture.home-background` both `pin (fixed)`; the `<canvas>` + 465 rAF/scroll). *Mechanism:* the 3D wolf canvas holds `position: fixed` across the whole first act while its render (camera angle / lighting / morph) advances with scroll position — the frames confirm it: s00 the wolf faces forward in cool blue → s03 it has turned and the nebula cooled → s09 it's reframed closer in pink/magenta light, the *same* pinned object lit and posed differently. *Reads as:* a single cinematic creature the page orbits — "this studio renders." *Transfers to:* any agency/brand hero where one rendered hero-object should persist and evolve through a section instead of scrolling off. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — fixed canvas + continuous rAF is the page's whole GPU/main-thread budget; LCP/SEO hit if the headline is canvas-bound; no reduced-motion fallback. Ship a static poster as LCP, lazy-init the canvas, pause off-screen, gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion`. *(No corpus recipe — pinned-canvas-scroll-scrub isn't a pattern file → `patterns_to_build`.)*
- **Headline swap over the pinned stage** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress / in-view (the pinned scene stays; the foreground text layer changes). *Mechanism:* as you scroll the held wolf, the left serif column cycles through statements — s00 "We Make Good Shit" → s09 "We … experiment … result." — each a fresh GT Sectra display block entering over the same fixed background, not a new section pushing the old one out. *Reads as:* scrollytelling — one stage, evolving caption; the claim changes while the evidence (the creature) persists. *Transfers to:* feature deep-dives or manifesto sequences that want one persistent visual under changing copy. *Safety:* **production-safe** as a mechanism (text swap over a pinned layer is cheap); the cost lives in the canvas behind it, not here. → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Ghost index reveal (dormant-until-active)** — *Trigger:* scroll into the "Featured projects" act, then hover/focus per row. *Mechanism:* the project list ("Tomorrowland", "MSI Chicago", "The Kennedy Center", "Royal Opera Of Wallonia") renders enormous but at ~10–15% lavender-alpha contrast — present but dormant in s03 — and brightens + surfaces its imagery on hover. *Reads as:* a list that withholds legibility then rewards the pointer; the reveal feels earned. *Transfers to:* portfolio/archive indexes that want the list itself to be the interaction. *Safety:* **spectacle-only as drawn** — resting contrast fails WCAG, hover-only reveal is invisible on touch/keyboard. Safe version keeps the giant scale + reveal delight but sets a legible resting token and reveals on focus too. → `ghost-index-hover-list`. *(Probe caught the rows' dormant state in-frame but logged 0 hover events — the brighten is hover-gated and un-instrumented; grounded in frames + the pattern, not a captured hover delta.)*
- **In-view fade-rise of project rows** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO; the `li "2017 The Kennedy Center"` and `li "2016 - Ongoing Royal Opera Of Wallonia"` each carry an `opacity 0→1` **fade-in reveal** + a paired `transform/parallax`). *Mechanism:* each archive row fades from 0 and rises/drifts a few px as it enters, fired once per row; the `rootMargin:300px` observer pre-arms it just before the row reaches the viewport. *Reads as:* the long tail of the portfolio settling in row by row under the pinned hero. *Transfers to:* any long list/feed that should enter with rhythm rather than pop in. *Safety:* **production-safe** (GPU-cheap opacity+transform, once-only) — *except* it currently has no reduced-motion gate; add one and ship the resting (visible) state in the DOM. → `scroll-reveal-in-view` (+ the drift is `parallax-drift`).
- **Layered parallax drift (debris + headline)** — *Trigger:* in-view / scroll-progress (3 `transform/parallax`: the top nav/header cluster, plus the two project rows). *Mechanism:* foreground leaf/particle debris around the wolf and the headline/row layer translate at rates offset from the fixed canvas, so the scene separates into depth planes (visible as the floating debris repositioning between s00→s03→s09). *Reads as:* dimensional, "the creature lives in space," not pasted on. *Transfers to:* any hero where floating elements should feel like depth around a focal object. *Safety:* **production-safe if subtle** — only 3 parallax layers here (far lighter than teak's 14), so the drift itself is cheap; the spectacle cost is the canvas it layers over, not the parallax. → `parallax-drift`.
- **Cross-fade / opacity decay on lazy media** — *Trigger:* in-view (`img.js-lazy` logged `opacity 1→0`). *Mechanism:* lazy-loaded imagery fades *out* (or cross-fades between states) as the scene transitions — a decay, not a reveal, so swapped media dissolves rather than cuts. *Reads as:* smooth, filmic transitions between the held visuals. *Transfers to:* media swaps / lazy galleries that should dissolve. *Safety:* **production-safe** (opacity-only) — keep a non-zero resting state under reduced-motion so content never stays at `opacity:0`. *(Closest corpus reference is `opacity-decay-index` for the receding-list feel, but this specific lazy-media cross-fade has no exact recipe → `patterns_to_build`.)*

## Production-safety note
- **A11y defects (measured, be honest):** `prefers-reduced-motion` is **NOT respected** (`prmRules:0`) — every motion technique runs regardless of user preference; this is the top liability. `focus_visible` is **false** (no focus ring → keyboard users are lost on a hover-driven index). **34 small tap targets** out of 70 interactive elements (~49% under-sized for touch). `img_alt_ratio 0.85` (≈ 2 of 13 images lack alt — decent, not complete). Before shipping anything from here: add `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` fallbacks, a visible `:focus-visible` ring, and enlarge tap targets to ≥44px.
- **3D/WebGL hero render (wolf) over painterly nebula** — *spectacle-only.* Cost: large asset payload, continuous GPU/animation, jank risk on low-end devices, and an LCP/SEO hit if the headline is canvas-bound. Safe adaptation: ship a static poster image as the LCP element and lazy-init the canvas after load; respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Ghost dormant-until-hover index** — *spectacle-only as drawn.* Resting contrast (~10–15%) fails WCAG and hides navigable links from low-vision/touch users; hover-only reveal is invisible on touch. Safe version: keep the giant scale and the reveal-on-hover *delight*, but set the resting state to a legible muted token and surface imagery on focus too (works for keyboard). (See pattern.)
- **Two-register serif/sans typography** — *production-safe.* Pure value lever; recolors the whole tone for free. Self-host the faces and set `font-display: swap`.
- **Margin date/category metadata** — *production-safe.* Plain tracked text; collapses independently on mobile.
- **Color-keyed case eyebrow + dimmed-image panels** — *production-safe* if you keep text on a darkened scrim (maintain contrast over the photo) rather than over raw imagery.
- **Single recurring coral laser line** — *production-safe* (a positioned element / pseudo-element); decorative, mark `aria-hidden`.
- **Long 1.2s cinematic easings** — *production-safe for content reveals, spectacle-only for chrome.* Sub-second feedback should stay snappy; reserve the slow curves for scroll/entrance moments and gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Pinned canvas scrubbed by scroll (the wolf stage)** — *spectacle-only.* Fixed `<canvas>` + ~465 rAF/scroll is the page's entire perf budget; combine the LCP-poster + lazy-init + off-screen-pause + reduced-motion gate from the hero note above. No corpus recipe → see `patterns_to_build: pinned-canvas-scroll-scrub`.
- **Headline-swap over a pinned stage / in-view fade-rise of rows** — *production-safe mechanisms* (text/opacity/transform over a fixed layer, fired once); the only fix needed is the missing reduced-motion gate and shipping the resting (visible) state in the DOM.
- **Layered parallax drift** — *production-safe here* — only 3 parallax layers (vs teak's 14), so the drift is cheap; the spectacle cost is the canvas underneath, not the parallax. Cap translate range and gate under reduced-motion.
