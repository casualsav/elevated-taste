A dark **cookie-consent modal** ("Nous utilisons des cookies !" — Accepter / Refuser / Personnaliser) is overlaid bottom-right in the desktop shot; ignore it as chrome, not design. ## What works
- **Spectacle lives entirely in type and motion, never in color.** The whole site is monochrome — warm light-gray canvas, near-black ink, dark chrome — with a lime accent used *three times*. Removing color as a variable forces hierarchy onto scale and rhythm, which is what makes a 20-year agency read as confident rather than loud. Transfers to any brand that wants "premium" without a palette: pick one ink, one canvas, and spend your budget on size and pacing.
- **One typeface (Moderat), two weights, a 6–9× scale jump.** Body sits at barely-there 400/18px; display explodes to ~160–180px. There is no mid-weight bolding to create emphasis — the *size delta alone* builds the entire hierarchy, so the page stays calm at rest and only the headlines shout. Reusable rule: if your type has a wide optical range, get hierarchy from a big scale ratio in one weight before you reach for a second weight.
- **Chrome is dark, content is light — the inversion makes controls legible without boxes.** The hero nav, the CTA, and the footer are near-black objects floating on the light page; everything navigational is dark, everything editorial is light. The user learns the rule once and never hunts for affordances. Transfers to any light editorial layout that needs persistent controls to stay findable.
- **Display headlines are set at brutal negative tracking (~ −0.04em).** At 160px+, default tracking looks gappy and amateur; pulling letters tight (−6.7px here) packs the word into a single dense graphic mass, which is what makes oversized type feel designed rather than just big. The rule scales inversely: the larger the type, the more negative tracking it wants.
- **Reveals are slow and uniform — one signature easing across the page.** Nearly everything animates in over 0.48–1.2s on a single custom `cubic-bezier(0.36,0.33,0,1)` (fast-out, long glide to rest). A consistent, slightly-too-slow ease is itself a brand signal; it reads as deliberate. Reusable: define one "house" duration+easing pair and apply it everywhere instead of per-component guesses.
- **Self-evidence as proof.** Awards are shown as inline running counts (Awwwards ×28 · CSSDA ×19 · FWA ×1) and client logos carry one-line "what we did" captions — credibility stated as data, not as a badge wall. Transfers to any agency/portfolio recognitions section.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** single family (Moderat) on a wide scale — ~0.625rem labels → 1.125rem body → ~5rem secondary display → ~10–11rem hero display (≈6–9× range). Hierarchy is created by **scale, not weight**: body is 400, a heavy 900 is reserved for a couple of marks, 500 is rare. Display runs very tight (≈ −0.04em); body runs slightly tight (−0.625px). Leading collapses toward 1.0 on display, ~1.5 on body.
- **Color:** effectively monochrome. Canvas warm light gray (~#eaeaea), ink near-black (#181717), chrome surfaces dark (segmented nav ~#2a2828, footer #0b0b0b), muted gray #939393 for recessed/inactive text. A single lime (#dcf48e) appears as a rare micro-accent only. Roles: bg=canvas, surface=white cards, fg=ink, chrome=dark control objects, accent=sparing lime.
- **Spacing rhythm:** ~8px base, but the headline story is the *macro* rhythm — section padding runs to ~166px (≈10rem) so huge type gets huge air. Density is deliberately low up top and tightens only in the logos/recognitions band. Whitespace, not rules or boxes, does the separating.
- **Layout system:** ~1440 canvas with ~60px inline gutters. Hero is asymmetric — a two-line headline offset diagonally ("Hello," upper-left, "again.\*" lower-right). Content alternates left-label / right-big-list (expertises) and a 2-up portfolio grid. Footer is a 3–4 column dark block with two agency addresses (Lille / Annecy), newsletter, and FR/EN toggle.
- **Motion / easing intent:** durations cluster at **240 / 350 / 420 / 480 / 550 / 600 / 840 / 1200ms** — micro-states near 240–350ms, content reveals 420–600ms, hero/brand glides stretching to 0.84–1.2s. The signature curve is `cubic-bezier(0.36,0.33,0,1)` (fast-out, long glide to rest); secondary curves `cubic-bezier(0.165,0.84,0.44,1)` (easeOutQuart), `cubic-bezier(0.77,0,0.175,1)` (easeInOutCirc), `cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1)` / `(0.19,1,0.22,1)` (deep easeOut settles), with bare `ease` only on chrome. A consistent slightly-too-slow ease is itself the brand signal. Full choreography (hero-glyph fade, parallax drift, the masthead marquee, the panel-reveal footer) is detailed in **Interaction & motion** below. Motion communicates craft and pace, not information.
- **Density:** low information density by design — one idea per viewport at the top, density rising only in the trust/recognitions band. Legibility holds because contrast is high (near-black on light) and line lengths stay short.
- **Hierarchy:** dominant ordering device is **scale**, with **dark-vs-light** as the secondary device (controls vs content).
- **Restraint:** no gradients, no drop-shadows for elevation (one 1px hairline exists), no second typeface, no real color. The lime is held back to a whisper. Spectacle is quarantined to two isolated motion moments.
- **Grid / container:** ~1440 canvas, **1 / 2 / 3 columns** (real, from `grid.columns` — not frame-estimated) collapsing to a single column on mobile (390vw); ~60px inline gutters (frame-estimated). The portfolio band is the 2-up; the footer is the 3-up. Whitespace, not rules, separates.
- **Tracking by size (varies, inverts at the bottom):** tightens as type grows — body 25px and most mid-display sit at a flat **−0.625px**, true display 53–61px pulls to **≈ −2.1 to −2.4px (≈ −0.04em)**. The exception: the smallest 10px labels run **positive at +0.2px** to keep all-caps eyebrows open. So it's not a single global value — gate the brutal negative tracking by size and let small caps breathe.
- **Components** (resting → state delta, from `craft.states`; honest gaps noted):
  - **Nav links** (`Contactez-nous`, `On recrute`, phone): focus shows a **1px solid `rgb(16,16,16)` outline** — real `focus-visible`. *Hover deltas were not captured* (`hover: []`) — treat hover styling as unverified, not absent.
  - **FR/EN toggle**: focus shows a **3px `rgb(255,255,255)` outline** (white ring, tuned for the dark chrome it sits on) — a deliberate per-context focus treatment.
  - **Segmented pill switcher** (the dark bottom bar): active label white, inactive muted `#939393`; lives as a real control (see `segmented-pill-switcher`). Active/inactive is carried by opacity/color, not a moving indicator in the frames.
  - **Newsletter input** (footer): a bare **hairline-underline field** ("Votre email") — no box, just a 1px bottom border on the dark panel; relies on the label + underline for affordance.
  - **Disabled states**: none captured (`disabled: []`) — the corpus has no evidence of a disabled treatment here.

## Interaction & motion

Wokine is **genuinely motion-led but lighter on spectacle than its agency peers** — the heavy lifting is a pervasive in-view fade plus a few signature gestures, not a WebGL scene. Evidence from the capture: **47 IntersectionObserver targets across 4 observers**, **19 in-view fade-in reveals** (the one effect with a real measured delta), **1643 `requestAnimationFrame` calls** during one scroll pass (a custom smooth-scroll/animation loop — **no GSAP/Lenis/Locomotive global detected**, `libraries: []`), **7 scroll + 10 mousemove listeners**, and just **1 real site `@keyframes` (`marquee-…`)** (the other three keyframes are the Plyr video player — chrome). `<canvas>` ×1, `<video>` ×3, marquee ×6. **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`, `respectsReducedMotion: false`) — a real defect for a site this animation-dense (see safety). Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` (marquee) · hand-rolled rAF scroll loop.

- **In-view fade-rise reveal** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO, 47 targets). *Mechanism:* one class flip on intersect → CSS transitions `opacity` `0 → 1` (some elements start at 0.05) on the house `cubic-bezier(0.36,0.33,0,1)` over ~480–600ms, fired once. **Strongest-evidenced effect** (19 measured deltas; corroborated s00→s03 as expertise lines and section blocks settle in). *Reads as:* the page "settling" section by section at a deliberate, slightly-slow pace. *Transfers to:* any long editorial/agency scroll. *Safety:* **production-safe** (GPU-cheap opacity, once-only) — **but here it lacks a reduced-motion off-switch**, so add one. → `scroll-reveal-in-view`.
- **Hero-glyph clear-on-scroll** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress / in-view. *Mechanism:* the lime inflated-3D "W" blob and the diagonal "Hello, again." headline carry `transform` + `opacity` (probe tags `div.brand-shield`, `div.brand-wokine` as parallax/transform targets); between **s00 (blob + headline present) and s01 (both fully cleared)** the hero empties as you leave the top. *Reads as:* a confident one-beat hero that dissolves to hand off to the work — the spectacle object doesn't linger. *Transfers to:* any oversized brand hero you want to exit cleanly rather than scroll away as dead weight. *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform/opacity only); gate under reduced-motion (currently not). → `parallax-drift` (drift) + `scroll-reveal-in-view` (fade).
- **Layer parallax drift** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (in-view). *Mechanism:* `transform` bound to scroll on ~8 named layers (`brand-shield`, `brand-wokine`, the "Play Showreel" button + label, the expertise word-stack, `span.icon`) so foreground marks and chrome track at a different rate than the page. *Reads as:* depth and "liveness" without 3D — the floating dark controls feel decoupled from the content plane. *Transfers to:* logo marks, floating CTAs, section labels on long scrolls. *Safety:* **production-safe when subtle → spectacle if heavy**; transform-only is compositor-cheap, but un-gated parallax is a motion-sickness vector. *Note: the probe flagged these elements as transform/parallax but did not measure a from→to delta, and the frames don't isolate the drift — presence is well-evidenced, magnitude is not.* → `parallax-drift`.
- **Horizontal masthead marquee** — *Trigger:* load (continuous). *Mechanism:* the only real site keyframe (`marquee-…`) translates the portfolio title "Nos réalisations" with a ✦ glyph separator sideways; **directly visible at s06** mid-slide ("ons ✦ Nos réalisati…"). *Reads as:* a kinetic section masthead that announces the work band. *Transfers to:* section dividers, logo/announcement tickers. *Safety:* **production-safe** as a ticker (cheap transform loop) **but** it's a continuous animation = ongoing GPU/battery cost, must pause on hover/focus and off-screen, and **must honor reduced-motion** (it doesn't here). → `announcement-ticker-marquee` (and the sibling ornament `ghost-watermark-marquee`).
- **Panel-reveal footer** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (2 `pin (fixed)` probes). *Mechanism:* the dark full-bleed case panels (s09) scroll up with rounded top corners to **uncover the pinned near-black footer behind them** (s10: manifesto + 3-col links + addresses + Play Showreel disc). *Reads as:* a deliberate closing "curtain" — the page resolves into its footer rather than just ending. *Transfers to:* closing CTAs / footers on long scrolls. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky/position; transform on the panel) — keep the footer reachable in DOM order and gate any motion under reduced-motion. → `scroll-reveal-panel`.
- **Subtle element rotation (~2°)** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (probe: `rotate 2.1°` ×17). *Mechanism:* a small rotation applied to a set of elements (consistent with a gentle tilt on the inflated hero glyph / thumbnails). *Reads as:* (intended) a soft organic wobble that keeps marks from feeling rigid. *Transfers to:* playful brand marks, sticker-like thumbnails. *Safety:* **production-safe** (tiny compositor transform, capped ~2°). *Note: probe-detected with a ~2° magnitude label but `from == to` (no measured delta) and **not corroborated in the frames** — cited as present-but-unconfirmed; do not treat as load-bearing.* — no standalone snippet; reuse the principle via `parallax-drift` with a rotate channel.

## Production-safety note
- **Monochrome system + scale-driven hierarchy** — **production-safe**, in fact a11y-friendly: high contrast, no color-coding to fail color-blind users. Cost: requires a typeface with real optical range; weak fonts look empty at this strategy.
- **Dark segmented pill switcher with embedded icon CTA** (`segmented-pill-switcher`) — **production-safe**. Cost: must be a real `<nav>`/tab control with focus-visible and keyboard order; the active/inactive contrast (white vs muted) must clear contrast minimums (the muted gray here is borderline — darken it in production).
- **Floating dark chrome on a light page** — **production-safe**. Cost: if it becomes `position: fixed`, reserve safe-area insets on mobile and ensure it never covers content under the keyboard.
- **Brutal negative tracking on display type** — **production-safe** only at large sizes; **never** apply below ~24px (it destroys legibility). Gate by size, not globally.
- **Multi-row ghost-text watermark marquee** (`ghost-watermark-marquee`) — **spectacle-only**. Cost: continuous transform animation = ongoing GPU/battery cost; the text is decorative so it must be `aria-hidden`; honor `prefers-reduced-motion` and pause off-screen. Pure ornament — never put navigation or real content in it.
- **Long 0.48–1.2s entrance reveals** — **spectacle-leaning but acceptable**. Cost: slow reveals delay perceived content and frustrate repeat visitors; cap functional UI nearer 200–320ms and reserve the long glides for hero/brand moments, always with a reduced-motion off-switch.
- **Hero-glyph clear-on-scroll + layer parallax drift** — **production-safe** (transform/opacity, compositor-cheap), and the cleanest way to make an oversized brand hero exit rather than overstay. Cost: parallax is a motion-sickness vector if heavy or un-gated — keep drift small and **gate under reduced-motion**.
- **Panel-reveal footer** — **production-safe** (native sticky + transform). Cost: ensure the footer stays correct in DOM/tab order under the panel and that no content is trapped under the moving panel on short viewports.
- **A11y read (honest, incl. one real defect):**
  - **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** (`prmRules: 0`) — the headline defect. A page with a continuous marquee, parallax on ~8 layers, a panel-reveal, and 19 slow fades **must** ship a reduced-motion branch that drops to resting state; this one doesn't. Fix before reuse.
  - **Focus-visible: present and thoughtful** — real outlines (1px dark on light chrome, a 3px white ring on the dark FR/EN toggle). Good.
  - **Image alt coverage: 1.0** (16/16 images carry alt) — excellent.
  - **Tap targets:** interactive elements are numerous (82) and the capture's small-target threshold is 44px — verify the inline text "tabs" in the segmented pill and the tiny FR/EN toggle meet 44×44 on touch (borderline by eye).
  - **Contrast:** the muted `#939393` inactive label (segmented switcher, recessed text) is **borderline** on both the light canvas and the dark chrome — darken in production.

## Extracted patterns
- segmented-pill-switcher — a dark rounded-full bar holding inline text "tabs" (active white / inactive muted) with a nested darker CTA pill + icon at one end; a compact nav/switcher that stays legible on light canvases without borders or shadows.
- ghost-watermark-marquee — oversized brand word repeated across several offset rows at ~6% ink opacity, drifting horizontally with a glyph separator; an ambient outro/section-break texture built from type alone.
- scroll-reveal-in-view — IntersectionObserver-gated opacity fade (here on the slow house ease) firing once per element; the page's primary reveal rhythm. (motion)
- parallax-drift — `transform` bound to scroll on foreground marks/chrome (logo, floating CTA, section labels) so layers track at different rates; reuse for depth without 3D. (motion)
- announcement-ticker-marquee — continuous horizontal translate of a section title with a glyph separator; reuse for kinetic mastheads / tickers (pause on hover + reduced-motion). (motion)
- scroll-reveal-panel — a panel scrolls up to uncover a pinned page/footer behind it; reuse for closing CTAs/footers that "resolve" rather than end. (motion)
