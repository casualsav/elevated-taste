## What works
- **The interface IS the hero — not a headline about it.** Instead of an oversized claim + product screenshot, the fold is a single short question ("What do you want to create?") sitting directly over a *working* prompt composer (textarea + model picker + mic). The largest, most central interactive object on the page is the actual entry point to the product. For any tool/AI/search product, promoting the real input above the fold converts intent into a first action immediately, and sidesteps the generic "big hero + vague tagline + dashboard mockup" template entirely. Transfers to any product where the first thing a user does is *type something*.
- **Zero accent hue — hierarchy is carried entirely by ink-weight.** The whole chrome is grayscale: near-black ink (`oklch .205`), mid-gray secondary (`.51`), and hairline borders (`.937`). There is no brand color; the only "accent" is the single black-filled **Sign Up / Run** button. Importance is signaled by *darkness of text* and *presence of a border*, never by color. A dense developer surface stays calm because nothing competes chromatically — and it lets the colorful content (template thumbnails) pop. Transfers to any tool UI that wants to read as precise and engineered rather than marketed.
- **Hairline borders, not shadows, are the structural system.** The raw profile counts ~2,100 border instances against a *single* box-shadow. Cards, the composer, chips, and the nav are all defined by 1px `#e6e6e6` borders + radius on a flat near-white ground. This is what gives the "Vercel/Geist" look its crisp, flat, technical feel. Reusable rule: for an engineered aesthetic, separate with border + radius and reserve elevation for true overlays (menus, the composer's lone soft shadow).
- **Suggestion chips kill the blank-page problem.** A row of example pills under the composer — *Contact Form · Image Editor · Mini Game · Finance Calculator* — plus a shuffle button to reroll them. One tap gives a hesitant user a concrete starting point and demonstrates the product's range without a feature list. Transfers to any generative or search input, and to empty states generally.
- **A compact, app-grade type scale — because it IS the app.** Body sits at 13–16px (14px is by far the most frequent size), with a single modest 32px display line; everything is one family (Geist Sans). Marketing pages go 5–10× display-to-body; this stays product-sized and differentiates tiers by *ink color and tracking*, not giant size. Negative tracking is applied only to the display/large sizes (≈ −0.04em at 32px). Transfers to product-led pages where the page and the app should feel like one continuous surface.
- **Color is quarantined into content tiles.** All the visual energy — dark, animated micro-demo thumbnails — lives *inside* bordered template cards; the surrounding chrome stays monochrome and quiet. Same governance idea as a brand keeping hue in props: keep the layout layer neutral, let the user/content layer carry richness. Transfers to galleries, marketplaces, and any grid of vivid media on a neutral shell.
- **The section header doubles as a toolbar.** "Start with a template" shares one baseline with a horizontally-scrollable row of category filter pills (*Apps and Games · Landing Pages · Components · Dashboards · Browse all →*). Title and its filtering affordance occupy a single row, saving vertical space and reading as a control bar rather than decoration. Transfers to any filtered grid or directory.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** One family — **Geist Sans** — across the whole page. Scale is compact and product-grade: display 48/32/24/20px over a UI/body tier of 16/14/13px (14px dominant). Hierarchy comes from *ink color* (black `.205` vs gray `.51`) and *negative tracking on the large sizes* (−2.88px@48, −1.28px@32, −0.96px@24, −0.4px@20 ≈ −0.06→−0.02em), not from weight escalation (weights stay 400/500, 600 is rare). Numbers use `tabular-nums`; ligatures disabled (`"liga" 0`) for a technical register. Leading ≈ 1.25 on display, 1.5 on body.
- **Color:** A pure neutral ramp, no chroma. Ground `#fafafa` (`oklch .985`); surfaces lift to `#fff`; ink `#1f1f1f` (`.205`); secondary text `#757575` (`.51`); tertiary `#8a8a8a` (`.59`). Borders are a single hairline `#e6e6e6` (`.937`). Hover fill is `#f5f5f5` (`.961`). The inverted black button (`#1f1f1f` bg, white text) is the lone high-contrast moment. Full dark theme supported (ground `#000`, fg `#f0f0f0`).
- **Spacing rhythm:** 4px base; the workhorses are tight — 6/8/12px gaps dominate, padding mostly 12px inline / 16–32px block, section gaps 64–96px. Density is high but legible because borders and whitespace do the dividing; small `6px` gaps cluster related controls (chips, stat rows).
- **Layout system:** Centered single-column hero (composer max-width ≈ 690px) opening into a wider content container (≈ 1264px). The template gallery is a 3-up card grid (desktop) → 2-up → 1-up (mobile). Nav is a three-zone bar: left wordmark, centered links (8 visible), right auth. Cards are border-defined tiles with a media thumbnail + an avatar/title/stat footer.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Base interaction is 150ms `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` (standard material ease) for hover/fill, 300ms for larger transitions, plus a custom `cubic-bezier(0.31,0.1,0.08,0.96)` for snappier UI and one springy `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` for playful pops. Motion communicates "responsive tool," not "spectacle" — micro-feedback on controls and self-running content demos, nothing that hijacks scroll. **Reduced motion is respected** (4 PRM rules).
- **Density:** Marketing-low at the hero (lots of air around one composer), product-high in the gallery (dense cards, small meta type). The contrast itself is the message: calm to start, lots-to-explore below.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **ink-weight + border presence**, backed by **position** (centered composer as focal point) and **scale** only on the one display line.
- **Restraint:** No brand accent color, no gradient mesh behind text, no oversized fake dashboard, no giant marketing headline. Exactly one box-shadow on the whole page. Spectacle is delegated to the small autoplaying card demos and never to the chrome.

## Interaction & motion
Be honest about the evidence: the captured scroll frames are **identical top-of-page stills** (the harness didn't scroll an inner container), and `motion.scroll_effects` lists an unconfirmed `pin`/`parallax` pair the frames don't corroborate — **dropped**. The page *chrome* is effectively static at rest. The real, production-grade motion lives in the content tiles and standard micro-interactions, and is backed by the probe counts: 15 IntersectionObservers (threshold 0.15, `rootMargin: 200px`), 11 `<video>` elements, 83 `@keyframes` whose names are almost all module-scoped to embedded demos (`coding-v1…codingScroll`, `diagnostics-v1…scan-line`, `typing-text…typing`, `logos-section…marquee`, `shimmer`, `accordion-down/up`). Reduced motion is respected.

- **In-view-gated media autoplay** — *Trigger:* in-view (IntersectionObserver, `rootMargin:200px`, threshold 0.15). *Mechanism:* each template card embeds a looping micro-demo (video/CSS animation) that only plays while the card is near/within the viewport and pauses offscreen; the 200px margin pre-warms it just before entry. *Reads as:* a gallery that feels alive without a wall of simultaneously-playing clips burning CPU. *Transfers to:* any grid of video/animated thumbnails (galleries, template marketplaces, case-study cards). *Safety:* **production-safe** — it *reduces* main-thread cost vs. autoplay-all, and pauses honor reduced-motion (show a static poster). See `patterns/in-view-autoplay-media-card.md`.
- **Hover fill on quiet controls** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* ghost nav links and chips transition background from transparent → `#f5f5f5` and text gray `.51` → ink `.205` over 150ms `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`; the black CTA lightens its fill on hover instead of changing shape. *Reads as:* crisp, low-key affordance feedback. *Transfers to:* any minimal nav / chip row. *Safety:* **production-safe** (color/opacity only, paired with visible `focus-visible` rings).
- **Accordion disclosure** — *Trigger:* tap/click. *Mechanism:* Radix-style `accordion-down/up` keyframes animate content height for the menu/FAQ disclosures (4 disclosure widgets detected). *Reads as:* standard expand/collapse. *Transfers to:* FAQs, nested nav. *Safety:* **production-safe**; height animation is cheap at these small sizes.
- **Shimmer / skeleton loaders** — *Trigger:* load/pending. *Mechanism:* `shimmer`/`shimmer-text`/`span-skeleton` keyframes sweep a gradient across placeholder blocks while generated content streams in. *Reads as:* "working," not "broken." *Transfers to:* any AI/streaming UI's loading state. *Safety:* **production-safe**; gate the sweep on reduced-motion.
- **Logos marquee** — *Trigger:* load (continuous). *Mechanism:* a 24s linear `marquee` translate loop scrolls a customer-logo strip. *Reads as:* social proof in motion. *Transfers to:* trust strips. *Safety:* **production-safe but pause on hover and on reduced-motion** (a 24s infinite loop is the one thing here to gate carefully).

## Production-safety note
This is a clean reference: `focus-visible` is present, image `alt` ratio is 1.0, and `prefers-reduced-motion` is honored across 4 rule blocks. The main caution is the autoplaying card media (11 videos) and the infinite logo marquee — both must keep their reduced-motion fallbacks (static poster / halted loop) when ported. The flagged small tap targets (96) come from the dense chip/stat rows; bump these to ≥44px on touch.

## Extracted patterns
- input-as-hero-composer — a centered short prompt over a real, bordered composer input (model picker + mic) with a suggestion-chip reroll row; reuse as the hero for any AI/tool/search product instead of a headline + mockup.
