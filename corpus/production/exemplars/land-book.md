## What works (transferable principles)

- **Warm-neutral canvas + one acid accent = calm page, loud CTA.** The whole site lives on a warm cream (`#fbf7eb`) / paper-white base with a warm near-black (`#322f25`) for type; the *only* saturated color is an electric acid-yellow (`#dbf400`) reserved for primary buttons and the brand's "trail" motif. Because everything else is achromatic-warm, the eye snaps to the one yellow shape on each screen — color does wayfinding, not decoration. Transfers to any product site that wants a single unmissable action without a rainbow of UI states. Cost: acid-yellow on white fails text contrast, so it's only ever a *fill behind dark text*, never colored text.
- **A light-weight serif display does the hierarchy; weight stays flat.** Every headline is ABC Arizona (a mixed serif) at **light/300**, 40–56px, tracked tight (`-0.02em`); body and all UI are a neutral grotesque (FT Regola) at 400. Hierarchy comes from the **typeface switch + a 3.5× scale jump**, not from bolding — 1025 of ~1070 nodes are weight 400. Reads as "editorial, considered, expensive." Transfers to any B2B page that wants gravitas without shouting; the risk is that a *light* serif at display size needs a quality face and tight optical sizing or it looks frail.
- **A brand metaphor carried as real art direction, not a stock illustration.** The product is "QA for CX teams"; the site dresses that as an *expedition* — a full-bleed mountain-ridge photo, a hand-drawn acid-yellow summit trail, and a closing line "We know the terrain. You know the destination." The metaphor recurs in copy, the route line, and a halftone-dot mountain in the footer. One consistent concept makes a dry enterprise category feel premium and memorable. Transfers to any category that's hard to dramatize: pick one physical metaphor and thread it through photo + copy + a single graphic device.
- **Emotion photo + real product surface, fused in one hero.** The hero pairs an evocative landscape (feeling) with a *real, dense dashboard panel* overlapping the lower-right (proof), unified by one serif headline. You get brand mood and "this is a real tool" in a single viewport, so the marketing copy can stay to two lines. Transfers to any product whose value is legible on screen. → `photo-overlap-product-hero`.
- **Generous vertical rhythm makes density read as calm.** Sections breathe on 80–200px block padding; inside, the dashboard is information-dense (stat row + scored-conversation table). The contrast — tight data inside, huge air around — makes the product look capable while the page feels unhurried. Transfers to any data-heavy product marketing: spend whitespace at the section seams, not inside the proof.
- **Pills everywhere as the shape language.** Buttons, chips, score badges, the "Case study" link, the cookie controls — almost every interactive object is a full-radius pill (`border-radius: 100px+`). One repeated shape gives an unbranded UI a coherent, friendly signature at zero cost. Transfers to any design system wanting instant cohesion: pick one radius regime and apply it ruthlessly.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Dual-family. Display = **ABC Arizona Mix Light (300)**, serif, 40/48/56px, leading ≈1.0, tracking ≈ -0.02em (`-1.12px` at 56px) — tight, editorial. Body/UI = **FT Regola Neue Regular (400)**, grotesque sans, 16/18 body and 12/14 for labels & the dashboard, leading ≈1.2–1.3. Scale is restrained (~5 steps); hierarchy = **family switch + ~3.5× scale jump**, never weight (300 serif vs 400 sans is the only weight contrast). The `sans-serif`/`700` rows in the raw profile are Framer placeholder noise.
- **Color:** Warm-achromatic base — `bg #fbf7eb`/`#ffffff`, warm surfaces `#ebe5d5`/`#e1e0dd`, `fg #322f25` (warm near-black), muted `#878683`. One chromatic accent: acid-yellow `#dbf400` (CTA fill) with a pale `#f4ff95` echo. Warm-tint section backgrounds: peach `#fdecd8`/`#ffcda9`. Dark UI pills + dark sections use `#2d2c29`; nature sections are dark-green *photography*, not a flat token. Roles are crisp: surface ≠ tint ≠ fg ≠ accent. **Honest note:** the raw profile's high-count `rgb(0,0,238)` is the *default UA link blue* on un-restyled anchors — it does **not** appear in the screenshots; treated as representation noise, not a palette color.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 4/8 base. Intra-component steps 4/8/10/12/16/24; section rhythm is large — 80/120/200px block padding. Whitespace, not rules, separates sections; the only dividers are hairlines and color-block seams.
- **Layout system:** ~1280px centered container (80px inline padding at 1440). Hero is **asymmetric** — serif headline + subcopy on the left ~45%, a product panel overflowing the right edge. Feature showcase is a full-width carousel with a "1/4" counter, thin progress track, and prev/next arrows. Logo wall is a marquee row. Mobile collapses to a single stacked column.
- **Layering / page architecture:** Mostly a **single flat scroll** on the warm canvas — `has_static_backdrop: false`, `has_blend_overlay: false`, and a shallow z-stack (58 nodes at z1, 24 at z0, one lone z100). Depth is built in only two places. (1) A **sticky full-viewport dark stage** — `section#key-benefits-sticky` pins a black frame at `top:0` while its ~2250px parent scrolls, so successive benefits play through one held viewport. (2) A **glass layer scoped to the "Reviewing scores" demo** — 32 `backdrop-filter` elements, all inside that one card: the visible ones are frosted pills (`blur(5px)`, radius 80px, a 0.5px white hairline border, near-transparent warm-tint gradient fill) floating over a *blurred* mountain photo, backed by ~20 fainter `blur(10px)` icon pills (opacity 0.25) as texture and a graduated progressive-blur ramp. The pinned nav sits **transparent over the hero photo, not frosted** — glass is a local demo device here, never the chrome.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Built in **Framer**; the one captured transition is `0.4s cubic-bezier(0.44,0,0.56,1)` — a near-symmetric ease-in-out — plus staggered entrance offsets (150/300/500ms). No CSS `@keyframes` (0); motion is JS/rAF-driven (460 rAF calls, 83 IntersectionObservers). Communicates "polished, alive," not "showpiece." **Reduced motion is NOT respected** (0 PRM rules) — the single biggest a11y defect; see Interaction & motion. Full choreography below.
- **Density:** Bimodal — high inside the dashboard/score chips, low everywhere else. The gap is the point.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device = **typeface + scale** (light serif display vs sans body), reinforced by the lone acid accent for actions and the warm-vs-dark section blocking.
- **Restraint:** No multi-hue palette, no gradients on chrome, no heavy shadows (only a ~5% ambient blur under floating cards), no decorative icons — photography + one accent + one serif do the work.

## Interaction & motion

Solidroad's motion is **Framer's standard appear/scroll kit, used tastefully**: staggered in-view reveals, a scroll-linked headline recolor, a section background that warms as you scroll, and small parallax/rotation on the floating product chips. Evidence: **0 `@keyframes`** (Framer animates via JS), **83 IntersectionObservers over 83 targets** (thresholds stepped 0→0.99, rootMargin `0px`/`100px`), **4 scroll + 9 mousemove listeners**, **460 rAF calls**, library **Framer**, 1 `<canvas>` (the decorative trail/halftone) and **9 marquee** elements (logo wall). One transition curve (`cubic-bezier(0.44,0,0.56,1)`, 400ms). **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT respected** — honest and important. Frames s00→s10 corroborate the techniques below; `from==to` opacity/background rows in the raw profile were dropped as noise.

- **Staggered in-view fade-rise reveal** — *Trigger:* in-view (IntersectionObserver) + a load stagger on the hero. *Mechanism:* blocks enter with `opacity 0→1` and a small `transform` rise; the hero sequences headline → subcopy → CTA → logo row at ~150/300/500ms (entrance capture). *Reads as:* the page "settles into place," calm and deliberate. *Transfers to:* any section-by-section marketing page. *Safety:* **production-safe** on the compositor (opacity/transform only) **BUT here reduced-motion is not gated** — the resting full-opacity state must be the DOM default so a no-JS/reduced-motion user still sees content. → `scroll-reveal-in-view`.
- **Scroll-linked headline recolor (read-along)** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the big statement headline ("CX teams use Solidroad to raise the quality bar… across all *conversations*") starts with its trailing lines in `muted`/light and darkens them to `fg` as the block scrolls up through the viewport (frames s04→s05). *Reads as:* the sentence is "read into focus" line by line. *Transfers to:* a single hero/manifesto statement you want to pace. *Safety:* **production-safe** (color only) — keep the unscrolled state legible (don't start below contrast). → `scroll-linked-recolor`.
- **Section background warm-shift on scroll** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* a section background crossfades from white to peach (`#ffffff → #fdecd8`) as it scrolls through view (captured on `framer-wchy1`), then the next block flips to a dark green photo — the page "warms then cools." *Reads as:* chapters; an ambient sense of progress without a hard divider. *Transfers to:* long pages that need rhythm/wayfinding between acts. *Safety:* **production-safe** (background paint; cheap if you crossfade a token, not an image). → `scroll-tinted-section-bg`.
- **Scroll-progress chip rotation + parallax** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the floating score chips in the "Reviewing scores" demo (Camila T. 85%, Drake S., …) rotate ≈ -6° and parallax-drift at slightly different rates as the card scrolls. *Reads as:* a loose, physical "pile of live scores," not a static screenshot. *Transfers to:* product demos that want to feel animated/real. *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform only) — keep rotation small to avoid motion-sickness; gate on reduced-motion (this site doesn't). → `scroll-progress-rotate` / `parallax-drift`.
- **Pinned nav + hover opacity dim** — *Trigger:* scroll (nav `position: fixed`) / hover. *Mechanism:* the nav pins to the top; links and buttons dip opacity (~1→0.77) on hover over the 400ms symmetric curve. *Reads as:* persistent wayfinding + quiet, responsive chrome. *Transfers to:* any marketing nav. *Safety:* **production-safe**.
- **Numbered progress carousel** — *Trigger:* tap/auto-advance. *Mechanism:* a feature showcase steps through panels with a "1/4" counter, a thin progress track that fills, and prev/next arrow buttons (transform slide; Framer renders it without a native carousel role). *Reads as:* "there's more, here's where you are." *Transfers to:* feature/benefit showcases too big for one screen. *Safety:* **production-safe** but needs real keyboard + ARIA wiring and a non-auto-advance fallback. *(No corpus recipe yet — flagged `patterns_to_build: numbered-progress-carousel`.)*

> **Honest note on thin spots:** the single `<canvas>` (likely the drawn summit trail / footer halftone) and most `transform/parallax` rows are gentle decorative drift, not a scrollytelling pin — not elevated to techniques. The acid-yellow "trail" over the mountain is a *static* brand graphic, not animated in the read frames.

## Production-safety note

- Warm-neutral + single acid accent — **production-safe**; strong a11y for text (warm-black on cream clears 4.5:1). **Acid-yellow `#dbf400` must stay a fill behind dark text** — never colored text on white (fails contrast badly).
- Light-weight serif at display size — **production-safe** but font-critical: subset ABC Arizona to display/latin, set a serif fallback with matching metrics to avoid CLS, and don't go below ~32px or the 300 weight reads frail.
- Full-bleed photo behind white headline text (hero, "Built by people who understand the work") — **production-safe** only with a scrim/overlay; verify headline contrast across the photo's bright patches.
- **Reduced motion: NOT respected (defect).** All reveals/recolors/parallax run regardless of `prefers-reduced-motion`. To reuse safely, wrap every effect in `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` and ship the resting (end) state in the DOM — the patterns below do this.
- **A11y (honest, inferred from build):** Framer marketing default — likely fine focus rings but **no reduced-motion path**; verify the numbered carousel exposes ARIA + keyboard, and that the score-chip rotation isn't the only signal. Tap targets are mostly large pills (good), but confirm the small dashboard rows on mobile reach 44px.
