## What works (transferable principles)

- **One emerald accent on a black-ink / pure-white base — color is rationed, not decorative.** Exactly one hue (`#199b5e`) carries the logo, links, live metrics, status dot, and hairline flourishes; everything else is near-black ink (`#08090a`) on white (`#ffffff`) with a cool-grey muted ramp. Because the page is otherwise monochrome, the single accent reads unambiguously as "this is interactive / this is a real number." Transferable rule: on an information-dense product page, spend color only where it must mean something; a lone accent against neutral ink does more work than a palette.
- **Monospace is a semantic tool, not a code-block default.** A second face (Paper Mono) is reserved for *values and labels that should feel machine-generated*: the `55.6262%` metric chips, eyebrow tags, stat rows, badges. Pairing a humanist sans (Inter, human context) with mono (system output) lets the typeface itself signal "live data" without extra UI. Reusable anywhere you want figures to read as measured rather than marketed — dashboards, pricing, status, changelogs.
- **Hierarchy from scale in a near-single-weight system.** Only weights 400 and 500 exist; a headline is a headline because it is ~2.5-3× body and tracked tighter (~-0.025em on display), not because it is bold. This keeps a copy-heavy page optically calm and removes the shouty feel of heavy display weights. Transferable to any content-dense surface that needs gravitas without aggression.
- **Show the product as live chrome, not a screenshot.** The hero renders an actual-looking docs dashboard (sidebar groups, tabs, content, card grid) on a lifted white surface that bleeds off the right edge. Seeing the real information architecture sells competence before the copy does, and DOM-built chrome stays crisp and theme-able. Transferable to any tool whose value is legible in its own UI.
- **Whitespace and hairlines do the sectioning — almost no boxes or fills.** Sections are separated by large vertical rhythm (block padding stepping 48 → 80 → 104px) and at most a 1px near-invisible rule; background stays white throughout except a few intentional full-bleed customer cards. A very long page (8k+ px) stays scannable because separation is spatial, not chromatic. Buy legibility with space, not with borders.
- **Color is concentrated into a few full-bleed "story" tiles for contrast relief.** Against the white field, customer case-study cards go edge-to-edge in a single brand color each (clay `#ef6333`, periwinkle `#99b7ff`, forest `#003320`) carrying a logo + one stat. The restraint everywhere else makes these saturated tiles land as punctuation rather than noise. Transferable: quarantine expressive color into a small number of deliberate blocks so the rest of the system stays disciplined.
- **An animated lime gradient "topo line" motif is the only ornament, and it never touches text.** Faint flowing green-to-lime lines sweep behind the hero and stitch the embedded frame to the page; they sit on white margins, never under body copy, so contrast is never at risk. One recurring decorative motif, contained — the signature without the cost.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Inter (sans, all headings + body) + Paper Mono (values/labels/eyebrows) + a trace of Arizona Flare serif (≈2 nodes, negligible). Effective scale ≈ 12 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 24 / 35-36 / 44 / 50px — roughly a 1.2-1.4 step, ~3× body-to-hero range. Weights only 400 + 500. Tracking is **size-graded, not one constant**: it tightens as type grows — 50px hero ≈ -2px (≈ -0.04em), 44px ≈ -0.88px (≈ -0.02em), 36px ≈ -0.72px (≈ -0.02em), 35px ≈ -0.35px (≈ -0.01em), 24px ≈ -0.24px (≈ -0.01em); body sits at ~0. So "bigger = tighter" rather than a single display em value. Hierarchy = scale + the sans/mono split, never weight.
- **Color:** Accent emerald `#199b5e` (logo, links, metrics, status, hairlines). Ink `#08090a`; secondary ink `#121715`. Muted ramp `#485450` → `#717d79` → `#969e9b`. Background pure white `#ffffff`, faint surface `#fafafa`, light fill `#f1f0ee`. Live-data wash = accent at 8% alpha (`rgba(31,167,122,0.08)`). CTA is inverted: black pill, white text. Story-card brand colors (clay/periwinkle/forest) are per-customer, not core. No warm cast (contrast with teak); neutrals are slightly cool.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 4/8px base. Common gaps 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32px; section block padding steps 40 / 48 / 80 / 104px — the big jumps create the airy section separation.
- **Layout system:** Centered ~1280px max content. Asymmetric hero (copy left, product frame right, bleeding off-edge). Logo wall and stat rows are evenly-spaced flex rows; case studies are a card grid with mixed spans. Strong left-alignment for prose. Mobile collapses to a single column, the product frame de-bleeds and stacks, CTAs go full-width.
- **Grid / container:** Container max ≈ 1280px (centered). The captured grid is a **24-column base track** with content items spanning **3 / 4 / 5** columns — i.e. the feature-card row is three ~8-col cells, the logo wall and case-study grid pack 4-/5-up. Common column gaps run 16 / 24 / 32px (no fixed gutter token surfaced; from the spacing scale). At 390px mobile the grid collapses to a **4-column base** with 1 / 2 / 3-span items (single- to half-width), so a desktop 3-up becomes 1- or 2-up.
- **Components (from captured states):**
  - **Button** — primary is a black pill (`#08090a` bg, white text); hover darkens fill slightly (to ~`oklab(0.139 … / 0.9)`, ≈ -10% lightness, ~280ms). Secondary/ghost links (Sign in, "Sign up with Google") are white/near-white (`rgb(254,253,251)`) and on hover wash to a faint ink tint (`rgba(0,0,0,0.03)`). **Focus** = a 2px emerald outline (`#199b5e`) on most actions; a couple of dark CTAs use a 2px near-black outline instead. **Disabled** = `opacity 0.4`, `cursor: default`, ink text held on the pale fill.
  - **Card** — feature/case-study cards are flat hairline surfaces (radius 12-24px, near-zero shadow); the "live" interest is supplied by their *in-view scale-pop* content and the topo-line ornament, not by elevation or a hover lift.
  - **Nav** — sticky top bar; items open disclosure dropdowns (Products / Solutions / Resources) with a slide/scale panel; nav-adjacent links carry the same faint-tint hover as ghost buttons. 10 visible links desktop → 7 + likely hamburger at mobile.
  - **Input** — the hero "Ask AI anything…" field and search are pill/rounded inputs on white with a hairline; no captured filled or error state (marketing surface, not a real form), so treat input styling as inferred from the chrome.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Captured durations cluster `100 / 150 / 200 / 280 / 300 / 500ms`, a `1050ms` count-up tier, and a `40000ms` ambient loop (the hero topo-line field). Easings: snappy UI on `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` / `(0,0,0.2,1)`; a decisive `(0,0,0,1)` entrance; a long cinematic settle `(0.23,1,0.32,1)` (easeOutQuint-ish) for scroll/scale reveals; and one spring `(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` with gentle overshoot for the in-view scale-pops and status pings. Stagger is short — `55 / 110 / 165 / 220ms` (≈55ms step) on the scaling chips and counters, not a page-load cascade. Intent: decisive on interaction, springy on small reveals, slow and ambient on the one decorative field. Full choreography below.
- **Density:** High — many sections, logos, stats, case studies, a changelog — kept legible by single-family type, the sans/mono semantic split, large inter-section whitespace, and rationed color.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant ordering device is **scale**; **color** (ink vs muted vs the lone emerald) is the secondary device. Weight is deliberately not a lever.
- **Restraint:** No bold weights; near-zero shadow; tiny radii on chrome; no per-section background boxes; one accent color; gradient kept off text. Expressiveness is concentrated in three places (hero gradient lines, embedded product frame, full-bleed story cards) and the rest is disciplined neutral.

## Interaction & motion

Mintlify's motion is **mostly functional with one ambient flourish** — sticky chrome, in-view scale-pops, ticking live counters, and a slow looping green "topo-line" field. No animation library is loaded (no GSAP/Lenis/Framer globals); it's **hand-rolled CSS `@keyframes` + IntersectionObserver + canvas/rAF**. Evidence from the capture: 16 `@keyframes` (`scaleIn`/`scaleOut`, `digit-in`, `stats-scroll`, `signal-ping`/`signal-flicker`, `enterFromRight`/`exitToLeft`/`navContentIn`, `loader-spin`, `spin`), 24 IntersectionObservers over 115 targets, 3 scroll listeners, 15 mousemove listeners, ~710 rAF calls per scroll pass, 15 `<canvas>` nodes, and **3 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks — reduced motion is respected.** Notably `entranceReveals: 0` / `staggeredEntrance: false` — there is **no page-load fade-rise cascade**; the in-view beat is a *scale* pop, not an opacity+translateY reveal (frames s00→s09 show content already settled, with chips/tags scaling in as cards enter). Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` · `<canvas>` + rAF. Triggers seen: scroll-progress, in-view, hover, drag/tap.

- **Ambient topo-line gradient field** — *Trigger:* load (continuous, ~40s loop). *Mechanism:* faint blue→emerald→lime lines (`linear-gradient(106deg, rgba(68,174,255,.5) … rgba(186,255,36,.5))`) flow across canvas behind the hero and *inside* each feature card, always on white margins. *Reads as:* a living "knowledge graph / data flow" signature without a single word obscured (frames s00, s01, s02, s05 show the lines shifting position card-to-card while text stays clear). *Transfers to:* dev-tool / data-platform heroes wanting one signature ornament. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** — 15 canvases + ~710 rAF/pass is the page's real GPU/battery cost; keep it decorative, off text, and `prefers-reduced-motion`-gated to a static gradient. (No file — bespoke ornament; reuse the *principle*: one contained animated field, never under copy.) → `patterns_to_build: ambient-gradient-line-field`.
- **In-view scale-pop** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO, 115 targets; `scale 0.9→1.1`, `1.21→0.9`, `0.85→0.8` per element). *Mechanism:* on intersect, a class flip drives a CSS `transform: scale()` settle via `scaleIn`/`scaleOut`, easing on the spring `cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1)` with a short 55-220ms stagger — the orbiting product-logo chips and floating "Agent 130 / User 007" role tags (frame s03) bloom as their card enters. *Reads as:* the card's contents "come online" rather than the whole section fading in. *Transfers to:* feature-card contents, badges, avatars — a lighter alternative to fade-rise. *Safety:* **production-safe** (compositor-only transform, once-only; cap overshoot and gate reduced-motion). → `scroll-scale`.
- **Live count-up / odometer stat rows** — *Trigger:* in-view, then a short rAF (~1050ms). *Mechanism:* mono digits in faint-emerald pills roll 0→value via `digit-in` + `stats-scroll`; the "Pages read 0123456789…" strip uses a `transform/parallax` **odometer roll** rather than a numeric tween. Frame s01 catches them mid-count ("Agents at work today 6,672 · API requests 126,096 · Content updates 21,551"); s06 shows the settled hero stats (300M+ / 2B+ / 99.99%). *Reads as:* "these are live, measured numbers," reinforcing the sans+mono semantic split. *Transfers to:* hero stats, status pages, changelogs, pricing. *Safety:* **production-safe** with `tabular-nums`, a visible label, and the final value shipped in the DOM (gate the roll behind reduced-motion). → `count-up-stat`.
- **Sticky top-nav** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the header is `position: sticky` and persists across the whole scroll (present in every frame s00→s09); dropdowns open with `navContentIn`/`enterFromRight` slide-scale panels. *Reads as:* wayfinding stays put on a very tall (8k+px) page. *Transfers to:* any long marketing/docs page. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky, no JS). → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling` (the pin primitive; here it's plain sticky chrome, not scrollytelling).
- **"Live" status ping** — *Trigger:* load (looping). *Mechanism:* an emerald status dot pulses via `signal-ping` / `signal-flicker` (opacity `0.22→0.05`, `scale 2.466→3.023` on the halo) — the "All systems normal" affordance and the green dots in the collaborator card (frame s03). *Reads as:* real-time / operational. *Transfers to:* status indicators, presence, "live" badges. *Safety:* **production-safe** when small and gated (transform/opacity only; a continuously-pulsing element should still pause under reduced-motion). (No standalone file; trivial CSS keyframe.)
- **Full-bleed story-card carousel** — *Trigger:* drag/tap (+ prev/next controls). *Mechanism:* 4 carousels swipe through the saturated edge-to-edge customer cards (clay / periwinkle / forest, frames s05–s06) carrying logo + one stat; `disclosure ×4` covers the nav menus. *Reads as:* color punctuation you can browse, user-controlled. *Transfers to:* logo/case-study walls, testimonials. *Safety:* **production-safe** (keep it keyboard-operable and arrow-controllable, not drag-only).
- **Hover recolor (chrome)** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* buttons/links shift `background` over ~280ms `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` — black CTA darkens ~10%, ghost links wash to a faint ink tint. *Reads as:* responsive, intentional chrome. *Transfers to:* all interactive elements. *Safety:* **production-safe** (no `transform`; recolor only). → `hover-recolor` (no file).

## Production-safety note

- Single-accent emerald system, ink/white base, sans+mono semantic pairing, two-weight scale-driven type, hairline + whitespace sectioning, flat low-shadow chrome: all **production-safe** and cheap — they aid a11y (contrast preserved, color never the sole signal where labels accompany it) and perf (two real faces, minimal shadow).
- The **embedded product frame** is **production-safe** when built from markup (crisp, theme-able, landmark-able) rather than a PNG; keep its illustrative copy out of the canonical document outline.
- **Inline metric chips / stat rows** are **production-safe** with `tabular-nums` and a visible label; if values count up, gate the animation behind `prefers-reduced-motion` and ship the final value in the DOM.
- The **animated lime gradient lines** are **production-safe when static or `prefers-reduced-motion`-gated**; as a continuously animated canvas/WebGL field they trend **spectacle-only** (GPU/battery, paint churn). Keep them decorative and never behind text.
- **A11y audit (honest):** **img alt — perfect** (alt-text ratio 1.0; all 44 images labeled). **Focus-visible — present** (focus-visible reported true; most actions show a 2px emerald `#199b5e` outline, a few dark CTAs a near-black one — verify the emerald ring clears 3:1 against adjacent surfaces). **Reduced motion — respected** (3 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks). **Tap targets — the real defect:** 36 of 85 interactive elements (~42%) measure under the 44px minimum — the dense nav links, carousel arrows, and inline metric/stat chips are the likely offenders; enlarge hit areas before reusing this density on touch. Also recall the **muted ramp** (`#969e9b`, `#717d79`) is borderline on white at small sizes — keep those ≥ body size or darken to hold 4.5:1.

## Extracted patterns

- embedded-product-frame — a styled, off-edge-bleeding recreation of the real product chrome used as the hero visual; reuse for any tool whose IA sells itself.
- inline-metric-chip — label (sans) + value (mono, accent) on a faint accent wash, signaling live/measured data; reuse for hero stats, stat rows, pricing, status, changelogs.
- scroll-scale — in-view `transform: scale()` pop (spring-eased, once-only) for card contents/badges/avatars; a lighter alternative to fade-rise. (motion)
- count-up-stat — figures roll 0→value (or odometer-style) when in view, in mono on `tabular-nums`; ship the final value in the DOM and gate behind reduced-motion. (motion)
- sticky-pin-scrollytelling — the `position: sticky` pin primitive; here used plainly for persistent top-nav on a tall page (not full scrollytelling). (motion)
- **patterns_to_build:** ambient-gradient-line-field — a single continuous canvas field of flowing blue→lime "topo lines" behind the hero / inside cards, on white margins only, `prefers-reduced-motion`-gated to a static gradient. No reusable recipe exists yet (spectacle-only). (motion)
