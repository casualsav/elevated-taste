## What works
- **The hero is a photograph with nothing written on it.** No overlaid headline, no overlaid CTA, no scrim, no gradient — a full-bleed image of a poolside lounger, a spritz and a dog, running edge to edge under the header. The page's actual first job (pick a city, pick a date) is carried by the fixed booking row directly above it, so the image is free to do only the thing an image is good at: set a temperature. This is the cheapest available escape from the generic template — when the product *is* the atmosphere (hotels, restaurants, venues, apparel, travel), an unlettered photo plus a working control bar beats any headline you could write over it. The corollary is strict: you may only drop the headline if the utility above it is unambiguous.
- **Hierarchy runs on the width axis, not the weight axis.** Headings are set in an *extended* grotesque at regular weight; body is the *light* (300) cut of the same superfamily. So the size jump (16px → 40–80px) is reinforced by letterforms getting physically wider and denser, while the weight actually goes *up* from 300 to 400 — a one-step change most systems would spend three weights on. Anywhere you have a width-axis face (or a variable font with `wdth`), this buys you a second, quieter hierarchy dimension and keeps the page from getting shouty.
- **Two creams two percent apart chunk the page — no rules, no cards, no shadows.** The ground alternates `#fef2e3` and `#fef9f3`. That difference is barely nameable in isolation, but it reliably says "new section" at a glance, and it does so without a single `<hr>`, container border, or elevation. Exactly one box-shadow exists on the entire page (a modal). Reuse: pick two tints of your paper colour ~2–4% apart and band with them before you reach for a divider.
- **One saturated colour, spent on one button.** Coral `#ff9272` appears on the *booking* CTA and essentially nowhere else. Everything else — headings, body, borders, footer — is a single warm ink `#3e3a37` (never black; black is reserved for the announcement bar). Scarcity is what makes the coral read as "this is the thing to press" without a size increase or an arrow.
- **The primary CTA's label is a dark shade of the CTA, not white.** Coral field, burnt-sienna text `#7f2006`. White on coral would be a contrast failure and a temperature break; a heavily darkened sibling of the accent passes contrast *and* keeps the button inside the palette. Transfers to any pastel or mid-tone accent that white can't sit on legibly.
- **Square by default; the pill is a state, not a style.** Cards, images, buttons and inputs are all hard-cornered. The only fully rounded objects on the page are the region filter chips — so roundness carries meaning ("this is a toggle") instead of being decoration. A radius scale of `{0, pill}` is a defensible system.
- **The card rail bleeds off both viewport edges.** The hotel-locations row is clipped mid-card left *and* right. A row that ends flush inside the container reads as "that's all of them"; a row cut by the viewport reads as "keep going" and earns its arrows. Cheapest possible affordance for horizontal content.
- **Each type step carries a baked-in line-height on an 8px grid.** The scale is a named px ladder (`size-16/20/24/32/40/48/56/72/80/96/136`) where every step declares its own leading, and every leading lands on a multiple of 8 (24, 32, 40, 48, 64…). Vertical rhythm therefore survives a section that mixes four sizes — which a bare `line-height: 1.4` ratio does not.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** One superfamily doing everything. `GT America Extended Regular` (fallback: a serif) for headings, buttons and CTAs; `GT America Light` (300) for all body copy; `GT America Medium` at 9px/1.5px-tracking/uppercase for field labels and eyebrows; a `Tiempos Fine` serif is loaded for editorial accents. The ladder is 14 · 16 · 20 · 24 · 32 · 40 · 48 · 56 · 72 · 80 · 96 · 136 · 195px with per-step leading (ratio ≈1.25 in the middle, tightening to ≈1.1 at display). Desktop h1 80/104, body 16/24. Mobile halves display (80 → 40) and leaves body untouched. Hierarchy = **size × width**, with weight nearly static.
- **Color:** Two creams as ground (`#fef2e3` page, `#fef9f3` alternate band), one warm ink `#3e3a37` for *all* text and all strong borders, `#efe4d6` as the only hairline, coral `#ff9272` + burnt sienna `#7f2006` as the single accent pair, pure black reserved for the announcement bar, and a near-black `#313130` footer. There is no muted *text* tier — de-emphasis is done with size, never with grey. The only grey (`#9e9e9e`) is the carousel's `1 — 5` position counter. Focus rings are olive `#415922`, a hue that appears nowhere else on the page: functional colour, deliberately outside the brand.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base; the dominant gap is 8px, section padding runs 72 → 80 → 128px via a four-step `sm/md/lg/xl` utility scale that grows at ≥768px. Content sits in a 1280–1400px shell; imagery and the card rails break out of it. The generosity is vertical, not horizontal — the cards themselves are tight.
- **Layout system:** Flex-dominant (958 flex containers vs 48 grid), 36 elements using `aspect-ratio` to lock image boxes. The page skeleton is a **catalog**: full-bleed photo → city carousel → filtered hotel rail → two 2-up editorial grids → newsletter → footer. The 2-up grids are separated by cross-hairlines (one vertical between cells, one horizontal between rows), so four cards read as a quadrant, not four boxes. Breakpoints at 375 / 480 / 576 / 640 / 768 / 1400.
- **Layering / page architecture:** A **single flat scroll**. `architecture.layers_summary` reports `has_static_backdrop: false`, `has_blend_overlay: false`; `blend_glass` is empty on all three axes; every one of the 7 content surfaces is opaque (`transparent_height_pct: 0`). All 14 fixed layers are chrome — announcement bar (z 3), header (z 12), the booking dialog scrim (`rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`, z 5), consent banner (z 2147483645). Depth on this page is created by *photography inside opaque frames*, not by z-stacking translucent planes. Worth stating: a hospitality page can feel rich with zero glass.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Transitions cluster at 300 / 450 / 600ms (`ease`), with one signature reveal curve `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0, 1)` (hard-out, long settle) on header and text reveals, and `cubic-bezier(0.165, 0.84, 0.44, 1)` on the link underline. 36 `@keyframes`, 280 transition rules, one library (Swiper), zero scroll-driven parallax. Motion here confirms interaction and hands off content; it never performs.
- **Density:** High in the chrome (17 visible nav links, a three-tab booking dialog with a 12-month calendar, 828 buttons), low in the body (4 h2s, 29 h3s, 56 images at 39 with `srcset` / 31 lazy). The page keeps a booking engine's complexity behind one coral button.
- **Hierarchy:** Scale first, then letterform width, then ground colour (which band am I in). Position fourth. Colour never ranks text.
- **Restraint:** No overlaid hero copy. No gradient, no glass, no blend mode, no shadow system, no dark mode, no three-feature-card grid, no fake dashboard. No second accent. No `text-decoration` anywhere — links carry drawn underlines instead.

## Interaction & motion
Evidence: 11 frames; 12 scroll effects (4 pin, 3 fade-in, 3 opacity, 2 transform); 6 IntersectionObservers over 23 targets at thresholds `0.01` / `0.5`; 4 scroll listeners; Swiper as the only library; 36 `@keyframes`; **6 `prefers-reduced-motion` rules** and `respectsReducedMotion: true`.

- **Scroll-condensing booking header** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (in-view sentinel). *Mechanism:* two header variants exist in the DOM — a tall one (logo · nav links · booking row) and a condensed one (logo · location · CTA). Past the fold the nav row crossfades out (`opacity 1 → 0`) and the header collapses from ~163px to ~95px, keeping the announcement bar and the booking row pinned. Frames s00 vs s03/s06 confirm the nav links are gone while "Select Location / Find a Room" survives verbatim. *Reads as:* the site dropping browsing chrome and keeping the transaction. *Transfers to:* any site with a primary conversion control in the header — booking, search, cart. *Safety:* **production-safe** (opacity + a height collapse confined to the fixed header subtree; the page below never reflows). See `patterns/scroll-condensing-booking-header.md`.
- **Masked line-rise reveal** — *Trigger:* in-view. *Mechanism:* `@keyframes translate-up { from { transform: translateY(103%) } to { translateY(0) } }` on each line inside an `overflow: hidden` wrapper, run on the `cubic-bezier(0.5, 0, 0, 1)` reveal curve. The 103% (not 100%) is the tell — it clears descenders so no tail peeks below the mask. *Reads as:* type setting itself. *Transfers to:* any headline or section title; costs one wrapper element per line. *Safety:* **production-safe** — transform-only, gated on the site's reduced-motion rules.
- **Announcement bar with rotating message** — *Trigger:* load / timer. *Mechanism:* a fixed black bar (4vh, z 3) whose single line swaps via paired `swipe-up-out` → `swipe-up-in` keyframes; a `ticker-animation` (`translate3d(-50%, 0, 0)`) marquee handles overflow on narrow viewports. Frames confirm two different offers at s00 and s03. *Reads as:* one promotional slot, not a stack of banners. *Transfers to:* offer/status bars anywhere. *Safety:* **production-safe** as a swap; the marquee variant is **spectacle-only** near body copy (perpetual motion is a reading tax) and must stop under reduced-motion.
- **Retracting underline link** — *Trigger:* hover/focus. *Mechanism:* the underline is drawn (`::after` bar at `scaleX(1)`, or a 1px `linear-gradient` background at `background-size: 100% 100%`) and *withdraws* on hover to `scaleX(0)`, 400ms on `cubic-bezier(0.165, 0.84, 0.44, 1)`. Inverting the usual direction means the link is fully underlined at rest — discoverable without hover, which the conventional grow-on-hover underline is not. *Transfers to:* any editorial link set. *Safety:* **production-safe**; keep the resting underline so the affordance survives reduced-motion and touch.
- **Filtered peek rail** — *Trigger:* tap. *Mechanism:* four pill `<button>`s (`Europe / UK & Ireland / USA / All`, each with an `aria-label`) reset a horizontally-scrolling Swiper of hotel cards that is clipped by the viewport on both sides. *Reads as:* a catalog you're browsing, not a grid you're comparing. *Transfers to:* store locators, category rails, plan pickers. *Safety:* **production-safe**. Ship the chips as real buttons with `aria-pressed` (this site sets `class="active"` only) and keep the rail keyboard-scrollable. See `patterns/filtered-peek-rail.md`.
- **Lazy image fade-in** — *Trigger:* in-view. *Mechanism:* `img.lazyload { opacity: 0 }` → `1` over 300ms once decoded, at IO threshold `0.01`. On a 56-image page this is the whole loading story: no skeletons, no blur-up. *Safety:* **production-safe**; reserve the box with `aspect-ratio` (this page does, 36 times) or the fade becomes a layout shift.

**Reduced-motion: respected** (`prmRules: 6`, `respectsReducedMotion: true`). Focus is visible everywhere (3px olive outline). Alt coverage is 100% across 56 images.

## Production-safety note
Everything here is production-safe in kind — opacity, transform, and a class toggle. Three cautions on reuse: (1) the announcement-bar *marquee* variant must be swap-only under `prefers-reduced-motion`; (2) the filter chips carry `aria-label` but no `aria-pressed`, so the active region is announced by colour alone — fix that; (3) 57 of 2,264 interactive elements fall under the 44px tap-target minimum, almost all of them calendar day cells inside the booking dialog — the page-level controls (56px CTA, 48px chips) are exemplary.

## Extracted patterns
- `scroll-condensing-booking-header` — a fixed multi-row header that drops its navigation row past the fold and keeps the conversion row; reuse wherever the header holds the primary transaction control.
- `filtered-peek-rail` — pill filter chips over a horizontally-scrolling card rail deliberately clipped by both viewport edges; reuse for locators, category browsers, and any set too long to grid.
