## What works
- **Typeface roles carry the brand instead of color.** A humanist geometric display sans (theFuture) does all the *expressive* work (headlines, pull-quotes) and a monospace (Azeret Mono) does all the *functional* work (nav, buttons, eyebrows, stat labels, breadcrumbs). The mono instantly signals "terminal/developer product" without a single saturated color or skeuomorphic device — the meaning lives in the type pairing. Transfers to any tool brand that wants technical credibility on a clean canvas: split type by *job* (display = voice, mono = chrome), don't reach for a neon accent.
- **Hierarchy is built from scale + a warm-neutral value ladder, not weight or hue.** Display jumps ~4–6× over body in a single regular weight; emphasis comes from ink (#0a0908) vs. warm-muted gray (#5f5d59) on warm off-white, never from bold. The page reads calm and editorial even though every section is information-dense. Transfers anywhere you want density without shout: one weight, big scale contrast, two grays.
- **A warm off-white + cream + pale-lavender canvas reads premium where pure #fff/#000 reads generic.** Shifting the "white" to #fcfcfc/#f8f5ee and the "black" to a warm #0a0908 removes the clinical default-template feel for nearly zero effort; lavender (#f6f1ff) tint panels and a lone terracotta dot are the only chroma. Transfers to any minimalist marketing site: warm your neutrals before adding accent color.
- **Feature sections use a strict repeating contract, so scanning is effortless.** Every feature is the same shape — left: short mono eyebrow → display heading → 2-line muted body → small mono pill button; right: a floating product-UI screenshot on a tinted panel. Predictable rhythm lets the *content* differentiate while the layout disappears. Transfers to any multi-feature page: pick one section template and repeat it, alternating only the image side.
- **The primary CTA is engineered to never disappear.** "Download Warp" is a solid ink control with a monospace label and a keycap "D" badge (a keyboard-shortcut affordance that reinforces the developer story); on mobile the nav collapses to a hamburger but this button stays pinned in the header and goes full-width below the fold. Transfers to any conversion page: protect one canonical action across every breakpoint.
- **Live product proof over marketing illustration.** Hero and every feature show the actual app (sessions sidebar, agent run lists, admin/credits dashboard, model picker) rather than abstract graphics; an animated count-up stats row (active developers / Fortune-500 / agents-per-day) and a mono logo marquee do the social proof. Transfers to dev tools: show the real UI; abstract hero art reads as a product that isn't ready.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** Three explicit roles. Display = theFuture, a warm humanist-geometric sans (single-story a, circular o), regular weight, tight leading (~1.06–1.1) and slightly negative tracking, used at ~3–6rem for headlines and curly-quote pull-quotes. Body = Inter at 1rem/1.5, muted gray. Chrome = Azeret Mono, ~0.75–0.875rem, often uppercase with ~0.75px positive tracking, for nav, buttons, eyebrows and stat captions. Hierarchy = scale contrast (~4–6×) in one weight, never bold.
- **Color:** Warm-neutral, not chromatic. bg #fcfcfc, cream surface #f8f5ee, ink #0a0908, muted #5f5d59, hairline borders ~#c4c0ba. Accents are deliberately tiny: lavender #c7aeff/#f6f1ff as panel tints and illustration fills, terracotta #d97757 as a single dot. Controls are ink-on-white / white-on-ink — no colored buttons. Roles: bg/surface(cream)/tint(lavender)/fg(ink)/muted/border/accent(lavender, decorative only).
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base; common steps 4/8/12/16/24/40/64/96. Tight 4–12px gaps inside controls and list rows (terminal density); generous 64–96px vertical gaps between sections. The contrast between dense components and airy section breaks is what keeps it calm.
- **Layout system:** ~1280px max content width, generous outer margins. Asymmetric hero (large headline left, narrow muted intro right). Feature sections are a 2-column text/visual split that alternates image side down the page. Stats stay 3-up even on mobile; nav collapses to hamburger while the CTA persists. Breakpoint behavior: multi-column → single-column stack, CTA → full-width.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Standard material easing `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` at 150–200ms for hover/state on controls; a snappier `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` for entrance; count-up odometers on the stats; CSS-keyframe scrolling strips (`scroll-left`/`scroll-right`/`scroll-up`) for the logo/capability tickers; an embedded auto-playing terminal/mini-app demo (pause control visible). Motion communicates "live system," kept short and utilitarian — full choreography (triggers, keyframes, probe counts) detailed in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** High inside components (terminal lists, credit tables, model menus) but legible because each dense block sits in lots of negative space and uses mono at a fixed small size — the eye treats dense blocks as "screenshots," not body text.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant ordering device is **scale**, backed by the value ladder (ink vs. muted) and by typeface role (display vs. mono) signaling importance.
- **Restraint:** No saturated brand color on type or primary buttons; no gradients on the marketing surface (tints are flat); near-zero drop shadows except soft elevation on floating product cards; no decorative illustration — every visual is real product. Bold weight is essentially unused.

## Interaction & motion

Warp's motion is **feedback and polish, not spectacle** — it sits firmly on the production/usability side (capture: 12 hover effects, 20 scroll effects, but **0 canvas, 0 carousels, 0 cursor-followers**). The grammar is in-view recolor/reveal + tight hover states + a few looping "live system" demos; nothing scrubs the page or hijacks scroll. Evidence from the motion capture: 16 `@keyframes`, 8 CSS animation rules, 7 `IntersectionObserver`s over 83 targets, 4 scroll listeners, 1603 `requestAnimationFrame` calls in one pass (the auto-playing demo + count-ups, not a canvas loop), and **4 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks — reduced motion is respected.** No GSAP/Lenis/Framer globals; hand-rolled CSS keyframes + IntersectionObserver. House params: durations cluster at **150 / 200 / 300ms** (plus 100/240/320/400/500), easings `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` (standard) and `cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1)` (entrance settle), stagger **80 / 160ms**, IO thresholds 0.12 / 0.15 / 0.5 and a scrubbed `[0.15,0.35,0.55,0.75]` with `rootMargin: -30% 0px -55% 0px` (a "centered in viewport" band). Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` · one `<video>`.

- **Count-up stat odometers** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO). *Mechanism:* the social-proof row (active developers / Fortune-500 % / agents-per-day) animates 0→value on entry; the static capture froze it mid-roll (desktop 794K/56%/198K, mobile 643K/45%/160K — neither is the resting number), and the odometer's transient type sizes show up as the responsive `50.4px` / `64.8px` freezes in the size profile. *Reads as:* "real, growing usage." *Transfers to:* any metric/social-proof strip. *Safety:* **production-safe**, but **gate behind `prefers-reduced-motion`** and ship the resting value in the DOM (a frozen mid-animation number is wrong). → `count-up-stat`.
- **In-view fade reveal** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO; `keyframes: reveal`, thresholds ~0.12–0.15, `rootMargin 0px 0px -10%`). *Mechanism:* one class flip on intersect → opacity (and quote figures swapping `0→1` / `1→0`, e.g. the "Claude Code + Warp is peanut butter and…" pull-quote crossfade); fires once. *Reads as:* a long page settling in section by section. *Transfers to:* any tall marketing page, rotating testimonials. *Safety:* **production-safe** (GPU-cheap opacity, once-only, reduced-motion gated). → `fade-reveal`.
- **Scroll-into-view recolor** — *Trigger:* in-view (9 of 20 scroll effects are text color-shifts; 4 are background-shifts). *Mechanism:* section anchors/labels resolve color and the "Scale Across Your Team" block flips to the dark surface (text `→rgb(252,252,252)` on bg `→rgb(10,9,8)`) as it enters — visible at frame s06 where the credits/admin dashboard sits on ink. *Reads as:* "this section just became active." *Transfers to:* section dividers, eyebrow→headline, a dark proof band inside a light page. *Safety:* **production-safe** (one-shot; meaning isn't carried by hue alone). → `scroll-color-shift`.
- **CSS-keyframe ticker strips** — *Trigger:* continuous (CSS animation; `keyframes: scroll-left`, `scroll-right`, `scroll-up`). *Mechanism:* the logo / capability / "CHOOSE ANY HARNESS — Claude · OpenAI · self-host" strips translate on a loop (the probe counts **0 `<marquee>` elements** because these are transform loops, not a marquee tag; one strip also has a light `transform/parallax` on scroll). *Reads as:* an always-on capability/social wall. *Transfers to:* logo walls, capability rows. *Safety:* **production-safe with care** — provide a pause/hover affordance and gate under reduced motion (continuous translate can distract). → `marquee`.
- **Hover micro-states** — *Trigger:* hover (12 captured). *Mechanism:* nav items, dropdown rows and stat chips wash in a faint tint (`bg → oklab(0.14…/0.05)`, ~5% ink) and the play/scrub button flips border+color, over ~150–200ms `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`; the boxy 2–3px radii keep the wash reading as a terminal "key press." *Reads as:* responsive, low-key, "rendered with intent." *Transfers to:* all interactive chrome. *Safety:* **production-safe**, zero cost.
- **Auto-playing demo + ambient loops** — *Trigger:* load / in-view (1 `<video>`; `keyframes: mini-app-typing-bounce`, `mini-app-message-enter`, `warp-lite-story-reveal`, `warp-lite-composer-reveal`, `warp-lite-shimmer`, `pulse`, `spin`/`spin-slow`, `fill-progress`, `loading-spinner-in`). *Mechanism:* an embedded terminal/mini-app demo plays a scripted agent run (typing bounce, message-enter, shimmer, progress fill) with a visible pause control; a button also pulses opacity `1→0.25→1` on entry. *Reads as:* "the product is doing work right now" — the interface IS the pitch, animated. *Transfers to:* any product-demo hero. *Safety:* **spectacle-leaning but production-safe if budgeted** — the costliest thing here (the 1603 rAF/pass come from this, not a canvas); ship a poster + lazy-load to protect LCP, keep the pause control, and freeze to a static frame under reduced motion. (Bespoke to the content — reuse the *principle*: animate the real product run, gated + pausable.)

## Production-safety note
- Typeface role-split (display sans + mono chrome) — **production-safe**; cost is two extra webfonts (subset + `font-display: swap`); mono at small sizes is legible for short labels but avoid mono for long body copy (reading speed).
- Warm-neutral value-ladder palette — **production-safe**; verify ink-on-cream and muted-gray-on-cream both clear WCAG AA (the warm muted #5f5d59 on #f8f5ee is borderline for small text — test).
- Count-up stat odometers — **production-safe** but a *capture caveat*: a static screenshot freezes them mid-animation (desktop showed 794K/56%/198K, mobile 643K/45%/160K) — neither is the resting value; gate the animation behind `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Auto-scrolling marquee + auto-playing terminal demo — **production-safe with care**: provide a pause affordance (present here) and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; keep the demo lightweight (poster + lazy) to protect LCP.

## Extracted patterns
- typographic-role-split — pairing a humanist display sans for voice with a monospace for all UI chrome, on a warm-neutral canvas, to signal "developer tool" without color. Reuse for any technical/devtool brand.
- product-ui-on-tinted-panel — a floating real-product screenshot card resting on a flat pale-tint panel, as the right half of a repeating feature section. Reuse for product-marketing feature blocks that need to show the actual app.
