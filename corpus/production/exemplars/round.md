## What works (transferable principles)

- **Serif display over a sans body, on a pure-neutral palette.** A high-contrast transitional serif (PS Times) carries every headline; Inter carries all UI/body. The *typeface* switch — not weight or color — is the hierarchy device, so headings can stay near-regular weight and small caps eyebrows do the rest. Transfers to any content-led B2B page that wants an "editorial, considered" tone without shouting. Cost: needs a quality serif and tight optical sizing or it reads dated.
- **Two-tone headline ("statement" + greyed continuation).** Each section title is one serif sentence where the lead clause is `fg` and the trailing clause drops to `muted` (e.g. "No more API key copying. *We handle everything.*"). One typographic move encodes a primary/secondary idea in a single line — cheaper and calmer than two stacked sizes. Reusable for any section intro.
- **Restraint as the system: one accent, grayscale everything else.** The entire palette is achromatic (lab a≈0,b≈0) except a single electric blue used *only* for primary CTAs, eyebrow labels, and live-product accents. Color becomes a wayfinding signal ("this is clickable / this is the brand"), never decoration. Transfers to any product site that wants UI screenshots to be the visual interest, not the chrome.
- **Product UI as the hero image.** Below the hero copy sits a real, dense app screenshot in a bordered card; subsequent sections each pair a one-line claim with a cropped UI fragment. The interface *is* the proof, so marketing copy can stay short. Transfers to any tool whose value is legible on screen.
- **Section-scoped contrast inversion for "serious" content.** The enterprise-security block flips to near-black (`#111`) with light text while everything else is white. The single inversion partitions the page into "what it does" (light) vs "can I trust it" (dark) without a hard divider. Reusable to demarcate a tonal shift (security, pricing, enterprise) inside an otherwise light page.
- **Quad feature grid with consistent card anatomy.** Repeated 4-up rows: muted eyebrow → bold title → one-sentence body, each over a faint surface tile or UI thumbnail. Same anatomy repeated builds scannability; the eye learns the pattern once. Transfers to any capabilities/feature matrix.
- **Spend depth only where the product lives.** The marketing chrome is deliberately flat — hairline borders, no card shadows — but the embedded app shots get a real z-axis: receding cards are **frosted translucent tiles** (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` over a ~5% neutral at 50% alpha) stacked over faint backers, so the demo reads as a live, layered interface while the page around it stays calm. Concentrating the sole depth cue inside the product makes the UI look capable without making the page busy. Transfers to any tool site whose proof is an embedded interface. Cost: `backdrop-filter` paints on every composited frame — scope it to a handful of tiles, not the page.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Dual-family. Serif (PS Times) for display at ~36px/56px, leading ≈1.0–1.1, near-regular weight. Sans (Inter) for everything else: body 16/24, secondary 14/20, eyebrow 12 uppercase tracked +0.3px. Scale is restrained (~4 steps); hierarchy comes from **family switch + scale**, not many weights — 533 of ~590 nodes are weight 400. **Tracking:** essentially `normal` across the board — the capture reports no size-keyed tracking ramp (`tracking_by_size` is empty; the H1 measures `tracking: normal`). The only deliberate spacing is the small uppercase eyebrow (~+0.3px / +0.025em); display serif is left at its native metrics rather than optically tightened, which is the one risk in the type system (see What works — quality serif needs tight sizing or reads dated).
- **Color:** Achromatic base — `bg #fff`, surfaces `#f9f9f9`–`#fcfcfc`, borders `#e0e0e0`, fg `#202020`, muted `#8d8d8d`/`#646464`. One chromatic role: accent blue `#005eee` (CTA fill) / `#0055d9` (links/eyebrows). Dark section bg `#111`. Roles are crisp: surface ≠ border ≠ fg ≠ accent.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base. Tight intra-component steps (4/6/8/16/24); generous section rhythm via large vertical padding (64/96px block). Whitespace, not rules, separates sections.
- **Layout system:** ~1200px max content, centered. Hero copy is centered & narrow; feature rows are 2-up or 4-up equal columns; cards share radius `10px` and a hairline `#e0e0e0` border. Single mobile breakpoint collapses all columns to one stacked column.
- **Layering / page architecture:** Essentially a **single flat marketing scroll** — the probe reads **no fixed backdrop and no blend overlay** (`has_static_backdrop`/`has_blend_overlay: false`; `mix_blend_mode`/`background_blend_mode` empty), and the only fixed layer is the **opaque pinned nav** (`bg-background`, alpha 1, z-30), so the page never scrolls over a still world. Depth is **local, confined to the embedded product-demo UI**: a small z-stack (z-10/z-20 `will-change-transform` cards over `z -1/-2` backers) plus **19 frosted tiles** — one systematic `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)` over a ~5% neutral surface at 50% alpha (`bg-accent/50`; `--accent` here is neutral `lab(94.8)`, *not* the brand blue) — so receding/placeholder cards soften the content behind them instead of just dimming it. Frames corroborate: the stacked "What Round does" and "Unread" rows fade **and** blur back into the mockup. This is the one place depth is spent — the marketing chrome stays flat with hairline borders. *Cost:* `backdrop-filter` repaints on every composited frame; keep it to the few demo tiles as here, never a full-viewport glass pane.
- **Grid / container:** the capture reads a **12-column** track for the wide feature/integration rows, with sub-grids resolving to **3** columns (the 4-up rows render as 3-col spans on this width) and **1** for full-bleed/hero blocks; mobile collapses every grid to a single column. Centered container ~1200px max. The 4-up vs 3-col discrepancy is a measurement of computed `grid-template-columns` at 1440px — read the *anatomy* (equal hairline-bordered tiles) as the durable signal, not the exact count.
- **Components:** *(captured hover/focus deltas)* — **Button (primary "Get Started" / "Schedule Demo"):** electric-blue fill that **darkens on hover** (lab L≈41→28, i.e. `#005eee`→a deeper `#0040b0`-class blue), focus adds a 1px blue ring + inset top highlight + soft drop shadow; no disabled state captured. **Button (secondary "Login" / "Learn More"):** white fill lifting to a faint near-white tint on hover (lab 100→97.9); focus ring is a 1px neutral border + 1px/2px shadow. **Nav link:** transparent → a **~5% neutral wash** behind the label on hover (`oklab(0.955…)` / `lab(94.8)` ≈ a `#f1f1f1` hover pad); focus is handled by the global `a` outline. **Input:** no dedicated text input surfaced in the capture (the FAQ uses disclosure rows, not fields), so input states are **unverified** here. Border-only buttons darken their border on hover (lab 82.8→58.6 ≈ `#d2d2d2`→`#7a7a7a`).
- **Motion / easing intent:** Conservative. Captured params: **durations 50 / 150 / 200 / 350 / 400ms** (micro hover/state at 50–200ms, entrances at 350–400ms) plus a single **~1300ms** long beat (the hero word stagger / "live" loops); **easings** are `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` (Material standard, hover/focus), `cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1)` (decelerate entrance), `cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1)` (easeOutExpo — the long soft settle), and bare `ease`. **Stagger** runs ~60 / 120 / 180 / 240ms per item for grouped reveals (with two long 850 / 1200ms offsets for the headline word cascade). Communicates "responsive tool," not "showpiece." Full choreography in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** High in the embedded UI shots, low in marketing copy — the contrast makes the dense product look capable while the page stays breathable.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device = **typeface + scale** (serif display vs sans body), reinforced by the lone accent color for actions.
- **Restraint:** No gradients on chrome (only the photographic sky behind hero/footer), no drop shadows on cards (hairline borders instead), no multi-hue palette, no decorative illustration — UI screenshots do the work.

## Interaction & motion

Round's motion is **conservative and functional** — an on-load entrance, a couple of scroll-tied scale/parallax beats on the product cards, and hover micro-states; **no canvas spectacle, no cursor-follower, no scrollytelling pin.** Evidence from the capture: 15 `@keyframes` (`stagger-in`, `reveal`, `enter`/`exit`, `swipe-out-left/right/up/down`, `step-check-pop`, `step-spinner-hide`, `marquee`, `spin`, `sonner-*`), only **3 IntersectionObservers over 4 targets** (rootMargin `200px`, threshold `0` — fire just before entry), 2 scroll listeners, **925 rAF calls** in one pass (the hero word cascade + a `spin`/loader loop, *not* a heavy WebGL scene — the single `<canvas>` is incidental), 9 mousemove listeners that drive **15 draggable** card elements (not a custom cursor — `cursor: []`), and **4 disclosure** (FAQ accordions). **Reduced motion is respected** (1 PRM block). No GSAP/Lenis/Framer globals — hand-rolled CSS keyframes + IntersectionObserver. Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` · rAF (hero cascade only).

- **Word-cascade headline entrance** — *Trigger:* load. *Mechanism:* the hero serif H1 splits into per-word spans that each `transform: scale(~0.64–0.96)→1` + fade, fired in a staggered sweep (later words start from a smaller scale: "Build" 0.96, "11-star" 0.64), with the eyebrow/body/CTA fading in behind at 300/500ms (`stagger-in`, `reveal` keyframes; rAF-driven). *Reads as:* the headline "assembles itself" — confident, editorial, deliberately paced (the ~1300ms long beat lives here). *Transfers to:* any serif/display hero you want to feel composed rather than slammed-in. *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform/opacity only, once on load, reduced-motion gated) — but the late words begin nearly invisible, so the resting (full-opacity) state MUST be the DOM default for no-JS/reduced-motion. → `scroll-reveal-in-view` (group/entrance variant).
- **Card-stack scale-and-dim** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* stacked product cards behind the active feature card scale down and fade as you scroll (captured `scale 0.959→0.92`, `0.999→0.96`), so the rearward cards recede. *Reads as:* a physical deck — depth and "there's more underneath" without a carousel. *Transfers to:* testimonial/feature decks, onboarding step stacks. *Safety:* **production-safe** (compositor-only transform+opacity; keep the scale range shallow as here). → `card-stack-scale-dim`.
- **In-view product-card fade + drift** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress / in-view. *Mechanism:* an entering product card crossfades from `opacity 0.02→1` while a sibling `transform` drifts it into place (IO rootMargin `200px` so it's settled before it's seen). *Reads as:* sections "develop in" as you reach them; quiet, not jumpy. *Transfers to:* any UI-screenshot-led feature row. *Safety:* **production-safe** (one-shot, GPU-cheap, reduced-motion off). → `parallax-drift` (subtle) / `scroll-scale`.
- **Two-tone serif headline (static, not animated)** — *Trigger:* none. *Mechanism:* the lead clause is `fg`, the trailing clause is `muted` in one serif line ("…No more API key copying. *We handle everything.*"). *Reads as:* primary/secondary idea in a single line — and it is **purely typographic, not a scroll recolor** (frames show no color animation on it; don't confuse with Attio's `scroll-linked-recolor`). *Transfers to:* section intros. *Safety:* **production-safe**, zero cost. → `two-tone-headline`.
- **Hover micro-states** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* primary buttons darken their blue fill (lab L 41→28), secondary/nav surfaces lift to a ~5% neutral wash, border buttons darken their border — 50–200ms on the Material curve. *Reads as:* responsive, "rendered with intent." *Transfers to:* all interactive chrome. *Safety:* **production-safe**.
- **Swipe-dismiss card deck (latent / interactive)** — *Trigger:* drag/tap. *Mechanism:* 15 draggable elements plus `swipe-out-left/right/up/down` + `step-check-pop` keyframes indicate a Tinder-style dismiss/advance interaction in the product demo (and `sonner-*` toasts). *Reads as:* "this is a live app you can poke," not a flat screenshot. *Transfers to:* interactive product demos, inbox/triage UIs. *Safety:* **production-safe** but **needs keyboard/non-pointer fallback** and a reduced-motion path; the swipe affordance must not be the only way to advance. *(No corpus recipe yet — flagged `patterns_to_build: swipe-dismiss-card-deck`.)*

> **Honest note on thin spots:** the FAQ accordions (`disclosure: 4`) are a real, useful interaction but Round has no standalone snippet in the capture beyond the open/close; flagged `patterns_to_build: disclosure-accordion-faq`. The single `<canvas>` and the `marquee`/`spin` keyframes are present but the read frames (s00/s03/s06/s09) don't show a marquee or canvas scene firing — likely a logo ticker and a loader spinner; not elevated to a technique on this evidence.

## Production-safety note

- Serif-display + sans-body dual loading — **production-safe**; budget the extra web font (subset the serif to display weights/latin).
- Single-accent achromatic system — **production-safe**; strong a11y (high fg/bg contrast, accent meets contrast on white). Verify the blue-on-blue button text and `muted #8d8d8d` body don't dip below 4.5:1 for small text.
- Dark inverted security section — **production-safe**; just maintain ≥4.5:1 for the light-on-dark body.
- Photographic sky behind centered text (hero/footer) — **production-safe** but ensure a scrim/overlay so headline contrast holds across the gradient.

**A11y (captured, honest):** `focus_visible: true` — every interactive element ships a visible focus state (1px blue `a` outline; buttons add ring + shadow), so keyboard nav is covered. `img_alt_ratio: 1` — **all 9 images carry alt text** (clean). `prefers-reduced-motion`: **respected** (1 PRM rule gates the animations; ship the resting state in the DOM). **Tap targets: a defect** — **16 of 24** interactive elements measure under the 44px target at the 1440px capture (nav links, small inline buttons); on touch/mobile these need padding to reach 44×44. **Dark-mode: not supported** (`supportsDark: false`; the dark "theme" value is just the inverted security section, not a real scheme).
