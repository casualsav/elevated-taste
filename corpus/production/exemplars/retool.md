## What works
- **One warm ink, three jobs.** The entire "light" is a single bone tone `#e9ebdf` (not `#ffffff`) used for body text, the hero's inverted prompt card, the NEW badge, *and* every hairline border (borders are that bone at 12% alpha). Collapsing fg + light-surface + stroke to one off-white gives a hand-mixed, paper-like warmth and makes the dark page feel composed rather than defaulted. Transfers to any dark product site: pick one warm near-white and route text, inverse panels, and borders through it instead of reaching for pure white.
- **Hierarchy is pure scale, on a near-flat weight band.** Display runs 48–72px while body sits at 16px, but the weight axis barely moves (≈380→400). A 3–4.5× size jump in one near-regular weight does all the ordering; nothing is bold. The result reads calm and editorial even on a dense enterprise page. Transferable rule: separate levels by size + whitespace, not by piling on weight.
- **A 3D-tilted "sea" of real screenshots as hero atmosphere.** Instead of one product shot, dozens of app captures are tiled and raked back in perspective into a receding, low-contrast field; a single sharp focal card (the AI prompt input) floats above it on an extreme `0 68px 116px` shadow. It says "this builds *many* apps" in one glance and creates depth without illustration. See `perspective-screenshot-field-hero`.
- **A pill that shows provenance as a face-pile.** The hero's "Import apps built in other platforms" / "Build via MCP" controls lead with a cluster of overlapping mini logo dots before the label — the universal "many sources" idiom (cf. avatar stacks) repurposed for integrations/tools. It compresses "works with N things" into ~24px of width. See `source-avatar-pill`.
- **Inverted product UI as the chromatic relief.** The page is achromatic warm-neutral; nearly all color enters through the *embedded product screenshots* (green dashboards, charts, table accents) and one muted azure `#518dd2` on the send button. The brand frame stays disciplined while the product gets to be colorful — a clean division of who is allowed to use color.
- **A giant wordmark as the closing chord.** The footer ends with the "Retool" logotype set near-full-bleed in the bone ink beneath a dense multi-column nav. The oversized mark turns the page's last screenful into a brand sign-off and gives the long scroll a definite floor. Transfers to most marketing footers.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** Two custom neo-grotesques by job, not by level — **Saans** for display/headings and **PX Grotesk** for UI/body (16px base, 14px small, 12px micro), plus a monospace reserved for metadata/labels (the `ss05` stylistic set and tabular numeric features appear in the metrics). Weight axis is deliberately narrow (380–400), so the type system is a *scale* system. Ratio is loose (~1.5 stepping at the top, larger jump body→display). **Tracking is not one constant em — it scales with size:** display tightens hard (72px → −1.584px ≈ −0.022em; 60px → −1.2px ≈ −0.02em; 48px → −0.48px ≈ −0.01em; 24px subheads → −0.24px ≈ −0.01em) while UI/body text runs *slightly positive* (16px → +0.16px, 18px → +0.18px, 14px → +0.14px, 12px → +0.12px, all ≈ +0.01em). So the rule is "tighten the big type, open the small type" rather than a single tracking value.
- **Color:** Warm dark monochrome. bg `#151515`, raised surface `#242424`, fg/border/inverse-surface all the one bone `#e9ebdf`, muted `#cbccc4`, subtle warm-grey `#8b867f`. Borders = bone @ 12% (felt, not seen). Single accent = muted azure `#518dd2` (send button, links). Semantic/brand color is delegated to the product screenshots.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base; tight cluster spacing (4/8px inside controls), generous section rhythm (40/80px block padding). Whitespace is the primary separator between the big-type heads and the dense card regions below them.
- **Layout system:** Centered ~1280px content column for hero/feature heads; bento/asymmetric card grids for customer stories and "Get the latest"; a 4-column mega-footer. Mobile collapses the hero to 1 column (prompt card full-width, send button becomes a filled azure circle), wraps the source pills and *truncates* their labels to keep the face-pile, and drops the footer to 2 columns.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Captured params — durations cluster at **600 / 400 / 300 / 200ms** (plus a 500ms mid-beat and one 6000ms ambient loop for the logo/integrations marquees). The signature ease is `cubic-bezier(0.72, 0, 0.12, 1)` (fast-out, settle-in) with sibling curves `cubic-bezier(0.72,0,0.36,1)`, `cubic-bezier(0.24,0.01,0.36,1)` and an overshoot `cubic-bezier(0.12,1,0.72,1)` (the pillar-tab `bounce-x`); bare `ease` is reserved for hovers. **No stagger delays were captured** (`stagger_delays_ms: []`) — group reveals fire together, not sequenced. The whole system reads "engineered/precise," matching the internal-tools positioning; the full choreography is in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Components:** *(from `.craft.states`)* **Button/CTA** — primary "Start for free" is a bone fill that lifts a hair on hover (`#e9ebdf`→`#ebede2`, ~+1% lightness, near-imperceptible); "Book a demo" is outline-only, border darkens on hover (`#8b867f`→`#433e38`); nav text-buttons sit dimmed (~0.45–0.50 opacity) and resolve to full opacity on hover. **Input** — the "Search ⌘K" control is a bordered field whose border warms from bone `#e9ebdf` toward `#8b867f` on hover. **Link** — "Sign in" crossfades color+border bone→muted (`#e9ebdf`→`#cbccc4`); `a.flex` logo/util links just drop to 0.8 opacity. **Disabled** — transparent bg, bone text held at full opacity with `cursor: default` (signalled by cursor + context, *not* by dimming — a contrast risk if reused without an additional affordance). No card hover-lift was captured; cards rely on the bone-@12% hairline, not elevation change.
- **Grid / container:** centered content column ~**1280px** for hero/feature heads (h1 72px on a 1440 viewport). Section bento/asymmetric grids resolve to roughly a **44 / 36 / 1 / 3** fractional column split on desktop, collapsing to **34 / 26 / 1 / 20** on mobile (390vw) — i.e. the wide content+rail layout reflows toward a near-stacked single column. *(Column figures are raw `grid-template-columns` fractions from `.craft.grid`, not a clean 12-col system — treat as proportional, frame-corroborated against the customer/editorial bentos.)* Block padding runs 40/80px.
- **Density:** High (logo wall, three-up feature row, customer bento, multi-column footer) yet legible because each region is introduced by one oversized head with air around it, and structure is drawn with whisper-thin borders rather than fills.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant ordering device is **scale** (then position/whitespace). Weight and color are intentionally held back.
- **Restraint:** No pure white anywhere; almost no chroma in the chrome; near-flat weight range; near-invisible borders; no decorative illustration — depth comes from real product UI, not graphics.

## Interaction & motion

Retool's motion is **busy but disciplined**: a sticky scroll-spy story in the upper-mid page, ambient marquees for the social-proof rows, and otherwise once-only in-view reveals — all hand-rolled, **no animation library** (`libraries: []`, no GSAP/Lenis/Framer globals). Evidence: 31 `@keyframes`, 23 `IntersectionObserver`s over 48 targets, 7 scroll listeners, 15 mousemove listeners, **1884 rAF callbacks** in one scroll pass (the marquees + the bobbing-cursor illustration, *not* a WebGL scene — `canvas: 0`), 27 CSS animation rules, 1 hero `<video>`. **15 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks — reduced motion is respected.** Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` · rAF (marquee/illustration loops). Choreography read top→bottom across frames s00→s10; `from==to` opacity-noise rows dropped.

- **Sticky scroll-spy pillar tabs** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (IO scroll-spy band `rootMargin: -59.99% 0px -39.99%`, i.e. a thin mid-viewport tripwire). *Mechanism:* the left heading "Build powerful apps from anywhere" holds via sticky while a vertical tab list (`Retool app builder` [NEW] · `MCP server` · `Import React code`) advances with scroll — the active tab is full-opacity, inactive tabs sit at ~0.20, and a pinned right-hand product panel swaps (AI-builder UI → MCP terminal → drop-zone) with a `progress-bar-fill` ticking the active item. *Reads as:* one claim, three evolving proofs, reader-paced — scrollytelling that feels like operating the product. *Transfers to:* multi-capability feature deep-dives where you'd otherwise stack three screenshots. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky + IO + opacity/transform; ship the resting state with all tabs legible and keyboard-reachable). Frame-confirmed s02→s04. → closest file `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`; the IO-driven tab variant is flagged `patterns_to_build: scroll-spy-pillar-tabs`.
- **Hero screenshot-field parallax** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress + in-view. *Mechanism:* the raked-back 3D field of app captures and its floating focal prompt card translate at scroll-linked rates (`transform/parallax`, 8 of the captured scroll effects) while the hero copy drifts/fades as it leaves; a `figure.absolute` overlay fades `0→1` and darkens its backdrop (`rgba(21,21,21,0)→rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`) to hand off into the next section. *Reads as:* depth and momentum — the "sea of apps" recedes as you enter the page. *Transfers to:* any hero built from real UI tiles that wants parallax depth without illustration. *Safety:* **production-safe if subtle / spectacle if heavy** — compositor transforms are cheap, but a field of many live DOM screenshots parallaxing risks paint cost and jank on low-end; keep the field a static/pre-composited layer and parallax only a few nodes. → `parallax-drift`.
- **In-view fade-rise section reveal** — *Trigger:* in-view (IO, threshold 0, generous `rootMargin: 200px` to pre-arm just below the fold). *Mechanism:* section blocks ("Securely connect to your production data", the send button) flip one class on intersect → CSS transitions `opacity` (0→1) plus a small `translateY`, fired once; the `HeroPillarsContent…fade-in-up` / `fade-in` keyframes name it. *Reads as:* a long enterprise page that "settles in" region by region. *Transfers to:* any tall marketing/docs page. *Safety:* **production-safe** (GPU-cheap, once-only, gated). Note: **no stagger was captured** — grouped items reveal together, not in sequence. → `scroll-reveal-in-view`.
- **Ambient logo / integration marquees** — *Trigger:* load (continuous rAF loop, ~6000ms cycle). *Mechanism:* the customer logo wall and the integrations strip translate horizontally on an infinite ticker (`LogosMarquee…marquee-forwards` ×4, `imageMarquee`, `PartnersIntegrationsScroll…scroll-left/right`, `LogoGrid…scroll-left`). *Reads as:* "works with everyone, always on" — social proof that breathes. *Transfers to:* logo walls, integration strips, press rows. *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform-only) **but** pause on hover/focus and under reduced-motion, and don't trap focusable links inside a moving track. → `announcement-ticker-marquee`.
- **Bobbing-cursor drop-zone affordance** — *Trigger:* in-view + pointer (15 mousemove listeners; a pinned `Cursor-module` node). *Mechanism:* in the "Import React code" panel a glassy faux cursor floats/bobs over a "Drop a .zip file" target (`BobbingGlassIllustration…floatItem`), demonstrating the drag interaction without a real drag. *Reads as:* "you drop files here" — an animated affordance standing in for a live demo. *Transfers to:* upload zones, drag-target features, any "show, don't tell" micro-demo. *Safety:* **production-safe** (small looped transform), but it's purely decorative — keep the real control operable and `aria`-label the drop target independently; gate the bob under reduced-motion. *(No standalone snippet — bespoke to the content; reuse the principle: animate a ghost cursor over the real target.)*
- **Hover micro-states** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* CTAs/nav/links shift `opacity` / `border` / `background` only (7 captured: bone fill +1% lightness, outline border bone→`#433e38`, dimmed nav links → full opacity), ~300ms `ease`. *Reads as:* responsive, restrained, "engineered." *Transfers to:* all interactive chrome on a dark warm-neutral system. *Safety:* **production-safe** (color/opacity only; no layout shift).

## Production-safety note
- **Inverted product card on extreme shadow** (`0 68px 116px rgba(0,0,0,0.35)`): production-safe. One large blur is cheap; ensure the light card keeps AA contrast for its dark text (bone `#e9ebdf` on `#151515`-derived darks is fine).
- **3D perspective screenshot field:** production-safe *if* the field is a single pre-composited/static image or a small number of `transform`ed nodes; **spectacle-risk** if built from dozens of live DOM screenshots animating on scroll (layout/paint cost, jank on low-end). Keep it `aria-hidden`/decorative and the focal card in real DOM.
- **Source-avatar pill:** production-safe. Give the pill an accessible name (the full label, even when visually truncated) and `alt`/`aria-hidden` on the decorative logo dots.
- **Bone @ 12% hairlines:** production-safe but verify visibility — they are intentionally faint; do not rely on them as the *only* affordance for interactive boundaries.
- **A11y (captured, honestly):** **focus-visible is present** — interactive elements take a real `outline: 1px rgb(16,16,16)` ring on focus (good). **Reduced-motion is respected** (15 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks for a heavily-animated page). **Image alt coverage is strong at ~0.97** (151/156 imgs). The one real defect: **~118 of 177 interactive elements are below the 44px tap-target minimum** — the dense mega-nav and footer link lists (49 nav links desktop, collapsing to a hamburger / 3 links on mobile) are finger-hostile; enlarge hit-areas if reusing this nav density on touch. Also note the **disabled state is signalled by `cursor: default` + context, not by dimming** (opacity stays 1) — pair it with an explicit affordance or it won't read as disabled, and the muted/subtle greys (`#8b867f`, dimmed-0.45 nav text) sit near the 4.5:1 floor on `#151515` — verify for any small text.

## Extracted patterns
- `perspective-screenshot-field-hero` — a hero whose background is a raked-back, low-contrast field of product screenshots with one sharp focal card floating above; use when the product makes *many* things and you want depth from real UI, not illustration.
- `source-avatar-pill` — a rounded pill that leads with an overlapping cluster of mini source/tool logos before its label; use to say "works with / pulls from many sources" in minimal width (integrations, importers, multi-provider controls).
