## What works
- **Single-weight type, hierarchy by scale + color.** The whole page is one grotesque (TWK Lausanne) at weight 400; importance is signaled by *size jumps* (~4× body→display) and by *desaturating* secondary text to gray — never by bolding. Result reads calm and editorial even at high marketing density. Transfers to any content-dense marketing or docs page that wants to avoid the "everything is bold" shout.
- **One high-chroma accent against a warm-neutral field.** The palette is achromatic (white, cream `#f4f2f0`, near-black) except for a single acid yellow-green used *only* on primary CTAs and inline value pills. Because nothing else competes, the eye goes straight to the action and the metrics. Transfers as a discipline: pick one accent, spend it only on the click target and the proof.
- **Two-tone "lead + continuation" headlines.** Headlines split into a black lead clause and a gray remainder ("Introducing Stack by Ramp. *The AI operating system…*"), so a long sentence still has a clear focal point without a separate eyebrow/subhead. Transfers to any hero or section header that needs both a hook and explanatory text in one breath.
- **Hairline-bordered cards, near-zero shadow.** Structure comes from 1px low-opacity borders and a flat cream surface, not elevation. Reads as precise/financial-grade and stays crisp on any background. Transfers to dashboards, pricing tables, feature grids where a "floating card" look would feel consumer/soft.
- **Proof rendered as live data, not adjectives.** Trust is built with a running payments ticker, "$400hrs saved" stat blocks, customer logos, and named quotes — concrete numbers slotted into the accent pills. Transfers to any B2B landing that has to substantiate ROI claims fast.
- **Whitespace as the section separator.** Sections are divided by large vertical gaps and centered single-line statements, not rules or background-color blocks. The page breathes despite carrying a lot of content. Transfers anywhere you want density without clutter.

## Design-system read
- **Type:** TWK Lausanne grotesque, one weight (400), with the `ss01` stylistic set and fluid (`clamp`) sizing on. Scale roughly 0.875 / 1 / 1.5 / 2 / 2.5 / 4rem — a wide ~4× body-to-display ratio. Pairing logic is *self-pairing*: one family, hierarchy from size and from a two-tone black/gray color split. Tight leading (display 64px sets at 64px lh, i.e. ~1.0; body ~1.5). **Tracking is near-zero, barely negative, and does NOT scale meaningfully with size** — the capture reads only sub-pixel adjustments at the big sizes (64px ≈ -0.01px, 48px ≈ -0.01px, 40px ≈ -0.005px). So unlike a `tracking-tight-display` system (e.g. Attio/Linear, where bigger = visibly tighter), Ramp holds essentially default tracking and lets the tight *leading* do the optical-density work; don't add a `-0.02em` display token when transferring this look.
- **Color:** warm-neutral base — white `#ffffff` + cream surface `#f4f2f0` + near-black `#0c0a08` text and borders; dark sections at `#1a1919`. Exactly one accent: acid yellow-green `#e4f222`, paired with *dark* text (it's a light accent), reserved for CTAs and inline metric pills. Roles: bg=white, surface=cream, fg=near-black, muted=gray (secondary clauses), accent=yellow-green, border=10%-black hairline.
- **Spacing rhythm:** 8px base. Common steps 6/12/24/64px — i.e. component padding clusters at 24px, section/edge padding jumps to 64px. Whitespace, not dividers, does the macro-separation.
- **Layout system:** centered max-width container; asymmetric two-column feature rows (text ⇄ visual) that collapse to a single column on mobile; bordered card grids (2–4 up); centered single-line section headers. A faint dotted-grid background texture sits behind hero/feature zones.
- **Motion / easing intent:** subtle, scroll-and-marquee driven, not entrance-driven. Captured durations cluster at **300 / 240 / 250 / 200 / 150 / 100ms** (micro at 100–150, transitions at 200–300). Easings: Material standard `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)`, a symmetric `cubic-bezier(0.45,0.05,0.55,0.95)` for hovers, an entrance-style `cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1)`, an emphasized `cubic-bezier(0.6,0,0.2,0.5)`, plus bare `ease`. **No stagger delays were captured and there are zero load/in-view entrance reveals** — Ramp deliberately skips the corpus-standard fade-rise; its motion budget goes to scroll choreography (scatter-parallax, sticky panels) and continuous marquees instead. IO thresholds `0` / `0.2`; reduced-motion is respected. Full choreography in **Interaction & motion** below.
- **Density:** high information density (many features, stats, logos, quotes) kept legible by the single typeface, generous whitespace, and the achromatic palette that keeps only the accent "loud."
- **Hierarchy:** dominant ordering device is **scale**, reinforced by **color** (black vs gray, plus the accent). Weight and shadow are deliberately not used as hierarchy tools.
- **Restraint:** no gradients, no drop shadows of note, no second accent, no multiple weights, no decorative illustration — discipline is the brand signal. The "fintech serious" feel is achieved by subtraction. (Two soft exceptions exist for *product* surfaces, not chrome: a deep many-layer ambient shadow under the floating product card — `0 232px 65px rgba(0,0,0,.008), 0 35px 59px rgba(0,0,0,.03), 0 20px 50px rgba(0,0,0,.09)` — and a few directional `linear-gradient` masks used to fade scattered captures and edge-bleed the logo/quote marquees.)

- **Components (from captured states):**
  - **Button — primary CTA** ("See a demo" / "Make the switch" / "Get started for free"): acid yellow-green `#e4f222` fill with **dark** text; the only captured hover delta is a *background* shift to a marginally lighter/greener yellow (lab→oklab of nearly the same hue — effectively a ~2–4% brighten), over ~150–240ms. No lift, no shadow, no border change. Affordance is color + shape, so keep the dark label for the non-hover state too.
  - **Nav item** (Products / Partners / Solutions menu buttons): default near-black label/border; on hover the **color and border lighten** toward a faint gray — a recede-on-hover, not a fill. Menus are disclosure-driven (see motion). 24 nav links on desktop.
  - **Card** (feature / capture cards, customer-quote cards): structure is a 1px low-opacity hairline border on flat white/cream, near-zero shadow at rest; no captured hover-elevation — cards stay flat (the "precise/financial-grade" read). Movement on cards comes from scroll (parallax/collapse), not hover.
  - **Input** (inline email capture, "What's your work email?"): hairline-bordered field paired inline with the accent CTA inside one rounded container; no distinct captured focus/disabled style on the field itself — **the only captured focus treatment is a `1px rgb(16,16,16)` outline** applied to links/buttons (see a11y). *Thin data:* `.craft.states.disabled` is empty, so disabled styling is unverified.

- **Grid / container:** centered max-width container; captured grid column counts are **1 / 2 / 8** on desktop, collapsing to **1 / 3** on mobile (390px) — i.e. 2-up asymmetric feature rows and an up-to-8-track utility grid (footer link columns / logo wall) on desktop, dropping to single-column stacks and a 3-up on small screens. The re-probe now measures a **centered ~1050px content container** (the wider 1440px band is full-bleed; the 500px value is a card) with a **~16px grid gutter**; component padding still clusters at 24px and section/edge padding at 64px (use those as the rhythm).

## Interaction & motion

Ramp's motion is **scroll-choreography + continuous-ticker, not entrance animation** — and that's a deliberate, honest signal: the capture shows **0 entrance reveals, no stagger, and no in-view fade-rise** (the corpus default is absent here). The text "color-shift" effects the profiler reports are all `from==to` (lab vs oklab of the same near-black) and are dropped as noise. Probes: 28 `IntersectionObserver`s over 198 targets, 13 scroll + 15 mousemove listeners, ~1317 rAF callbacks per pass (driven by the marquees + 7 `<canvas>`), 38 `@keyframes`, 24 animation rules, **6 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks — reduced motion is respected**, and **no GSAP/Lenis/Framer** (hand-rolled IO + CSS). Watching frames s00→s09, the spine is a single narrative: *scattered systems → dissolve → re-collapse into one → proof.* Stack: `IntersectionObserver` · CSS `@keyframes` · CSS marquee/keyframe loops · `<canvas>`.

- **Scatter-then-collapse capture field** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress (parallax) into in-view (settle). *Mechanism:* a grid of product screenshots is first splayed as loose, mis-aligned "papers" that `transform`/parallax-drift at differing rates under "Systems that never spoke" (frames s01–s03), then under "Now sing together" the same cards re-collapse into a tighter aligned grid (s05). *Reads as:* the core pitch made kinetic — disconnected tools literally snapping into one platform. *Transfers to:* any "fragmented → unified" or "before/after consolidation" story. *Safety:* **production-safe if subtle** (compositor-only `transform`; cap the drift, ship the collapsed/aligned state as the reduced-motion resting layout). → `parallax-drift` (drift) + `card-stack-scale-dim` (the collapse/align beat).
- **Section background dissolve** — *Trigger:* in-view / scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* the scattered-card section's `background` animates from solid white to transparent (`rgb(255,255,255)→rgba(0,0,0,0)`) as it scrolls out, fading the loose papers down so the "Introducing Stack" headline + cream Stack-gradient panel take over (s04→s05). Reinforced by directional `linear-gradient` masks that fade the field edges. *Reads as:* a clean hand-off between two acts rather than a hard cut. *Transfers to:* act-to-act transitions on a long scroll. *Safety:* **production-safe** (opacity/bg only; ensure text under it never drops below contrast mid-fade). → no recipe file yet → `patterns_to_build: scroll-background-dissolve`.
- **Sticky-pinned proof panel** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* `position: sticky/fixed` pins a section while content scrolls past — the capture shows 3 pins: the nav bar, and a portrait/quote panel ("Lauren — Controller, Perplexity," with a play button) that holds while its supporting copy scrolls beneath (s06). *Reads as:* scrollytelling — one face/claim, evolving evidence. *Transfers to:* customer-proof deep-dives, feature walk-throughs. *Safety:* **production-safe** (native sticky, no JS; provide a non-sticky stacked fallback for short viewports). → `sticky-pin-scrollytelling`.
- **Edge-faded proof marquees** — *Trigger:* load (continuous), pause-on-hover/focus. *Mechanism:* infinite horizontal CSS-keyframe tickers (`…scroll` / `…marquee` keyframes) drive the achromatic logo wall (s00), the accolades strip, the customer-stats wall, and the "We've got the receipts" quote wall (s08), each masked with edge gradients so cards fade in/out at the rails. **25 marquee instances** captured — this, plus canvas, is what the ~1317 rAF reflects. *Reads as:* "always-on" social proof and motion energy without an entrance budget. *Transfers to:* logo rows, testimonial strips, "as seen in." *Safety:* **production-safe** (transform translate; MUST pause on hover/focus and under reduced-motion, and the marquee is decorative so its content must also exist statically for SR/keyboard). → `announcement-ticker-marquee`.
- **Hover recede / accent-warm** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* two distinct micro-states — nav labels+borders *lighten/recede* toward gray, while accent CTAs *warm* their fill a few percent — both over ~150–240ms `cubic-bezier(0.45,0.05,0.55,0.95)`. No lift/shadow. *Reads as:* restrained, financial-grade responsiveness. *Transfers to:* any calm B2B chrome where you want feedback without bounce. *Safety:* **production-safe** (color/background only). → (no standalone snippet; covered by `hover-recolor` in motion-key).
- **Disclosure mega-menus** — *Trigger:* click/tap. *Mechanism:* 18 disclosure instances; the desktop nav opens mega-menu panels with named fade/rise/slide keyframes (`desktopNavContentFadeIn`, `…RiseIn`, `…EnterFromStart/End`) and accordion open/close (`accordion-down/up`) elsewhere. *Reads as:* a deep IA kept tidy behind progressive disclosure. *Transfers to:* product/solutions nav, FAQ, docs. *Safety:* **production-safe** (keep panels keyboard-operable and `aria-expanded`-correct; animate height via grid-rows or clip, not raw `height`, to avoid reflow jank).

## Production-safety note
- Single-weight + scale/color hierarchy — **production-safe**; ensure the gray secondary text still meets contrast (the muted gray on white is borderline at small sizes — keep it ≥ body size or darken it).
- One-accent-on-CTA — **production-safe**; the light accent needs **dark** foreground text for AA contrast (white-on-yellow-green fails). Don't use the accent for body links.
- Hairline borders — **production-safe**; 10%-black borders can disappear in high-ambient-light / low-quality displays — fine for marketing, verify for true a11y-critical UI.
- Dotted-grid background texture — **production-safe**; keep it very low contrast so it never reduces text legibility.
- **A11y (captured, honestly):** `prefers-reduced-motion` **respected** (6 blocks) and `:focus-visible` **present** (a `1px rgb(16,16,16)` outline on links/buttons) — both good. Image **alt coverage ≈ 0.94** (60 / 64 images) — strong but **not complete; ~4 images lack alt** (likely decorative captures — verify they're truly decorative or add alt). **Tap targets are the real defect: 105 of 209 interactive elements are below the ~44px minimum** (the dense 24-link desktop nav + marquee cards) — fine on pointer, **risky on touch**; enlarge hit areas before shipping to mobile. Dark theme: the page advertises dark support but the captured dark tokens are thin (transparent bg, white fg, **no dark-mode accent resolved**) — treat dark mode as unverified.
