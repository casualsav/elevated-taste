## What works (transferable principles)

- **The hero's mood is a painting, not a gradient — emotion is art-directed, not CSS-generated.** A hand-painted dusk landscape (cool-blue mountains, layered clouds, a star field with warm pink/orange highlights) literally illustrates the headline "Ship software peacefully." Against the wall of gradient-on-dark dev-tool heroes, a bespoke painting reads as craft and sets a *specific* feeling rather than a generic "techy" vibe. The mechanism that keeps it legible: the type lives in the calm upper sky and the image fades into the page via a transparent→bg scrim, so the art *becomes* the page instead of being pasted on. Transfers to any brand willing to commission art where the feeling is the pitch — reserve a low-detail zone for the headline and dissolve the image edge into the page color.

- **A serif display voice paired with a sans UI voice splits "message" from "machinery."** Every headline — hero and all four section titles ("Instant networking.", "Logs, metrics, and alerts…") — is set in IBM Plex Serif; all body, labels, nav, and product chrome are Inter; tiny system tags are mono. The serif gives the marketing copy a calm, human, editorial tone that matches the artwork, while Inter keeps the dense feature lists and product UI looking like software. Transfers to product-marketing that wants warmth without losing technical credibility: serif for the *claims*, sans for the *interface*.

- **Hierarchy is scale contrast in one near-single weight, not bold-for-emphasis.** Display headlines hit ~54px while body sits at 16px/26px (a ~3.4× jump), almost everything in regular/medium; the page leans on size and large vertical voids (raw shows 64/96/128px section padding) rather than heavy weights or rules. Even a colossal serif line reads quiet. Transfers anywhere you want authority without loudness: scale up and add air, don't bold up.

- **Every section gets its own faintly tinted dark surface — "dark mode" here is a family of darks, not one flat black.** The page bg is a deep plum-black `#0d0c14`; feature/product canvases sit on a cool blue-grey `#1b2132`, the promo bar on a warm plum `#1f132a`, the testimonial band on a desaturated teal-green `#1f2b2e`. Each panel is barely distinguishable yet gives sections quiet identity and depth without borders or shadows. Transfers to any dark UI: derive 3–4 low-chroma tints of the base bg and assign them per zone instead of shipping a single `#0a0a0a` everywhere.

- **Each feature block closes with a muted "Alternative to {incumbents}" logo row — competitive positioning done implicitly.** Greyed competitor marks (Heroku, Render, Fly, Vercel, AWS) let the visitor self-locate ("this is my Heroku") and frame the product as a category replacement with zero boasting and no comparison table. Keeping the marks dim and equal-weight makes it read as a neutral positioning note, not trash-talk, and keeps the brand's own accent unrivaled. Transfers to any product entering a crowded category: name what you replace, quietly, under the relevant feature.

- **The accent purple is rationed to almost nothing, so it lands.** `#a667e4` appears only on inline links, the primary button (a deeper `#553f83` fill), focus glow, and a faint ambient hero glow; the decorative per-row icons use a fixed green/orange/pink trio. Starving the chroma means the single purple reads unmistakably as "Railway / do this." Transfers to brand-forward dark UI: pick one accent, spend it on the primary action and links only, and let everything else stay near-neutral.

- **Repeatable feature-section anatomy makes a long page feel authored, not assembled.** Each block follows the same beats: a small pill "sticker" label → serif headline → short lede + "Learn more →" → a triplet of icon/title/description rows split by hairlines → the "Alternative to" logo row. The fixed rhythm lets a tall multi-section page scan predictably while the rotating artwork/tint keeps it from feeling templated. Transfers to any content-heavy marketing page: define one section schema and vary only the payload.

## Design-system read

- **Type:** Deliberate two-family pairing — **IBM Plex Serif** for all display/section headlines, **Inter** (with **Inter Tight** for some display) for body/labels/UI, plus mono for tiny system tags. Scale runs 12/14/16/18/20/24/36px UI with a display jump to ~54px (line-height ~60–65px); body is 16px/26px (~1.6). Hierarchy is **size**, not weight: 400 dominates with 500/600/700 used sparingly. **Tracking is size-proportional, not one constant em** — it scales with size: 54px → **-1.96px** (≈-0.036em), 40px → -0.6px (≈-0.015em), 36px → -0.72px (≈-0.02em), down to 18px → -0.09px and 16px → -0.1px (≈-0.006em, near-zero). Rule of thumb: tighten more the bigger the serif gets. Type is fluid (`clamp`) so sizes/tracking interpolate between breakpoints.
- **Color:** A *family of darks*, not one black. bg `#0d0c14` → section panels `#13111c` / cool `#1b2132` / warm `#1f132a` / teal testimonial `#1f2b2e`. Foreground `#f7f7f8`, muted `#a1a0ab`, subtle `#878593`, faint `#54525f`. Borders are a near-invisible hairline `#33323e` (raw's dominant border, count 1220) plus white-alpha hairlines on glass chips. One accent: purple `#a667e4` (button fill `#553f83`, focus border `#a05fcf`). Decorative icon trio: green `#609f89`, orange `#c67839`, pink `#f9a8d4`. No gradients beyond the hero scrim/glow.
- **Spacing rhythm:** ~8px base. Controls cluster tight (6/8/12/16px); sections break with large vertical voids (24/48/64/96/128px in raw). Whitespace is the primary separator; visible dividers are rare 1px hairlines.
- **Layout system:** Centered, single-column content frame (most feature copy is left-aligned within a ~720–760px column; hero and social-proof headers centered). Feature rows lay out as a 3-up icon/title/desc grid above the logo row. Logo wall is two even monochrome rows. Captured at 1440×900; mobile collapses to one stacked column and re-crops the hero painting into a large rounded card.
- **Grid / container:** A **12-column** base grid with nested sub-grids of **3 / 1 / 5** columns (captured `gridCols: [12,3,1,5]`) — i.e. a 12-col page frame whose feature rows split into 3-up cells and the testimonial/footer into 5- and 1-col runs. Content sits in a centered container (~720–760px text column on feature copy, wider for testimonial/footer grids; gap follows the 8px rhythm, controls at 6/8/12/16px). On mobile (390px) the grid collapses to **1 / 12 / 2** columns — effectively a single stacked column with the testimonial row going 2-up.
- **Motion / easing intent:** Captured params: durations cluster at **50 / 150 / 250ms** (hover/state) with **700ms** and **1000ms** for larger reveals/loops (and a 120 000ms = 2-min ultra-slow ambient drift). Easings: `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1)` (Material standard) and `cubic-bezier(0.4,0,1,1)` (accel) for state, plus `cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1)` / `(0.19,1,0.22,1)` (easeOutCubic/Quint) for the slow brand loops; bare `ease` / `ease-in-out` also present. One stagger value (~1100ms) — used by the looping marquees, not a per-item entrance cascade. Motion communicates **a calm settle plus a "living" brand world** (a train that keeps moving, balloons that float) — the full choreography is in **Interaction & motion** below. Note: **`prefers-reduced-motion` is NOT gated** (0 PRM blocks) — a real defect for the continuous loops.
- **Density:** Moderate-to-high information density (long page, triplet feature lists, dual testimonial rows) kept legible by generous leading, large section voids, and per-section tinting that chunks the content.
- **Hierarchy:** Dominant device is **scale** (serif display vs sans body), reinforced by the **serif/sans register split** and per-section surface tint.
- **Restraint:** No gradients (besides hero scrim), no drop shadows (only inset hairline rings), no multi-weight headings, no comparison tables, and the lone accent purple is rationed to actions/links. Competitors are named only as dim footnote logos.
- **Components (captured states — hover/focus/disabled):**
  - **Button (primary "All Aboard"):** resting dual-glow shadow (magenta `rgba(180,40,180,0.25)` + violet `rgba(102,43,223,0.25)`, 6/16px blur); *hover* blooms the same glow to ~0.5 alpha and ~12/32px blur (≈2×); *focus-visible* drops the outline to transparent and rings with a 2px `rgb(161,160,171)` shadow plus a `rgb(33,31,45)` bg. **Icon button** (`group/button`): *hover* fills `bg`+`border` from transparent → `rgb(33,31,45)`.
  - **Card (feature / testimonial):** flat dark panel bounded by the inset hairline ring `inset 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.10)` (stronger 1.5px/0.20 variant on glass chips); no captured hover-lift — cards are static, depth comes from the per-section tint, not elevation.
  - **Nav (links + dropdown triggers):** *hover* recolors muted → fg (`rgb(161,160,171)→rgb(247,247,248)`; the lighter-grey "Pricing" goes `rgb(220,220,224)→rgb(242,241,243)`); *focus-visible* shows a `1px rgb(16,16,16)` outline on links and the Product/Developers/Enterprise/Company triggers; menus open via slide disclosure.
  - **Input:** none captured on the marketing page (no form fields in the frames) — **thin data**; treat the button focus-ring token (2px `rgb(161,160,171)` glow) as the field-focus precedent if building forms.
  - **Disabled:** **no disabled states captured** (`states.disabled: []`) — define your own; the system gives no precedent.

## Interaction & motion

Railway's motion is **almost entirely "brand-world" ambient loops, not scroll choreography** — the page barely reacts to scroll position. The probe found **0 IntersectionObservers, 0 scroll listeners, 0 rAF, no GSAP/Lenis/Framer**, and **`entranceReveals: 0`** — so the section-by-section "settle in" that most marketing pages do does **not** happen here; sections just appear at their resting state. What *does* move is a layer of CSS-keyframe loops that keep the railway metaphor alive (a train sweeping across, balloons floating, status dots pulsing) plus two horizontal **marquees** for social proof. The frame sequence confirms this: s00→s06 are static feature blocks (only the per-section dark tint changes, cool-blue → teal); the motion lives at the train band (s09) and the testimonial row (s08). Evidence: **39 `@keyframes`, 43 animation rules, 1 `mousemove` listener, 1 scroll-progress parallax effect, 4 disclosure widgets** (nav menus). **Reduced motion is NOT respected (0 PRM blocks)** — see safety. Stack: hand-rolled CSS `@keyframes` + a `mousemove` handler; no scroll/animation libraries.

- **Train-car horizontal drift (signature motif)** — *Trigger:* load/continuous (an ultra-slow ~120s loop) layered with scroll. *Mechanism:* an outlined train-car SVG (hairline windows + rounded body) translates horizontally across a deep-plum band; keyframes `train-scroll-forwards` / `train-scroll-backwards` drive `transform: translateX`, with `pulse-station` blinking a stop marker. *Reads as:* the brand's "railway" made literal and alive — the strongest non-static moment on the page (visible mid-sweep in frame s09). *Transfers to:* a brand willing to animate one signature object as ambient world-building (transform-only, off the hot path). *Safety:* **spectacle-leaning but cheap** — pure compositor `translateX`; cost is it never stops and currently **ignores reduced-motion**. Gate it and ship a parked state. *(No existing recipe — flagged `patterns_to_build: train-scroll-drift`; it is a looping translateX marquee, distinct from layer `parallax-drift`.)*

- **Testimonial tweet marquee** — *Trigger:* load/continuous, pause-on-interact. *Mechanism:* a horizontal ticker of tweet cards scrolls sideways (keyframes `tweets`, `marquee-scroll`), cards bleeding off both viewport edges; the lone `mousemove` listener powers pause/drag on hover. *Reads as:* an endless wall of social proof you can graze without clicking (frame s08: "…and loved by developers", cards clipped at both edges). *Transfers to:* logo walls, testimonial strips, "as seen in" rows. *Safety:* **production-safe** as a technique — animate `transform`, duplicate the track for a seamless loop, **pause on hover/focus**, and add the reduced-motion stop this site is missing. → `announcement-ticker-marquee`.

- **Scroll-progress parallax (single card)** — *Trigger:* scroll-progress. *Mechanism:* one testimonial card (the "Thales Gelinger / Real talk" div) has its `transform` bound to scroll progress so it drifts at a slightly different rate than the column. *Reads as:* a faint depth cue in the testimonial band. *Transfers to:* accenting a hero card or pull-quote with subtle layer depth. *Safety:* **production-safe** when subtle (transform-only, small range); becomes motion-sickness-y if the range is large or many layers move. → `parallax-drift`.

- **Ambient float / glow loops** — *Trigger:* load/continuous. *Mechanism:* decorative elements run perpetual CSS loops — `float` / `float-delayed` / `balloon-float` / `balloon-morph` (rising/morphing decoration), `rotate-blob` (slow hero-glow rotation), `ai-glow-rotate` / `ai-border-spin` (a spinning conic border on AI surfaces), `shimmer` / `pulse` / `pulse-station` / `ping` (status-dot life). *Reads as:* the page feels "warm and breathing" rather than dead-static, reinforcing the peaceful-dusk mood. *Transfers to:* status indicators, AI/active surfaces, hero ambiance. *Safety:* **spectacle-only** by default — perpetual loops burn battery and **none are reduced-motion-gated here**; keep them tiny, `transform`/`opacity`-only, and gate them.

- **Hover micro-states** — *Trigger:* hover. *Mechanism:* nav links recolor `rgb(161,160,171)→rgb(247,247,248)` (muted→fg); the icon button fills `bg`+`border` transparent→`rgb(33,31,45)`; the primary "All Aboard" CTA blooms its dual magenta/violet glow shadow to ~2× opacity/blur; inline links go 0.8→1.0 alpha. ~50–150ms via the Material easing. *Reads as:* crisp, responsive chrome — the expected polish. *Transfers to:* all interactive nav/CTAs. *Safety:* **production-safe** (cheap color/shadow transitions). *(No standalone recipe — matches the generic `hover-recolor` / `hover-lift` family.)*

- **Disclosure nav menus** — *Trigger:* click/hover. *Mechanism:* the Product / Developers / Enterprise / Company nav items open dropdown panels via `collapsible-slide-down` / `-up` (and `accordion-slide-*`, `enterFromRight/Left`, `exitToRight/Left`, `modal-from-*` for overlays) — `transform`/`opacity` slide-ins, 4 disclosure widgets detected. *Reads as:* standard, keyboard-operable menu reveals. *Transfers to:* any nav megamenu or mobile accordion. *Safety:* **production-safe** (slide via transform; keep ARIA/keyboard intact).

## Production-safety note

This is a live, performance-minded platform marketing site; nearly everything is **production-safe**, with two watch items:
- Painterly illustrated hero: **production-safe**, but a **payload watch** — a single oversized hero painting is the main weight risk on an otherwise light page; ship responsive `srcset`/AVIF with art-directed crops, `fetchpriority="high"` above the fold, and verify headline contrast against the lightest pixels after the scrim. The mobile rounded-card re-crop is the safe way to keep an art-led hero from cropping its subject on narrow screens.
- Serif/sans register split: **production-safe**, free; just load the serif as a `woff2` subset (display glyphs only) so it isn't a render-blocking cost.
- Family-of-darks tinting: **production-safe**, zero cost — but keep tints low-chroma so panel-to-panel steps don't read as banding on cheap displays.
- "Alternative to" logo row: **production-safe** as a *technique*; the real cost is non-engineering — third-party trademark/logo-usage policies and comparative-advertising rules in some jurisdictions. Clear it with legal for regulated verticals; keep marks dim and equal-weight so it reads as positioning, not attack.
- Rationed purple accent + hairline `#33323e` borders: **production-safe**; a11y caveat — the near-invisible hairline and the 0.45-opacity competitor logos can fail non-text contrast (3:1), so never let them be the *only* boundary or the only place a fact lives. Verify muted/subtle greys on the dark bg meet 4.5:1 for small body text.
- **A11y scorecard (captured, honest):** **Alt text — pass:** `img_alt_ratio: 1.0` (all ~60 images carry alt). **Focus-visible — present:** `focus_visible: true` — links/triggers get a `1px` outline and the primary button a 2px grey focus ring (low-contrast on dark; verify it clears 3:1). **Reduced motion — FAIL:** `reduced_motion: false`, **0 `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks** — the perpetual train/balloon/marquee loops keep running for motion-sensitive users; this is the page's clearest a11y defect, gate every loop before reusing. **Tap targets — FAIL:** **53 of 61 interactive elements are below the 44px minimum** (`small_tap_targets: 53`) — the dense nav/footer link lists are the culprit; enlarge hit areas for touch before shipping a similar nav.
